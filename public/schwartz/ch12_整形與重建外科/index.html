Schwartz 外科學

CH12 整形與重建外科 (Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery)

Source: Schwartz’s Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

Key Points

  1. Wound healing understanding is essential for surgical and nonsurgical wound care
  2. Reconstructive choices in tissue repair are critical for trauma and oncologic reconstruction
  3. Children with cleft/craniofacial differences require coordinated interdisciplinary team care
  4. Robin sequence（micrognathia, glossoptosis, airway obstruction）：management options include prone positioning, tongue-lip adhesion, mandibular distraction, tracheostomy
  5. Hemangiomas： first-line treatment is oral propranolol
  6. Pressure sore prevention requires understanding of etiology and reconstructive options
  7. Oncologic reconstruction has evolved significantly; surgeons must understand reconstructive choices for cancer patients
  8. Abdominal wall defects（ventral hernias, bariatric surgery）benefit from combined general + plastic surgery approach

傷口癒合原則 (Principles of Wound Healing)

傷口癒合階段

Phase 時間 內容
炎症期 (Inflammatory) 0-3 days Hemostasis, neutrophil infiltration
增生期 (Proliferative) 3-21 days Granulation tissue, angiogenesis, collagen synthesis
重塑期 (Remodeling) 3 weeks-1 year Collagen cross-linking, scar maturation

影響傷口癒合的因素

局部因素 全身因素
Infection 營養不良
Tension 糖尿病
Poor blood supply 吸煙
Radiation 年齡
Foreign bodies 免疫抑制
Hematoma/seroma 藥物（steroids, chemotherapy）

併發症預防

  • Dehiscence： Adequate hemostasis, tension-free closure, infection control
  • Keloid/hypertrophic scar： Silicone gel sheeting, steroid injections
  • Wound infection： Prophylactic antibiotics, sterile technique

皮膚切口原則 (Skin Incision Principles)

Langer’s Lines (Relaxed Skin Tension Lines)

  • Incisions parallel to skin tension lines → less conspicuous scars
  • 垂直於張力線 → wider scars
  • 沿著皺紋和自然輪廓切口

切口技巧

  • 銳利切開 (sharp dissection)
  • 避免張力 (tension-free)
  • 多層閉合 (layered closure)
  • 避免死腔 (dead space)

傷口閉合選擇 (Wound Closure Options)

選項 適應症
Primary closure Clean wounds, surgical incisions
Delayed primary closure Contaminated wounds
Secondary intention Infected wounds, areas with poor blood supply
Skin graft Large defects
Local/regional flap Moderate defects
Free flap Complex defects, composite tissue

皮膚移植 (Skin Grafts)

類型 厚度 用途
Split-thickness (STSG) Epidermis + partial dermis Large defects, burns
Full-thickness (FTSG) Full dermis Face, hands, functional areas
Mesh graft STSG Large defects, drainage

存活條件：

  • 受區良好的血液供應
  • 無感染
  • 無移動
  • 無接觸抑制（Contact inhibition）

皮瓣 (Flaps)

類型 血供 範例
Local flap Random pattern Advancement, rotation
Regional flap Axial pattern Propeller flap
Free flap (free tissue transfer) Microvascular anastomosis ARCAP, DIEP, fibula free flap

軟組織重建原則 (Soft Tissue Reconstruction)

階梯原則 (Reconstructive Ladder)

  1. Primary closure
  2. Secondary intention
  3. Skin graft
  4. Local flap
  5. Regional flap
  6. Free flap

重建選擇考量

選擇重建方式時，應考量：

  • Location and size of defect
  • Available local tissue
  • Functional requirements
  • Aesthetic considerations
  • Patient comorbidities

壓瘡 (Pressure Ulcers / Decubitus Ulcers)

病因學

  • Pressure > capillary pressure (32 mmHg) → ischemia → necrosis
  • Common sites： sacrum, trochanter, ischium, heels
  • Risk factors： immobility, poor nutrition, incontinence, sensory loss

分期 (NPUAP)

Stage 內容
I Nonblanchable erythema（不可漂白紅斑）
II Partial-thickness skin loss（部分厚度皮膚缺損）
III Full-thickness skin loss（全層皮膚缺損）
IV Full-thickness tissue loss（全層組織缺損）

預防

  • 翻身（every 2 hours）
  • 減壓床垫
  • 皮膚護理
  • 營養支持
  • 控制大小便失禁

治療

  • 解除壓力
  • 清創（壞死組織）
  • 傷口護理
  • 營養支持
  • 手術修復： flap reconstruction（gluteal flap, posterior thigh flap）

頭頸部重建 (Head and Neck Reconstruction)

口腔和咽部缺損

  • Small defects： primary closure, local flap
  • Medium defects： radial forearm free flap, anterolateral thigh flap
  • Large/composite defects： fibula free flap（with bone）, scapular flap

常見游離皮瓣

皮瓣 組成 用途
Radial forearm free flap Skin, subcutaneous tissue Oral lining, soft tissue
Fibula free flap Bone + skin Mandible + soft tissue
Scapular flap Bone + soft tissue Maxilla, mandible
ALT (anterolateral thigh) Myocutaneous Medium-large defects

乳房重建 (Breast Reconstruction)

時機

  • Immediate（立即重建）： at time of mastectomy（最常見）
  • Delayed（延遲重建）： months to years after mastectomy

重建方式

方式 優點/缺點
Implants (saline/silicone) 簡單，但併發症（capsular contracture, rupture）
Tissue expander + implant 需要多次手術
Autologous tissue (TRAM, DIEP, latissimus) 長期效果好，但大手術
Hybrid (fat grafting + implant) 結合兩者優點

游離皮瓣選項

皮瓣 血管 組織
DIEP (deep inferior epigastric perforator) DIEA Skin + fat（no muscle）
TRAM (transverse rectus abdominis) DIEA Skin + fat + muscle
Latissimus dorsi TDAP Muscle + skin

肢體重建 (Extremity Reconstruction)

創傷後重建

  • Soft tissue coverage for exposed bone, tendon, hardware
  • Timing： early coverage reduces infection risk
  • Flap selection： local, regional, or free flap based on defect

慢性傷口

類型 治療
下肢潰瘡 (Venous ulcers) Compression therapy, skin graft
糖尿病足潰瘡 Debridement, glycemic control, revascularization, offloading
缺血性潰瘡 Revascularization first

燒傷後重建 (Burn Reconstruction)

攣縮預防和治療

  • 動態夾板 (dynamic splinting)
  • 物理治療
  • 壓力治療 (pressure garments)
  • 手術： 攣縮鬆解, Z-plasty, skin graft, flap

常見手術

手術 適應症
Z-plasty Linear contractures
Skin grafting Large areas
Flap coverage Joints, functional areas
Tissue expansion Large defects, burns

美容手術原則 (Aesthetic Surgery Principles)

臉部美容

手術 內容
Facelift (rhytidectomy) SMAS 拉伸，去除多餘皮膚
Blepharoplasty 眼瞼整形
Rhinoplasty 鼻整形
Liposuction 脂肪抽吸

乳房美容

手術 內容
Augmentation mammoplasty 植入物或自體脂肪
Mastopexy (breast lift) 提升下垂乳房
Reduction mammoplasty 縮小過大乳房

身體輪廓

  • Abdominoplasty： 腹部拉皮
  • Liposuction： 脂肪抽吸
  • Body contouring after massive weight loss： 多部位手術

先天異常 (Congenital Anomalies)

唇腭裂 (Cleft Lip and Palate)

手術時機：

  • Cleft lip → 3-6 months
  • Cleft palate → 12-18 months
  • Alveolar bone graft → 9-11 years
  • Orthognathic surgery → after growth complete

Team approach： plastic surgeon, orthodontist, ENT, speech therapist, geneticist

血管畸形

類型 治療
Hemangioma Propranolol（first-line）；observation
Port-wine stain Laser therapy（pulsed dye laser）
AV malformation Embolization, surgery

顯微手術原則 (Microsurgery)

游離皮瓣手術

  • 需要顯微鏡下手術
  • 小血管吻合（artery + vein, typically 1-3 mm）
  • 抗凝治療（aspirin, heparin）
  • 監測皮瓣： clinical exam, Doppler

常見游離皮瓣

皮瓣 用途
Fibula free flap 顎骨重建
Radial forearm 口腔內襯, 軟組織
Anterolateral thigh (ALT) 軟組織
Groin flap 軟組織
Scapula flap 骨 + 軟組織

併發症

  • 血管危象： 血栓形成
  • 皮瓣壞死： partial or complete
  • 受區併發症： infection, hematoma

In modern medical prac -tice, patients undergo monitoring to detect pathologic varia -tions in physiologic parameters, providing advanced warning of impending deterioration in the status of one or more organ systems.
