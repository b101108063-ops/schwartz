輸血基礎 (Blood Transfusion Basics)

輸血適應症

  • 傳統準則（已不再有效： Hct >30%
  • 目前做法：
    • 有症狀的貧血
    • 重大心臟疾病
    • 危重症需要增加氧氣攜帶能力
    • 決定閾值：Hgb ≤7 g/dL 或 Hct ≤21%

圍手術期抗凝血管理 (Perioperative Anticoagulation Management)

橋接抗凝血 (Bridging Anticoagulation)

  • DOAC 使用者： 通常不需要橋接（PERIOP-2 trial）。
  • Warfarin 使用者（高血栓風險）： 考慮 LMWH 橋接。
  • Warfarin 使用者（低風險）： 手術前 5 天停止 warfarin。

血栓栓塞風險分層

高風險（>10%/年） 中風險（5-10%/年） 低風險（<5%/年）
任何機械心臟瓣膜 CHADS₂ 5-6 CHADS₂ 0-1
心房顫動 + 中風病史 風濕性心臟瓣膜疾病 無中風病史
3 個月內 VTE 3-12 個月內 VTE

大量輸血 (Massive Transfusion)

大量輸血協定 (MTP)

  • 定義： 24 小時內 >10 units pRBC 或 1 小時內 >4 units。
  • 比例： 1:1:1 (pRBC:FFP:Platelets) — PROMMTT/PROPPR trials。
  • 目標： 穩定患者 Hgb 7-9 g/dL。

創傷誘導凝血病 (TIC)

  • 早期急性凝血病： 受傷後立即發生。
  • 機制： 低灌注 → 抗凝血 + 高度纖維蛋白溶解。
  • 診斷： PT/aPTT >1.5× 正常、fibrinogen <150 mg/dL、BE >-6。
  • 治療： MTP + 損傷控制手術。

大量輸血輔助製劑

製劑 適應症 劑量
Tranexamic acid 高度纖維蛋白溶解 1 g bolus → 1 g over 8h（受傷後 3 小時內）
Fibrinogen (Cryo) 低纖維蛋白原血症 最初 4-6 g
Calcium 低血鈣（citrate toxicity） 每 1000 mL 血液給 1 g CaCl₂

特定凝血疾病 (Specific Coagulation Disorders)

Heparin 誘導血小板減少症 (HIT)

特徵 描述
類型 Type II（免疫媒介）
時間 Heparin 暴露後 5-14 天
機制 抗 PF4/heparin 抗體 → 血小板活化
4 T’s Score Thrombocytopenia, Timing, Thrombosis, oTher causes
治療 停止所有 heparin；改用 argatroban/bivalirudin
避免 Warfarin（導致皮膚壞死、肢體壞疽）

瀰漫性血管內凝血 (DIC)

特徵 描述
病理生理 全身性凝血活化 → 消耗 + 出血
原因 敗血症、外傷、產科急症、惡性腫瘤
實驗室發現 ↓血小板、↓纖維蛋白原、↑D-dimer、↑PT/aPTT、裂紅血球
治療 治療根本原因、支持性（FFP, platelets, cryo）
rFVIIa 通常不建議（CONTROL trial 顯示無益處）

重症患者血小板減少症

原因 機制 治療
敗血症 消耗、稀釋 治療感染
HIT 免疫媒介 停止 heparin、argatroban
DITP 藥物誘導 停止致病因
ITP 自體免疫 類固醇、IVIG、脾臟切除
輸血後 TRALI, TACO 支持性

手術出血併發症 (Surgical Bleeding Complications)

過度術中/術後出血原因

  1. 無效止血（技術失敗）
  2. 未發現的凝血病
  3. 消耗性凝血病 (DIC)
  4. 纖維蛋白溶解
  5. 大量輸血 → 稀釋性凝血病、低溫、酸中毒

再探查適應症

  • 前 2-3 小時每小時 >150 mL
  • 接下來每小時 >100 mL
  • 任何突然增加的引流
  • 無法用其他原因解釋的血流動力學不穩定

圍手術期管理清單 (Perioperative Management Checklist)

術前

  • 審視抗凝血/抗血小板藥物
  • 若為高出血風險手術，手術前 5 天停止 warfarin
  • 高血栓風險者考慮橋接
  • 根據腎功能，手術前 24-48 小時停止 DOACs
  • 視情況檢查 CBC, PT, aPTT, fibrinogen

術中

  • 精確止血
  • 減少失血（低血壓麻醉、血液回收）
  • 維持正常體溫（>36°C）
  • 有條件時用 TEG/ROTEM 監測
  • 凝血病時及早補充因子

術後

  • 監測引流管和血紅蛋白
  • 根據症狀而非任意數字輸血
  • 根據出血風險恢復抗凝血（通常 24-72 小時）
  • DVT 預防（機械 + 安全時的藥物）

Neonatal gynecomastia is caused by the action of placental estrogens on neonatal breast tissues, whereas in adolescence, there is an excess of estradiol relative to testosterone, and with senescence, the circulating testosterone level falls, which results in relative hyperestrin -ism. In gynecomastia, the ductal structures of the male breast enlarge, elongate, and branch with a concomitant increase in epithelium. During puberty, the condition often is unilateral and typically occurs between ages 12 and 15 years. In contrast, senescent gynecomastia is usually bilateral. In the nonobese male, breast tissue measuring at least 2 cm in diameter must be present before a diagnosis of gynecomastia may be made. Mammography and ultrasonography are used to differentiate breast tissues. Dominant masses or areas of firmness, irregular-ity, and asymmetry suggest the possibility of a breast cancer, particularly in the older male. Gynecomastia generally does not predispose the male breast to cancer. Identifying hereditary risk for breast cancer is a four-step process that includes: (a) obtaining a complete, multigenerational family history, (b) assessing the appropriateness of genetic testing for a particular patient, (c) counseling the patient, and (d) interpret-ing the results of testing.110 Genetic testing should not be offered in isolation, but only in conjunction with patient education and counseling, including referral to a genetic counselor. Initial determinations include whether the individual is an appropriate candidate for genetic testing and whether genetic testing will be informative for personal and clinical decision-making. In the early 1970s, the National Cancer Institute in Milan, Italy, initiated two prospective randomized multimodality clinical trials for women with T3 or T4 breast cancer.310 The best results were achieved when surgery was interposed between chemotherapy courses, with 82% local-regional control and 25% having a 5-year dis -ease-free survival. There was no difference in the 5-year disease-free survival rates for the two groups, but after neoadjuvant chemotherapy there was an increase in the number of lumpectomies performed and a decreased incidence of node positivity. Mittendorf and colleagues evaluated a contemporary series of almost 3000 patients treated with breast conserving surgery and radiation therapy who received either neoadjuvant or adjuvant chemotherapy for breast cancer.312 They found that the risk of LRR was driven by bio-logic factors and disease stage and was not impacted by the timing of chemotherapy delivery. Patients whose tumors achieve a pathologic complete response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy have been shown to have statisti-cally improved survival outcomes to those of patients whose tumors demonstrate only a partial response, remain stable, or progress on treatment. Researchers at MD Anderson Cancer Center have shown that residual cancer burden (RCB)— categorized into four classes, RCB-0 or pathologic complete response, RCB-1, RCB-2, and RCB-3—is predictive of 10-year relapse-free survival with neoadjuvant chemotherapy in triple negative, ER-positive, and HER2-positive tumors.313 Patients who experience progression of disease during neoadjuvant che-motherapy have the poorest survival.314,315 This means that while patients who achieve a pCR will have a better prognosis based on their response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy.
