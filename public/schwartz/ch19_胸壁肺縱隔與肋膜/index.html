CHAPTER 19 CHEST WALL, LUNG, MEDIASTINUM, AND PLEURA 胸壁、肺、縱隔與肋膜

CHAPTER 19 CHEST WALL, LUNG, MEDIASTINUM, AND PLEURA 胸壁、肺、縱隔與肋膜

source: Schwartz’s Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

Chest Trauma 胸部創傷

Initial Assessment 初步評估

  • ATLS protocol (ABCDE) is the standard approach
  • Immediate life threats requiring intervention:
    • Airway obstruction
    • Tension pneumothorax
    • Massive hemothorax
    • Flail chest
    • Cardiac tamponade

Specific Injuries 特定傷害

Injury 傷害類型 Presentation 表現 Treatment 治療
Tension pneumothorax 張力性氣胸 Absent breath sounds, hypotension, tracheal deviation, JVD Needle decompression → Chest tube 針刺減壓→胸管
Open pneumothorax 開放性氣胸 Sucking chest wound Three-sided dressing → Chest tube 三面敷料→胸管
Massive hemothorax 大量血胸 >1500 mL blood, hypotension Chest tube, possible thoracotomy 胸管，可能開胸
Flail chest 連枷胸 Paradoxical movement, respiratory failure Mechanical ventilation, pain control 機械通氣，疼痛控制
Cardiac tamponade 心包填塞 Beck’s triad (hypotension, JVD, muffled heart sounds) Pericardiocentesis, surgical repair 心包穿刺，手術修復

Chest Tube Insertion 胸管置入

  • Indications 適應症: Pneumothorax, hemothorax, pleural effusion, empyema 氣胸、血胸、胸腔積液、膿胸
  • Position 位置: 4th-5th intercostal space, midaxillary line 第4-5肋間，腋中線
  • Management 管理: -20 cm H₂O suction 負壓抽吸，拔除前夾閉

Tracheal Emergencies 氣管急症

Tracheoinnominate Artery Fistula 氣管無名动脉瘻管

  • Cause 病因: Tracheostomy tube erodes into innominate artery 氣切管侵蝕無名动脉
  • Presentation 表現: “Sentinel bleeding” followed by massive hemorrhage 前驅出血後大出血
  • Emergency treatment 緊急處理:
    1. Overtube the tracheostomy 更換氣切管
    2. Apply pressure against manubrium 壓迫胸骨柄
    3. Orotate intubation 經口氣管插管
    4. Emergent surgical resection 緊急手術切除

Tracheoesophageal Fistula (TEF) 氣管食道瘻管

  • Cause 病因: Prolonged ventilation + nasogastric tube 長期通氣+鼻胃管
  • Diagnosis 診斷: Bronchoscopy/esophagoscopy 支氣管鏡/食道鏡檢查
  • Management 處理:
    1. Remove tubes, wean from ventilator 移除管路，脫離呼吸器
    2. Gastrostomy (decompression) + jejunostomy (feeding) 胃造口(減壓)+空腸造口(餵食)
    3. Cervical esophagostomy if needed 必要時頸部食道造口
    4. Tracheal resection + esophageal repair (after weaning) 氣管切除+食道修復(脫離後)

Chest Wall Tumors 胸壁腫瘤

Benign Tumors 良性腫瘤

Tumor 腫瘤 Features 特徵
Fibrous dysplasia 纖維發育不良 Most common benign chest wall tumor 最常見良性胸壁腫瘤
Osteochondroma 骨軟骨瘤 Most common benign bone tumor 最常見良性骨腫瘤
Desmoid tumors 硬纖維瘤 Locally aggressive, may recur 局部侵襲性，可能復發

Malignant Tumors 惡性腫瘤

Tumor 腫瘤 Features 特徵
Chondrosarcoma 軟骨肉瘤 Most common malignant chest wall tumor 最常見惡性胸壁腫瘤
Osteosarcoma 骨肉瘤 Often with prior radiation 常有放射治療史
Ewing sarcoma 尤文氏肉瘤 Primarily in children/adolescents 主要見於兒童/青少年
Askin tumor Peripheral PNET of chest wall 胸壁周邊性原始神經外胚層腫瘤

Surgical Resection 手術切除

  • Wide excision with 2-4 cm margins 廣泛切除，邊距2-4公分
  • Reconstruction 重建: Mesh, methylmethacrylate, myocutaneous flaps (pectoralis, latissimus dorsi, TRAM) 網片、甲基丙烯酸甲酯、肌皮瓣

Lung Cancer 肺癌

Classification 分類

Type 類型 Frequency 發生率 Characteristics 特徵
Adenocarcinoma 腺癌 40% Most common overall, peripheral, mutations (EGFR, KRAS)
Squamous cell carcinoma 鱗狀細胞癌 25-30% Central, smoking-related, keratin pearls
Small cell carcinoma 小細胞癌 15-20% Central, aggressive, paraneoplastic syndromes
Large cell carcinoma 大細胞癌 10-15% Poorly differentiated, aggressive
Bronchial carcinoid 支氣管類癌 1-5% Low-grade neuroendocrine, better prognosis

Staging (AJCC 8th Edition - TNM) 分期

T (Primary Tumor) 原發腫瘤

Stage 分期 Size 尺寸 Criteria 標準
Tis - Carcinoma in situ 原位癌
T1a ≤1 cm ≤3 cm, no visceral pleural involvement 無臟層肋膜侵犯
T1b >1-2 cm ≤3 cm
T1c >2-3 cm ≤3 cm
T2a >3-4 cm >3-4 cm, or involving main bronchus 或侵犯主支氣管
T2b >4-5 cm >4-5 cm
T3 >5-7 cm >5-7 cm or invading chest wall, pericardium 或侵犯胸壁、心包
T4 >7 cm Invading aorta, carina, heart, etc. 侵犯主動脈、氣管叉、心臟等

N (Lymph Nodes) 淋巴結

Stage 分期 Location 位置
N0 No nodal involvement 無淋巴結侵犯
N1 Ipsilateral peribronchial, hilar 同側支氣管旁、肺門
N2 Ipsilateral mediastinal, subcarinal 同側縱隔、氣管叉下
N3 Contralateral mediastinal, supraclavicular 對側縱隔、鎖骨上

M (Distant Metastasis) 遠處轉移

Stage 分期 Finding 發現
M0 No distant metastasis 無遠處轉移
M1a Separate tumor nodules in contralateral lung, pleural nodules 對側肺部獨立腫瘤結節、肋膜結節
M1b Single extrathoracic metastasis 單一胸腔外轉移
M1c Multiple extrathoracic metastases 多發胸腔外轉移

Stage 分期 T N M 5-Year Survival 5年存活率

Surgical Treatment 手術治療

Resectability Criteria 可切除標準

Factor 因素 Operable 可手術 Inoperable 不可手術
T stage T1-3, selected T4 T4 with aorta/carina/heart invasion
N stage N0-1 N2-3 (generally)
M stage M0 M1
PFTs FEV1 >1.5L, DLCO >40% FEV1 <1.5L, DLCO <40%

Pulmonary Resections 肺切除術

Procedure 術式 Extent 範圍 Typical Use 典型用途
Wedge resection 楔形切除 Small peripheral wedge 小周邊楔形 Small tumors, limited reserve 小腫瘤、肺功能儲備不足
Segmentectomy 肺節切除 Single bronchopulmonary segment 單一支氣管肺節 Stage I NSCLC <2 cm
Lobectomy 肺葉切除 Single lobe 單一肺葉 Standard for NSCLC 標準術式
Bilobectomy 雙肺葉切除 Two lobes (right) 右側兩肺葉 Central tumors 中央型腫瘤
Pneumonectomy 全肺切除 Entire lung 單側全肺 Central/hilar tumors 中央/肺門腫瘤

VATS (Video-Assisted Thoracoscopic Surgery) 胸腔鏡手術

  • Advantages 優點: Less pain, shorter stay, faster recovery 疼痛少、住院短、恢復快
  • Indications 適應症: Stage I-II NSCLC, mediastinal masses, pleural disease I-II期肺癌、縱隔腫瘤、肋膜疾病
  • Contraindications 禁忌症: Bulky N2 disease, chest wall invasion, large tumors N2轉移嚴重、胸壁侵犯、大腫瘤

Lung Nodules 肺結節

Evaluation 評估

Characteristic 特徵 Benign 良性 Malignant 惡性
Size 尺寸 <6 mm >8 mm
Edges 邊緣 Smooth, calcified 平滑、鈣化 Spiculated, irregular 毛刺、不規則
Growth 生長 Stable 穩定 Growing 增大
Risk factors 危險因子 None 無 Smoking, age, family history 吸菸、年齡、家族史

PET-CT

  • Sensitivity 敏感度: 80-90%
  • False negatives 偽陰性: Carcinoid, ground-glass nodules, low SUV 類癌、磨玻璃結節、低SUV值
  • False positives 偽陽性: Infection, inflammation 感染、發炎

Lung-RADS Classification

Category 分類 Description 描述 Recommendation 建議
3 Probably benign 可能良性 6-month follow-up 6個月追蹤
4A Suspicious 可疑 3-month follow-up or biopsy 3個月追蹤或切片
4B Very suspicious 高度可疑 Biopsy/resection 切片/切除

Pleural Diseases 肋膜疾病

Pleural Effusion 肋膜積液

  • Transudate 漏出液: Heart failure, cirrhosis, nephrotic syndrome 心衰竭、肝硬化、腎病症候群
  • Exudate 滲出液: Infection, malignancy, PE, pancreatitis 感染、惡性腫瘤、肺栓塞、胰臟炎
  • Light’s criteria: Pleural protein/serum protein >0.5, pleural LDH/serum LDH >0.6

Pneumothorax 氣胸

Type 類型 Mechanism 機制 Treatment 治療
Primary spontaneous 原發性自發性 Ruptured bleb 破裂肺大皰 Observation, needle aspiration, chest tube 觀察、針吸、胸管
Secondary spontaneous 續發性自發性 Underlying lung disease 潛在肺部疾病 Chest tube 胸管
Traumatic 外傷性 Penetrating/blunt injury 穿刺/鈍傷 Chest tube 胸管
Tension 張力性 One-way valve 單向閥 Emergency needle decompression 緊急針刺減壓

Hemothorax 血胸

  • Definition 定義: >50 mL blood in pleural space 肋膜腔>50mL血液
  • Massive: >1500 mL or 1/3 of blood volume 大量：>1500mL或血量1/3
  • Treatment 治療: Chest tube, possible thoracotomy (>1500 mL initial or persistent) 胸管，可能開胸(初始>1500mL或持續性)

Empyema (Pyothorax) 膿胸

  • Stages 分期: Exudative → Fibrinopurulent → Organizing 滲出期→纖維膿性期→肉芽形成期
  • Treatment 治療:
    • Early 早期: Chest tube + antibiotics 胸管+抗生素
    • Late 晚期: Decortication, VATS debridement 剝脫術、胸腔鏡清創
    • Chronic 慢性: Window procedure, Eloesser flap 窗戶手術、Eloesser皮瓣

Mediastinal Masses 縱隔腫瘤

Location-Based Differential 按位置區分

Location 位置 Common Tumors 常見腫瘤
Anterior 前縱隔 Thymoma, teratoma, thyroid cancer, lymphoma, substernal goiter 胸腺瘤、畸胎瘤、甲狀腺癌、淋巴瘤、胸骨下甲狀腺腫
Middle 中縱隔 Lymphoma, bronchogenic cyst, pericardial cyst 淋巴瘤、支氣管囊腫、心包囊腫
Posterior 後縱隔 Neurogenic tumors (schwannoma, ganglioneuroma), esophageal lesions 神經源性腫瘤(神經鞘瘤、神經節細胞瘤)、食道病灶

Mediastinoscopy 縱隔鏡檢

  • Approach 入路: Cervical (most common) 頸部(最常見)
  • VATS: Alternative for anterior/superior mediastinum 胸腔鏡用於前/上縱隔
  • Chamberlain procedure: Left parasternal 2nd intercostal space (for aortopulmonary window nodes) 左胸骨旁第2肋間(主肺窗淋巴結)

Specific Tumors 特定腫瘤

Thymoma 胸腺瘤

  • Associated 關聯: Myasthenia gravis (30% of patients with MG have thymoma) 重症肌無力(30% MG患者有胸腺瘤)
  • Treatment 治療: Complete surgical resection (+ radiation for invasive) 完全手術切除(+侵襲性者放療)
  • Prognosis 預後: 5-year survival 80-90% (complete resection) 5年存活80-90%(完全切除)

Mediastinal Germ Cell Tumors 縱隔胚細胞腫瘤

  • Benign 良性: Teratoma (most common) 畸胎瘤(最常見)
  • Malignant 惡性: Seminoma, non-seminomatous (AFP, β-hCG elevated) 精細胞瘤、非精細胞瘤(AFP、β-hCG升高)

Neurogenic Tumors 神經源性腫瘤

  • Most common posterior mediastinal tumors 後縱隔最常見腫瘤
  • Schwannoma: Most common (benign, resectable) 最常見(良性，可切除)
  • Ganglioneuroma: Benign, may mature 良性，可能成熟

Thoracic Outlet Syndrome (TOS) 胸廓出口症候群

Types 類型

Type 類型 Compression 壓迫 Symptoms 症狀
Neurogenic 神經性 Brachial plexus 臂神經叢 Hand numbness, weakness, pain 手麻、無力、疼痛
Venous (Paget-Schroetter) 靜脈性 Subclavian vein 鎖骨下靜脈 Arm swelling, cyanosis 手臂腫脹、發紺
Arterial 動脈性 Subclavian artery 鎖骨下动脉 Arm ischemia, emboli 手臂缺血、栓塞

Diagnosis 診斷

  • Adson’s test, Wright’s test, Roos test (variable reliability) 準確度不一
  • Imaging 影像: CXR, CT, MRI, angiography X光、電腦斷層、核磁共振、血管攝影

Treatment 治療

  • Conservative 保守: Physical therapy, NSAIDs 物理治療、NSAID
  • Surgical 手術: First rib resection, scalenectomy, pec minor release 第一肋切除、斜角肌切除、胸小肌放鬆

Diaphragm 橫膈膜

Hiatal Hernia 食道裂孔疝氣

Type 類型 Features 特徵
Sliding (Type I) 滑動型 GE junction moves above diaphragm, most common 胃食道接合處移至橫膈上方，最常見
Paraesophageal (Type II-IV) 旁食道型 GE junction below, stomach herniates alongside 胃食道接合處在下方，胃沿旁疝出

Traumatic Diaphragmatic Hernia 創傷性橫膈疝氣

  • Left side more common (liver protects right) 左側較常見(肝臟保護右側)
  • Diagnosis 診斷: CXR, CT X光、電腦斷層
  • Treatment 治療: Surgical repair (laparoscopic or open) 手術修復(腹腔鏡或開腹)

Lung Surgery Principles 肺臟手術原則

Preoperative Assessment 術前評估

  • Pulmonary function 肺功能: FEV1, FVC, DLCO
  • Ventilation-perfusion scan: For borderline patients 適用於邊緣患者
  • Exercise testing: 6-minute walk, stair climb 運動測試：6分鐘步行、爬樓梯

Postoperative Complications 術後併發症

Complication 併發症 Incidence 發生率 Prevention/Treatment 預防/治療
Arrhythmias (AF) 心律不整(房顫) 15-30% Beta-blockers, amiodarone
Pulmonary embolism 肺栓塞 2-5% Prophylaxis, anticoagulation 預防、抗凝血
Bronchopleural fistula 支氣管胸膜瘻 <5% Secure bronchial stump 穩固支氣管殘株
Empyema 膿胸 2-5% Drainage, antibiotics 引流、抗生素
Prolonged air leak 長期漏氣 Common 常見 Chest tube, possibly surgery 胸管，可能手術

Lung Volume Reduction Surgery (LVRS) 肺容積減少手術

  • For emphysema with significant hyperinflation 適用於明顯過度充氣的肺氣腫
  • Upper lobe predominant disease → better outcomes 上葉為主疾病→預後較好
  • NOT for FEV1 <20%, DLCO <20%, severe PH 不適用於：FEV1<20%、DLCO<20%、嚴重肺高壓
