Cardiac Risk Assessment 心臟風險評估

NYHA Functional Classification NYHA功能分級

Class 分級 Description 描述
I No limitation of physical activity 無體能活動限制
II Slight limitation; fatigue/palpitation with ordinary activity 輕微限制；一般活動時疲勞/心悸
III Marked limitation; symptoms with less than ordinary activity 明顯限制；低於一般活動時有症狀
IV Unable to carry on any activity; symptoms at rest 無法進行任何活動；休息時也有症狀

CCS Angina Classification CCS心絞痛分級

Class 分級 Description 描述
I Ordinary activity does not cause angina 一般活動不引起心絞痛
II Slight limitation; angina with rapid stairs, hills, cold, stress 輕微限制；快速爬樓、爬山、寒冷、緊張時心絞痛
III Marked limitation; 1-2 blocks walking, 1 flight stairs 明顯限制；走1-2條街、爬1層樓即發生
IV Angina at rest 休息時心絞痛

Noncardiac Surgery Risk Stratification 非心臟手術風險分層

Risk Category 風險類別 Procedures 手術類型 MACE Risk MACE風險
Low (<1%) 低風險 Endoscopy, cataract, breast, superficial, ambulatory 內視鏡、白內障、乳房、淺層門診手術 <1%
Intermediate 中風險 Intraperitoneal, intrathoracic, head/neck, orthopedic, prostate 腹腔、胸腔、頭頸、骨科、攝護腺 1-5%
Vascular 血管手術 AAA repair, peripheral vascular 腹主動瘤修復、周邊血管 >7%

ACC/AHA Stepwise Approach ACC/AHA逐步法

  1. Step 1: Identify known CAD/risk factors 確認已知CAD/危險因子
  2. Step 2: Emergency → evaluate for ACS 緊急手術→評估ACS
  3. Step 3: Estimate MACE risk (<1% vs elevated) 估算MACE風險
  4. Step 4: Low risk + ≥4 METs → proceed to surgery 低風險+≥4 METs→進行手術
  5. Step 5: Elevated risk + ≥4 METs → may proceed 高風險+≥4 METs→可進行
  6. Step 6: Poor/unknown METs → consider stress testing 運動能力差/未知→考慮壓力測試
  7. Step 7: Elevated risk + poor functional capacity → pharmacologic stress testing 高風險+運動能力差→藥物壓力測試

Active Cardiac Conditions Requiring Pre-op Evaluation 需要術前評估的活躍心臟病

  • Acute coronary syndrome (ACS) 急性冠脈症候群
  • Decompensated heart failure 失代償性心衰竭
  • Significant arrhythmias 重大心律不整
  • Severe valvular heart disease 嚴重心臟瓣膜疾病

Preoperative Risk Factors (Revised Cardiac Risk Index) 術前危險因子

  1. History of ischemic heart disease 缺血性心臟病病史
  2. History of prior/compensated heart failure 過去/代償性心衰竭病史
  3. Cerebrovascular disease 腦血管疾病
  4. Diabetes mellitus 糖尿病
  5. Renal insufficiency 腎功能不全

Type 類型 Use 用途

TEE (Transesophageal) 經食道 Better mitral valve/LA imaging, intraoperative, thrombus 二尖瓣/左心房影像更佳、術中、血栓

Dobutamine stress echo 多巴酚丁胺壓力心圖 Ischemia assessment, viability 缺血評估、存活能力
Strain imaging (speckle-tracking) 應變影像 Myocardial deformation, LV function 心肌變形、左心室功能

Nuclear Medicine 核子醫學

Study 檢查 Sensitivity/Specificity 敏感度/特異性 Use 用途
SPECT (Tc-99m sestamibi) 87%/73% Regional ischemia, viability, EF 區域缺血、存活能力、射出率
PET Higher specificity (86%) Absolute perfusion + metabolism, diffuse disease 絕對灌流+代謝、瀰漫性疾病
Thallium-201 Older agent 較舊製劑 Perfusion imaging 灌流影像

Cardiac Catheterization 心導管

  • Gold standard for coronary artery disease 冠狀动脉疾病的黃金標準
  • Significant stenosis: ≥70% lumen (≥50% left main) 顯著窄狹：≥70%管腔(左主幹≥50%)
  • FFR/iFR: For borderline lesions (FFR ≤0.80 indicates need for revascularization) 邊緣病灶用(FFR≤0.80表示需要血管重建)
  • IVUS: Intracoronary ultrasound 冠狀动脉內超音
  • Mortality: 0.11%; Major complications <2% 死亡率：0.11%；主要併發症<2%

Cardiac CT 心臟電腦斷層

  • Coronary calcium score: Quantifies atherosclerotic burden 冠狀动脉鈣化積分：量化粥狀硬化負擔
  • CT coronary angiography: Triple rule-out (ACS, PE, aortic dissection) 冠狀动脉CTA：三排除(ACS、肺栓、主動服剝離)

Cardiopulmonary Bypass (CPB) 體外循環

Circuit Components 迴路組成

  • Venous cannulae → Venous reservoir → Pump → Oxygenator → Filter → Arterial cannula 靜脈插管→靜脈儲槽→幫浦→氧合器→過濾器→動脉插管

Technique 技術

Parameter 參數 Details 詳情
Heparin dose 肝素劑量 300-400 units/kg (ACT >450 sec)
Cannulation 插管 Aorta (distal ascending), femoral, axillary, or innominate artery 主動服(遠端升主)、股动脉、腋动脉或無名动脉
Venous 靜脈 Right atrium (single or bicaval), femoral vein 右心房(單或雙腔)、股靜脈
Flow (normothermia) 流量(正常體溫) ~2.4 L/min/m²
Flow (hypothermia 18°C) 流量(低體溫18°C) ~1 L/min/m²
Protamine reversal 魚精蛋白逆轉 1 mg per 100 units heparin 每100單位肝素1毫克

Adverse Effects 不良反應

Complication 併發症 Mechanism 機制
Bleeding 出血 Fibrinogen consumption, fibrinolysis, platelet consumption 纖維蛋白原消耗、纖維蛋白溶解、血小板消耗
Inflammation 發炎 Complement activation, cytokine release 補體活化、細胞激素釋放
HIT (1-5%) 肝素相關血小板減少 Heparin-PF4-IgG complex → thrombocytopenia → thrombosis (HITT)
End-organ dysfunction 器官功能障礙 Hypoperfusion, embolization, inflammatory response 低灌流、栓塞、發炎反應

Myocardial Protection 心肌保護

Method 方法 Description 描述
Antegrade cardioplegia 順行性心麻痹 Into aortic root/coronary ostia 注入主動服根部/冠狀动脉口
Retrograde cardioplegia 逆行性心麻痹 Via coronary sinus catheter 經冠狀竇導管
Cold blood K+ cardioplegia 冷血鉀心麻痹 Most common in US 美國最常用
Tepid/warm 溫血/熱血 Alternative temperature strategies 替代溫度策略

Coronary Artery Disease (CAD) 冠狀動脈疾病

Pathophysiology 病理生理學

  1. Endothelial injury → inflammation → lipid accumulation 內皮損傷→發炎→脂肪堆積
  2. Macrophage infiltration → cholesterol-laden foam cells 巨噬細胞浸潤→膽固醇充盈泡沫細胞
  3. Smooth muscle proliferation → atheroma formation 平滑肌增生→粥狀瘤形成
  4. Fibrous cap rupture → thrombosis → acute coronary syndrome 纖維帽破裂→血栓→急性冠脈症候群

Risk Factors 危險因子

  • Age, smoking, hypertension, dyslipidemia, sedentary lifestyle, obesity, diabetes 年齡、吸菸、高血壓、血脂異常、久坐、肥胖、糖尿病

Clinical Presentations 臨床表現

Presentation 表現 Features 特徵
Stable angina 穩定型心絞痛 Substernal chest pain with exertion, relieved by rest/nitroglycerin 努力時胸骨下胸痛，休息/硝化甘油可緩解
ACS (UA/NSTEMI/STEMI) 急性冠脈症候群 Plaque rupture, thrombosis 粥狀斑塊破裂、血栓形成
Ischemic HF 缺血性心衰竭 LV dysfunction from chronic ischemia 慢性缺血導致左心室功能障礙
Arrhythmias 心律不整 AF, heart block, VT from structural remodeling 結構重構導致房顫、傳導阻滯、室速
Sudden death 猝死 Malignant arrhythmias 惡性心律不整

Secondary Prevention (Class I) 次級預防

  • Smoking cessation 戒菸
  • BP control: <140/90 mmHg (<130/80 in DM/CKD) 血壓控制
  • LDL: <100 mg/dL
  • Aspirin all patients 所有患者
  • BMI: <25 kg/m²
  • HbA1c: <7%
  • Moderate aerobic exercise 適度有氧運動
  • β-blockers post-MI, ACS, LV dysfunction 心肌梗塞、急性冠脈症候群、左心室功能障礙後
  • ACE-I/ARB for HTN, LV dysfunction, DM, CKD 高血壓、左心室功能障礙、糖尿病、慢性腎病

CABG vs PCI Trials CABG對比PCI試驗

Trial 試驗 Finding 發現
NY State Registry (2005) 紐約州登錄 CABG > PCI in multivessel survival advantage CABG在多血管存活優勢優於PCI

Cardiac Valve Disease 心臟瓣膜疾病

Aortic Stenosis (AS) 主動脈瓣窄狹

Feature 特徵 Detail 詳情
Etiology 病因 Degenerative (most common), bicuspid valve, rheumatic 退化性(最常見)、雙葉瓣、風濕性
Pathophysiology 病理生理 Pressure overload → LV hypertrophy → decreased compliance 壓力過度負荷→左心室肥大→順應性降低
Classic triad 經典型三聯 Angina, syncope, heart failure (late) 心絞痛、暈厥、心衰竭(晚期)
Murmur 雜音 Crescendo-decrescendo systolic ejection murmur (RUSB) 漸強-漸弱收縮期噴射性雜音(右鎖骨上)
Diagnosis 診斷 Echo: valve area, gradient; Cath: direct pressure measurement 超音：瓣膜面積、壓力差；導管：直接測壓
Severe AS criteria 嚴重AS標準 AVA <1.0 cm², mean gradient >40 mmHg, jet velocity >4 m/s

Treatment 治療

Type 類型 Indication 適應症
SAVR 手術主動服瓣置換 Severe symptomatic AS; Severe asymptomatic AS (certain criteria) 嚴重有症狀AS；特定條件的嚴重無症狀AS
TAVR 經導管主動服瓣置換 High/intermediate surgical risk; expanding to lower risk 高/中手術風險；正向低風險族群擴展
Balloon aortic valvuloplasty 氣球主動服瓣整形 Bridge to surgery, palliative 過渡至手術、姑息

Aortic Regurgitation (AR) 主動服瓣逆流

Feature 特徵 Detail 詳情
Etiology 病因 Bicuspid valve, endocarditis, aortic root dilation, rheumatic 雙葉瓣、心內膜炎、主動服根部擴張、風濕性
Pathophysiology 病理生理 Volume overload → LV dilation 容量過度負荷→左心室擴張
Murmur 雜音 Early diastolic decrescendo murmur (LUSB), displaced PMI 舒張早期漸弱雜音(左鎖骨上)、心尖外移
Signs 徵象 Water-hammer pulse, wide pulse pressure 水衝脈、寬脈壓
Indications for surgery 手術適應症 Severe symptomatic AR; Severe asymptomatic with LV dysfunction 嚴重有症狀AR；伴左心室功能障礙的嚴重無症狀AR

Mitral Stenosis (MS) 僧帽瓣窄狹

Feature 特徵 Detail 詳情
Etiology 病因 Rheumatic heart disease (almost always) 風濕性心臟病(幾乎總是)
Pathophysiology 病理生理 Pressure overload → LA dilation → pulmonary HTN 壓力過度負荷→左心房擴張→肺高壓
Murmur 雜音 Low-pitched diastolic rumble (apex), opening snap 低音調舒張期隆隆音(心尖)、開放音
Treatment 治療 PMC (percutaneous mitral commissurotomy), MVR 經皮僧帽瓣分離術、僧帽瓣置換

Mitral Regurgitation (MR) 僧帽瓣逆流

Feature 特徵 Detail 詳情
Etiology 病因 Degenerative (MVP), ischemic, rheumatic, endocarditis 退化性(MVP)、缺血性、風濕性、心內膜炎
Pathophysiology 病理生理 Volume overload → LV dilation, decreased forward output 容量過度負荷→左心室擴張、前向輸出降低
Murmur 雜音 Holosystolic murmur (apex, radiating to axilla) 全收縮期雜音(心尖，放射至腋下)
Indications for surgery 手術適應症 Severe symptomatic MR; Severe asymptomatic with LV dysfunction or AF 嚴重有症狀MR；伴左心室功能障礙或房顫的嚴重無症狀MR

Mitral Valve Repair vs Replacement 修復對比置換

Factor 因素 Repair 修復 Replacement 置換
Advantage 優點 Preserves LV function, lower mortality, no anticoagulation 保留左心室功能、較低死亡率、不需抗凝血 Definitive, better for complex pathology 明確性、較適合複雜病理
Disadvantage 缺點 Technically demanding, risk of recurrence 技術要求高、有復發風險 Valve-related complications, LV dysfunction 瓣膜相關併發症、左心室功能障礙

Tricuspid Regurgitation (TR) 三尖瓣逆流

  • Etiology: Functional (from pulmonary HTN/LV dysfunction), primary (endocarditis, TV dysplasia) 功能性(來自肺高壓/左心室功能障礙)、原發性(心內膜炎、TV發育不良)
  • Indications for surgery: Severe symptomatic TR; Severe TR with LV disease requiring cardiac surgery 手術適應症：嚴重有症狀TR；需心臟手術的左心室病變伴嚴重TR
  • Technique: Tricuspid valve repair (ring annuloplasty) preferred 技術：三尖瓣修復(環形整形)優先

ACC/AHA Stages ACC/AHA分期

Stage 分期 Description 描述 Treatment 治療
A At risk, no structural disease 有風險、無結構性疾病 Risk factor modification 危險因子修正

Ventricular Tachycardia 室性心動過速

  • Secondary prevention: ICD indicated 次級預防：建議ICD
  • VT ablation: For recurrent monomorphic VT 對於復發性單形性室速
  • Surgical: Substrate modification, aneurysmectomy 手術：基質修飾、瘤切除

Pericardial Disease 心包疾病

Pericarditis 心包炎

  • Etiology: Viral (most common), TB, uremic, post-MI (Dressler syndrome) 病因：病毒性(最常見)、結核、尿毒症、術後(DRESSLER症候群)
  • Presentation: Pleuritic chest pain, friction rub, diffuse ST elevation 表現：胸膜性胸痛、摩擦音、瀰漫性ST上升
  • Treatment: NSAIDs, colchicine, treat underlying cause 治療：NSAID、秋水仙鹼、治療根本原因

Pericardial Effusion/Tamponade 心包積液/填塞

  • Beck’s triad: Hypotension, JVD, muffled heart sounds 貝克氏三聯：低血壓、頸靜脈怒張、心音悶
  • Pulsus paradoxus: >10 mmHg SBP drop with inspiration 奇脈：吸氣時收縮壓下降>10mmHg
  • Treatment: Pericardiocentesis (echo-guided), surgical window 治療：心包穿刺(超音引導)、心包窗

Constrictive Pericarditis 縮窄性心包炎

  • Pathophysiology: Rigid pericardium → impaired filling 病理生理：僵硬心包→填充受損
  • Presentation: Right heart failure, Kussmaul’s sign (JVD with inspiration) 表現：右心衰竭、庫斯莫徵(吸氣時頸靜脈怒張)
  • Treatment: Pericardiectomy (only cure) 治療：心包切除術(唯一根治)

Cardiac Tumors 心臟腫瘤

Primary Benign 原發良性

Tumor 腫瘤 Features 特徵
Myxoma 黏液瘤 Most common primary cardiac tumor; LA (75%); “tumor of the fossa ovalis”; surgical excision 最常見原發心臟腫瘤；75%在左心房；“卵圓窩腫瘤”；手術切除
Papillary fibroelastoma 乳頭狀纖維彈力瘤 Most common valve tumor; mobile, pedunculated 最常見瓣膜腫瘤；活動性、有莖
Rhabdomyoma 橫紋肌瘤 Associated with tuberous sclerosis; often regresses 關聯結節性硬化；常自行消退

Primary Malignant 原發惡性

Tumor 腫瘤 Features 特徵
Angiosarcoma 血管肉瘤 Most common malignant; RA; poor prognosis 最常見惡性；右心房；預後差
Rhabdomyosarcoma 橫紋肌肉瘤 No predilection for chamber 無特定心腔好發

Secondary/Metastatic 續發性/轉移性

  • More common than primary 比原發性更常見
  • Lung, breast, melanoma, lymphoma commonly metastasize to heart 肺癌、乳癌、黑色素瘤、淋巴瘤常轉移至心臟

Postoperative Complications 術後併發症

Complication 併發症 Incidence 發生率 Prevention/Treatment 預防/治療
Postoperative AF 術後房顫 15-40% β-blockers, amiodarone, anticoagulation
Bleeding 出血 2-5% Re-exploration, hemostasis 再開刀、止血
Infection (mediastinitis) 感染(縱隔炎) 1-2% Sternal precautions, antibiotics 胸骨注意事項、抗生素
Stroke 中風 1-3% Careful cannulation, TEE, BP control 謹慎插管、TEE、血壓控制
Renal failure 腎衰竭 2-5% Avoid hypoperfusion, maintain perfusion pressure 避免低灌流、維持灌流壓
Respiratory failure 呼吸衰竭 2-5% Ventilator weaning, pulmonary toilet 呼吸器脫離、肺部清潔
Graft occlusion 移植血管阻塞 Early: rare 早期：罕見 Aspirin, antiplatelet therapy 阿斯匹靈、抗血小板

Cardiac Rehabilitation 心臟康復

Phase I (Inpatient) 第一期(住院)

  • Early mobilization 早期活動
  • Education on activity progression 活動進展教育
  • Discharge planning 出院計畫

Phase II (Outpatient) 第二期(門診)

  • Supervised exercise (3x/week for 12 weeks) 監督下運動(每週3次，共12週)
  • Risk factor modification 危險因子修正
  • Psychological support 心理支持

Phase III (Long-term) 第三期(長期)

  • Independent exercise maintenance 獨立運動維持
  • Continued lifestyle modification 持續生活型態修正
