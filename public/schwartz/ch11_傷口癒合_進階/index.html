CH11 傷口癒合進階 (Advanced Wound Healing)

Source: Schwartz’s Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

傷口癒合的組織特異性 (Healing in Specific Tissues)

胃腸道傷口癒合 (GI Tract Healing)

胃腸道修復有獨特考量，包括漿膜癒合與黏膜癒合的差異。吻合口強度取決於縫合 holding capacity，尤其是 submucosa 層。

縫合/吻合原則： 無單一技術優於其他。

  • hand-sutured vs. stapled
  • continuous vs. interrupted
  • absorbable vs. nonabsorbable
  • single-layer vs. two-layer

Stapled ileocolic anastomosis leak rate 低於 hand-constructed。但 small bowel/colonic anastomosis 無此差異。

Early collagenolysis（早期膠原蛋白分解）： 術後前 3-5 天，膠原蛋白分解超過合成，稱為 “weak period”。Colon collagenase 活性比 small bowel 高。Colon fibroblasts 比 skin fibroblasts 產生更多膠原蛋白。

骨骼癒合 (Bone Healing)

骨折癒合階段：血腫形成 → 發炎 → 軟骨痂 → 硬骨痂 → 重塑

階段 時間 內容
血腫形成 受傷後立即 骨折端出血形成血腫
發炎期 1-3 天 炎症細胞浸潤，清除壞死組織
軟骨痂 3-4 天 soft callus bridge 在骨裂縫兩端形成
硬骨痂 2-3 個月 礦化軟骨 → 骨；可承受 weight-bearing
重塑 數月至數年 哈佛系統重建，恢復骨強度

BMP (Bone Morphogenetic Proteins)： 屬於 TGF-β 超家族。刺激間質細胞分化為軟骨母細胞或骨母細胞。

肌腱/韌帶癒合 (Tendon & Ligament Healing)

癒合階段：血腫形成 → 肉芽組織 → 纖維組織沉積 → 疤痕形成

癒合過程中，matrix 含 type I 和 type III collagen、water、DNA、glycosaminoglycan。低血管肌腱癒合慢、疤痕多。

恢復機械完整性： 可能永遠無法達到未受傷肌腱水準。

神經癒合 (Nerve Healing)

三種神經損傷分類：

類型 損傷程度
Neurapraxia 局部脫髓鞘；軸突完整
Axonotmesis 軸突中斷但 Schwann cell basal lamina 完整
Neurotmesis 完全切斷

神經再生三要素：

  1. 神經細胞本體存活
  2. 軸突穿越斷端生長
  3. 正確連接到遠端目標

Wallerian degeneration： 吞噬細胞清除遠端退化軸突和髓鞘。

癒合相關生長因子：NGF, BDNF, bFGF, neuroleukin。

軟骨癒合 (Cartilage Healing)

Superficial injury（淺層損傷）： 無發炎反應，軟骨細胞增加 proteoglycan 和 collagen 合成。癒合能力有限。

Deep injury（深層損傷）： 涉及骨頭和軟組織。血管長入 → 肉芽組織形成 → 可能形成纖維軟骨。

胎兒傷口癒合 (Fetal Wound Healing)

主要特徵：無疤痕癒合。

過渡期大約在第三孕程開始。此後癒合轉為典型成人模式（有疤痕）。

胎兒環境特殊之處：

  • sterile：無菌環境
  • temperature-stable fluid：溫度穩定的羊水

發炎反應較弱： 胎兒嗜中性球和巨噬細胞數量少。

TGF-β 缺失：

  • TGF-β1/β2 阻斷可減少成人疤痕
  • TGF-β3 表達增加與無疤痕癒合相關

Hyaluronic acid 豐富： 胎兒傷口產生大量 hyaluronic acid。膠原蛋白 pattern 為網狀（reticular），與正常組織相似。

傷口分類與癒合方式 (Wound Classification & Healing Types)

類型 說明
Primary intention（一期癒合） 乾淨切口、縫線關閉
Secondary intention（二期癒合） 開放傷口、靠肉芽組織和攣縮癒合
Tertiary intention（三期癒合） 開放數天後延遲縫合

影響傷口癒合的因素 (Factors Affecting Wound Healing)

全身因素

因素 對傷口癒合的影響
高齡 膠原蛋白合成不變但非膠原蛋白堆積減少；上皮的延遲癒合；整體導致延遲而非不癒合
營養不良 Protein-calorie malnutrition → 膠原蛋白沉積減少、傷口感染增加、免疫低下
糖尿病 發炎減少、血管新生受阻、膠原蛋白合成減少；高血糖和胰島素抗性加劇
尿毒症 膠原蛋白合成減少；透析可改善
類固醇 抑制發炎（血管新生、嗜中性球/巨噬細胞遷移、纖維母細胞增生）；topical vitamin A 可逆轉
化療藥物 抑制細胞增生、DNA/蛋白質合成、纖維蛋白沉積；術前給藥影響最大
肥胖 脂肪組織代謝活躍（產生發炎因子和 adipokines）；傷口併發症增加（dehiscence、SSI、hernia、seroma）
抽菸 組織缺血；CO 和尼古丁減少組織血流和氧合

局部因素

  • 缺氧/低灌注： 膠原蛋白合成需要氧氣（prolyl hydroxylase 的 cofactor）
  • 感染： >10⁵ microorganisms/g 顯著增加感染風險
  • 壞死組織/異物： 延遲癒合
  • 水腫： 影響血流

傷口癒合的營養支持 (Nutritional Support for Healing)

蛋白質

0% 或 4% 蛋白質飲食導致：

  • 膠原蛋白沉積減少
  • 傷口強度下降
  • 傷口感染增加

蛋白質-能量營養不良（PEM）患者：hydroxyproline accumulation 減少、傷口併發症增加。

氨基酸

Arginine： 增強纖維母細胞增生。健康成人補充 30g arginine/day × 14 天 → 傷口膠原蛋白沉積增加。

Arginine + β-hydroxy-β-methylbutyrate + glutamine： 顯著增強老年人膠原蛋白沉積。

維生素

Vitamin C：

  • 缺乏導致 scurvy（壞血病）
  • 膠原蛋白合成和交聯受損
  • Wound infection 增加
  • 正常人 RDA = 60 mg/day
  • 重傷/燒傷可增至 2 g/day

Vitamin A：

  • 增強發炎反應
  • 增加巨噬細胞流入和活化
  • 逆轉類固醇對傷口癒合的抑制
  • 劑量：25,000–100,000 IU/day

礦物質

Zinc：

  • 超過 150 種酶的必需 cofactor
  • 鋅缺乏 → 纖維母細胞增生減少、膠原蛋白合成減少、上皮延遲癒合
  • 補充僅對缺乏者有效

傷口感染的預防與治療 (Wound Infection Prevention & Treatment)

抗生素預防原則

  • 目標：手術切口時組織中已有足夠抗生素濃度
  • Class II–IV 手術： 預防性抗生素有效
  • Class I（clean）手術： 通常不需要預防性抗生素
  • 紅黴素 + neomycin（口服） 用於結直腸手術
  • 手術時間超過抗生素 half-life → 需要重複給藥
  • MRSA/VRE 出現限制了某些抗生素的常規使用

手術切口分類與感染率

Class 類型 感染率
I Clean（清潔） <5%
II Clean-contaminated（清潔-污染） 5-10%
III Contaminated（污染） 10-20%
IV Dirty（骯髒） >30%

常見致病菌

  • S. aureus（凝固酶陽性）
  • S. epidermidis（凝固酶陰性）
  • Enterococci
  • E. coli

傷口攣縮與癒合不良 (Wound Contraction & Poor Healing)

攣縮機制

Myofibroblast： 含 α-smooth muscle actin 的纖維母細胞，負責攣縮。

  • 傷口邊緣纖維母細胞在 day 6 開始表達 α-SMA
  • 持續約 15 天
  • 4 週後可能發生凋亡

Fibroblast contraction： 纖維母細胞在膠原蛋白 lattice 中移動並收縮（不需表達 stress fibers）。

慢性傷口

正常癒合超過 4 週 → 轉變為慢性傷口。

病理性疤痕

類型 特徵
Keloid（蟹足腫） 超出原始傷口邊界的病理性疤痕；常見於深色皮膚人種
Hypertrophic scar（肥厚性疤痕） 停留在傷口邊界內的病理性疤痕

Other high reliability industries and organizations include aircraft carrier flight decks, nuclear power plants, and the Federal Aviation Administration’s air traffic control system. In fact, one reason why nuclear power plants have such an excellent reliability record may be that their operators are often former naval submarine officers whose pre-vious experience and training within one highly reliable organi-zation are easily transferable to other organizations.One of the assumptions underlying the science of high reliability organizations is that humans who operate and manage complex systems are themselves not sufficiently complex to sense and anticipate the problems generated by the system.This introduces another important idea undergirding the sci -ence of patient safety: the concept of normal accident theory. Instead of attributing accidents to individual error, this theory states that accidents are intrinsic to high-volume activities and even inevitable in some settings. Accidents should not be used merely to identify and punish the person at fault, but should be seen as a systems problem and addressed at a broader level. Several complications of urinary cath -eters can occur that lead to an increased length of hospital stay and morbidity. In general, use of urinary catheters should be minimized and every opportunity to expeditiously remove them should be encouraged. If needed, it is recommended that the catheter be inserted its full length up to the hub and that urine flow is established before the balloon is inflated because mis -placement of the catheter in the urethra with premature inflation of the balloon can lead to tears and disruption of the urethra.Enlarged prostatic tissue can make catheter insertion dif-ficult, and a catheter coudé may be required. If this attempt is also unsuccessful, then a urologic consultation for endoscopic placement of the catheter may be required to prevent harm to the urethra. For patients with urethral strictures, filiform-tipped catheters and followers may be used, but these can potentially cause bladder injury. If endoscopic attempts fail, the patient may require a percutaneously placed suprapubic catheter to obtain decompression of the bladder. Follow-up investigations of these patients are recommended so definitive care of the ure-thral abnormalities can be pursued.The most frequent nosocomial infection is urinary tract infection (UTI). These infections are classified into compli -cated and uncomplicated forms. The uncomplicated type is a UTI that can be treated with outpatient antibiotic therapy. The complicated UTI usually involves a hospitalized patient with an indwelling catheter whose UTI is diagnosed as part of a fever workup. The interpretation of urine culture results of less than 100,000 CFU/mL is controversial. Before treating such a patient, one should change the catheter and then repeat the cul-ture to see if the catheter was simply colonized with organisms. Cultures with more than 100,000 CFU/mL should be treated with the appropriate antibiotics and the catheter changed or removed as soon as possible. One of the most debilitating infections is an empyema, or infection of the pleural space. Frequently, an overwhelming pneumonia is the source of an empyema, but a retained hemothorax, systemic sepsis, esophageal perforation from any cause, and infections with a predilection for the lung (e.g., tuberculosis) are potential etiologies as well. The diag -nosis is confirmed by chest X-ray or CT scan, followed by aspiration of pleural fluid for bacteriologic analysis. Gram’s stain, lactate dehydrogenase, protein, pH, and cell count are obtained, and broad-spectrum antibiotics are initiated while the laboratory studies are performed. Once the specific organisms are confirmed, anti-infective agents are tailored appropriately.
