# CHAPTER 25 ESOPHAGUS AND DIAPHRAGMATIC HERNIA 食道與橫膈膜疝氣

source: Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

---

## Key Points 重點摘要

1. **GERD** is a chronic disease requiring lifelong management. Antireflux surgery is the only treatment that restores the gastroesophageal barrier.
2. **Barrett's esophagus** (CLE) is a premalignant condition with risk of progression to esophageal adenocarcinoma. It requires surveillance.
3. **Esophageal cancer** staging (TNM) determines treatment approach. Multimodality therapy improves survival.
4. **Achalasia** treatment: pneumatic dilation, POEM, or surgical myotomy (Heller myotomy).
5. **Esophageal perforation** requires early diagnosis and treatment. Contrast esophagogram for diagnosis.
6. **Hiatal hernia** classification (I-IV) guides treatment. Giant paraesophageal hernias (Type III) require surgical repair.
7. **Eosinophilic esophagitis** is an allergic/immune-mediated disease. Topical steroids are first-line treatment.
8. **Scleroderma esophagus** results in absent peristalsis and severe GERD. Treatment is medical.

## Esophageal Anatomy 食道解剖學

### 重要測量

| Landmark 地標 | Distance 距離 |
|--------------|-------------|
| 門齒到環咽肌 | 15 cm |
| 門齒到LES | 40 cm |
| 門齒到賁門 | 40-45 cm |
| 主動脈弓水平 | T4 (25 cm) |
| 總長度 | ~25 cm (cervical 5 cm + thoracic 20 cm) |

### 食道分段

- **Cervical esophagus:** C6 to T1 (約5 cm)
- **Thoracic esophagus:** T1 to diaphragm (約20 cm)
  - Upper 1/3: upper sphincter to aortic arch 上1/3：上括約肌到主動脈弓
  - Middle 1/3: aortic arch to inferior pulmonary vein 中1/3：主動服弓到下肺靜脈
  - Lower 1/3: inferior pulmonary vein to GE junction 下1/3：下肺靜脈到胃食道接合處

### 食道括約肌

| Sphincter 括約肌 | Location 位置 | Function 功能 |
|-----------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Upper esophageal sphincter (UES)** | C6 (cricopharyngeus) | 防止吸入 |
| **Lower esophageal sphincter (LES)** | T11 (diaphragm) | 防止胃食道逆流 |

### 血液供應

| Region 區域 | Arterial Supply 動脈供應 |
|------------|----------------------|
| **Cervical** 頸部 | Inferior thyroid artery (branch of thyrocervical trunk) 甲狀腺下动脉(甲狀腺頸幹分支) |
| **Upper thoracic** 上胸段 | Bronchial arteries, intercostal arteries 支氣管动脉、肋間动脉 |
| **Lower thoracic** 下胸段 | Left gastric artery, short gastric arteries 左胃动脉、短胃血管 |
| **Abdominal** 腹部 | Left gastric artery 左胃动脉 |

### 淋巴引流

- **Cervical:** Deep cervical nodes → supraclavicular nodes 頸深淋巴結→鎖骨上淋巴結
- **Thoracic:** Paratracheal, mediastinal nodes 氣管旁、縱隔淋巴結
- **Abdominal:** Left gastric (celiac) nodes 左胃(腹腔)淋巴結

## Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD) 胃食道逆流疾病

### Epidemiology 流行病學

- 40-50% of Western population experiences heartburn monthly 西方人口40-50%每月有火燒心
- 20% have weekly symptoms 20%每週有症狀
- **50 million Americans** have nighttime heartburn at least weekly 至少5000萬美國人每週有夜間火燒心
- 63% report GERD affects sleep and next-day work 63%報告GERD影響睡眠及次日工作
- 45% report current remedies don't relieve all symptoms 45%報告目前治療無法完全緩解症狀

### Pathophysiology 病理生理學

**主要機轉:**

- **Transient LES relaxations (tLESRs)** — most common mechanism 短暫性LES弛緩(最常見機轉)
- **LES hypotension** — low baseline tone LES低張力
- **Hiatal hernia** — disrupts LES competency 食道裂孔疝氣
- **Delayed gastric emptying** — increases gastric volume 胃排空延遲

**影響因素:**

- Obesity (increased intra-abdominal pressure) 肥胖
- Pregnancy 懷孕
- Smoking 吸菸
- Foods: chocolate, fatty foods, alcohol, coffee 食物
- Medications: anticholinergics, calcium channel blockers, nitrates 藥物

### Clinical Presentation 臨床表現

**典型症狀:**

- **Heartburn** (substernal burning, epigastric → upward radiation) 火燒心
- **Regurgitation** (effortless return of acid/bitter contents) 逆流
- **Dysphagia** (if complicated) 吞嚥困難(如併發)

**非典型症狀:**

- Chronic cough 慢性咳嗽
- Hoarseness 聲音嘶啞
- Chest pain (需排除心源性) 胸痛
- Asthma 氣喘
- Recurrent pneumonia/aspiration 復發性肺炎/吸入

### Diagnosis 診斷

| Test 檢查 | Purpose 目的 |
|----------|------------|
| **Empirical PPI trial** 經驗性PPI試驗 | Initial management 初始處理 |
| **Upper endoscopy** 上消化道內視鏡 | Evaluate esophagitis, Barrett's, stricture, malignancy 評估食道炎、Barrett's、狹窄、惡性腫瘤 |
| **24-hour pH monitoring** 24小時pH監測 | Gold standard for reflux diagnosis 逆流診斷黃金標準 |
| **Barium esophagogram** 鋇劑食道造影 | Evaluate anatomy, strictures, hiatal hernia 評估解剖、狹窄、疝氣 |
| **Esophageal manometry** 食道動力學 | Evaluate motility disorders 評估動力障礙 |

### Treatment 治療

**Lifestyle modifications 生活方式調整:**

- Weight loss 減重
- Head of bed elevation 床頭抬高
- Small frequent meals 少量多餐
- Avoid triggers (foods, alcohol, smoking) 避免誘發因子
- No eating 2-3 hours before bedtime 睡前2-3小時不進食

**Medical therapy 藥物治療:**

| Drug 藥物 | Mechanism 機轉 | Examples 範例 |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Antacids** 制酸劑 | Neutralize acid 中和酸 | Tums, Maalox |
| **H2 blockers** H2阻斷劑 | Reduce acid secretion 減少酸分泌 | Famotidine, Ranitidine |
| **PPIs** 氫離子泵抑制劑 | Inhibit H+/K+ ATPase 抑制氫離子泵 | Omeprazole, Pantoprazole |
| **Prokinetics** 促動力藥 | Improve gastric emptying 促進胃排空 | Metoclopramide |

**PPI strategy:**

- 8-week initial course 初始8週療程
- Step-down to lowest effective dose 降至最低有效劑量
- Long-term/"as-needed" for mild cases 輕度病例長期/必要時使用
- Maintenance for severe cases 重度病例維持治療

**Surgical therapy (Antireflux surgery, Nissen fundoplication) 手術治療:**

- **Indications 適應症:**
  - Failed medical therapy 藥物治療失敗
  - Patient preference (young patients, lifelong medication concerns) 患者偏好
  - Large hiatal hernia 大型食道裂孔疝氣
  - Extraesophageal manifestations 食道外表現
- **Complications:** dysphagia, gas bloat, wrap failure 併發症

## Barrett's Esophagus Barrett's食道

### Definition 定義

- Columnar-lined esophagus (CLE) — intestinal metaplasia replacing squamous epithelium
- **Premalignant condition** — progresses to dysplasia → esophageal adenocarcinoma
- 0.5-1% annual risk of adenocarcinoma 每年0.5-1%腺癌風險

### Diagnosis 診斷

- **Endoscopy:** Salmon-colored mucosa extending above GE junction
- **Histology:** Intestinal metaplasia (goblet cells)
- **NBI (Narrow Band Imaging)** helps identify mucosal irregularities

### Risk Stratification 風險分層

| Type 類型 | Description 描述 | Cancer Risk 癌症風險 |
|----------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Non-dysplastic** 無發育不良 | No dysplasia | Low (~0.5%/year) |
| **Low-grade dysplasia** 低度發育不良 | Low-grade cellular atypia | Intermediate |
| **High-grade dysplasia** 高度發育不良 | High-grade cellular atypia | High (6-19%/year) |

### Surveillance Strategy 監測策略

| Situation 情況 | Recommendation 建議 |
|--------------|-------------------|
| **Non-dysplastic** 無發育不良 | EGD every 3-5 years 每3-5年追蹤 |
| **Low-grade dysplasia** 低度發育不良 | EGD every 6-12 months or endoscopic eradication 每6-12個月或內視鏡根除 |
| **High-grade dysplasia** 高度發育不良 | Endoscopic eradication (EMR, RFA) 內視鏡根除 |

### Treatment 治療

**Endoscopic eradication therapy 內視鏡根除治療:**

- **EMR (Endoscopic Mucosal Resection):** visible lesions 可見病灶
- **RFA (Radiofrequency Ablation):** flat dysplastic tissue 扁平發育不良組織
- **Cryotherapy** 冷凍治療

## Esophageal Motility Disorders 食道動力障礙

### Achalasia 弛緩不能

**定義:** LES無法弛緩 + 食道體部蠕動消失

**病因:**

- Primary (idiopathic): Loss of inhibitory neurons (NO, VIP) 原發性(特發性)
- Secondary: Chagas disease, gastric cancer, PCV 續發性

**臨床表現:**

- **Dysphagia** (progressive, for solids AND liquids) 吞嚥困難(漸進性，固體和液體都有)
- Regurgitation (undigested food) 逆流(未消化食物)
- Chest pain 胸痛
- Weight loss 體重減輕

**診斷:**

- **Barium esophagogram:** "bird's beak" narrowing at GEJ, dilated esophagus
- **Upper endoscopy:** Rule out malignancy 排除惡性腫瘤
- **Esophageal manometry:** Gold standard
  - IRP >15 mmHg (incomplete LES relaxation)
  - Absent peristalsis

**治療:**

| Treatment 治療 | Indication 適應症 |
|--------------|------------------|
| **Pneumatic dilation** 氣球擴張 | Most adult patients 大部分成年患者 |
| **POEM (Peroral Endoscopic Myotomy)** 經口內視鏡肌肉切開術 | Alternative to laparoscopic Heller myotomy 腹腔鏡Heller手術替代 |
| **Laparoscopic Heller myotomy** 腹腔鏡Heller肌肉切開術 | Gold standard surgical 黃金標準手術 |
| **Medical (CCB, nitrates)** 藥物治療 | Poor surgical candidates 手術風險高患者 |

### Diffuse Esophageal Spasm (DES) 瀰漫性食道痙攣

**特徵:**

- Simultaneous contractions in >20% of swallows >20%吞嚥時同時收縮
- Chest pain (intermittent) 胸痛(間歇性)
- Dysphagia 吞嚥困難

**治療:**

- Medical: CCBs, nitrates 藥物治療
- Endoscopic: Botox injection 內視鏡注射
- Surgery: Extended myotomy (rarely needed) 手術

### Jackhammer Esophagus 胡嚕嚕食道

- Hypercontractile esophagus (≥20% swallows with distal contractile integral >8000 mmHg·cm·s)
- Treatment: Medical management, POEM

## Eosinophilic Esophagitis (EoE) 嗜伊紅性食道炎

### 特徵

- Allergic/immune-mediated 過敏/免疫媒介
- Symptoms: dysphagia, food impaction, heartburn 症狀
- Endoscopy: concentric rings, furrows, white spots 內視鏡所見
- Biopsy: ≥15 eosinophils/HPF 切片確診

### 治療

| Treatment 治療 | Details 詳情 |
|--------------|------------|
| **Topical corticosteroids** 局部類固醇 | Fluticasone, budesonide (first-line) |
| **Elimination diet** 排除飲食 | 6-food elimination diet 六種食物排除飲食 |
| **Proton pump inhibitors** PPI | PPI-responsive EoE |
| **Dupilumab** | Anti-IL-4/IL-13 (biologic therapy) |

## Scleroderma Esophagus 硬皮症食道

### 特徵

- **Absent peristalsis** in 75-85% 75-85%無蠕動
- Severe GERD due to incompetent LES 括約肌功能不全導致嚴重GERD
- Risk of Barrett's, stricture Barrett's、狹窄風險

### 治療

- Aggressive acid suppression (PPIs) 積極抑酸
- Prokinetics (limited efficacy) 促動力藥(效果有限)
- Surveillance for Barrett's Barrett's監測

## Hiatal Hernia 食道裂孔疝氣

### 分型 (Belsey/Allison)

| Type 類型 | 內容 | 頻率 |
|----------|------|------|
| **Type I (Sliding)** 滑動型 | GEJ slides above diaphragm | 90%+ |
| **Type II (Paraesophageal)** 旁食道型 | GEJ in normal position, fundus herniates | Rare |
| **Type III (Mixed)** 混合型 | Both GEJ and fundus herniate | 5-10% |
| **Type IV (Complex)** 複雜型 | Other organs (colon, spleen, pancreas) | Rare |

### 臨床表現

- **Type I:** Usually asymptomatic, may have GERD symptoms 通常無症狀，可能有GERD
- **Type II-IV:** Postprandial fullness, chest pain, dysphagia, obstruction 飯後腹脹、胸痛、吞嚥困難、阻塞

### 治療

**Type I (Sliding):**

- Lifestyle modifications 生活調整
- PPI therapy PPI治療
- Surgery if refractory or complications 頑抗或併發症時手術

**Type II-IV (Paraesophageal/Giant):**

- **Surgical repair recommended** (even asymptomatic — risk of strangulation) 建議手術修補(即使無症狀—有嵌頓風險)
- Laparoscopic approach 腹腔鏡方式
- Collis gastroplasty if short esophagus 短食道時做Collis胃整形

---

## Giant Diaphragmatic Hernias 巨大橫膈膜疝氣

### 定義

- >30% of stomach in chest >30%胃在胸腔
- Often Type III hiatal hernia 通常為Type III食道裂孔疝氣

### 手術適應症

- All symptomatic patients 所有有症狀患者
- Asymptomatic patients: controversial (consider age, comorbidities) 無症狀患者：有爭議(考慮年齡、共病症)

### 手術原則

- **Laparoscopic or open approach** 腹腔鏡或開腹
- **Reduction of hernia contents** 回復疝氣內容物
- **Excision of hernia sac** 切除疝氣囊
- **Repair of diaphragmatic defect** (primary repair or mesh) 修補橫膈缺陷
- **Fundoplication** (to prevent reflux) 胃底摺疊術
- **Collis gastroplasty** if short esophagus 短食道時

## Esophageal Cancer 食道癌

### Epidemiology 流行病學

- **SCC (Squamous cell carcinoma):** More common worldwide; associated with tobacco, alcohol
- **Adenocarcinoma:** More common in Western countries; associated with BE, obesity, GERD

### Risk Factors 危險因子

**SCC:**

- Tobacco 菸草
- Alcohol 酒精
- Nitrosamines (diet) 亞硝胺
- Achalasia 弛緩不能
- Thermal injury (hot beverages) 熱損傷

**Adenocarcinoma:**

- Barrett's esophagus Barrett's食道
- Obesity 肥胖
- GERD
- Male sex 男性
- Caucasian race 白人

### Staging (TNM 8th edition) 分期

**T (Primary Tumor) 原發腫瘤:**

| Stage 分期 | 內容 |
|-----------|------|
| Tis | High-grade dysplasia 高度發育不良 |
| T1a | Lamina propria 黏膜固有層 |
| T1b | Submucosa 黏膜下層 |
| T2 | Muscularis propria 肌肉層 |
| T3 | Adventitia 外膜 |
| T4a | Pleura, pericardium, diaphragm, azygous vein 肋膜、心包、橫膈膜、奇靜脈 |
| T4b | Aorta, vertebral body, trachea 主動服、椎體、氣管 |

**N (Lymph Nodes) 淋巴結:**

- N0: No regional nodes 無區域淋巴結侵犯
- N1: 1-2 nodes 1-2個
- N2: 3-6 nodes 3-6個
- N3: >6 nodes >6個

**M (Distant Metastasis) 遠處轉移:**

- M0: No 無
- M1: Yes (cervical nodes, celiac nodes = M1)

### Treatment 治療

**Early stage (T1a):**

- **EMR (Endoscopic Mucosal Resection)** or ESD
- Endoscopic ablation (RFA) for Barrett's with cancer

**Locally advanced (T1b or beyond):**

- **Multimodality therapy 多模式治療:**
  - Neoadjuvant chemoradiation (CROSS protocol: carboplatin/paclitaxel + 41.4 Gy) → surgery
  - Or definitive chemoradiation (for SCC, especially upper/middle esophagus)
- **Surgical approaches 手術方式:**
  - **Ivor Lewis (transhiatal):** Laparoscopy + right thoracotomy (intrathoracic anastomosis)
  - **McKeown:** Right thoracotomy + laparotomy + cervical anastomosis
  - **Transhiatal:** No thoracotomy (for selected patients)

**Palliative 緩解治療:**

- Chemoradiation 化放療
- Stent placement 支架置放
- Laser ablation 雷射燒灼
- Feeding tube (J tube) 餵食管

### Prognosis 預後

- Overall 5-year survival: ~20% 整體5年存活率
- Improved with multimodality therapy 多模式治療可改善
- Nodal involvement significantly worsens prognosis 淋巴結侵犯顯著惡化預後

## Esophageal Trauma and Perforation 食道創傷與穿孔

### Etiology 病因

| Type 類型 | Cause 原因 |
|----------|-----------|
| **Iatrogenic** 醫源性 | Endoscopy, dilation, TEE (most common) |
| **Boerhaave syndrome** | Spontaneous (forceful vomiting) 自發性(用力嘔吐) |
| **Traumatic** 外傷性 | Penetrating injury 穿刺傷 |
| **Caustic injury** 腐蝕性損傷 | Ingestion of caustic substances 吞入腐蝕物 |

### Diagnosis 診斷

- **Contrast esophagogram:** Gastrografin first (if suspicion), then barium for negative cases
- **CT scan:** Perforation, mediastinal air, fluid
- **Upper endoscopy:** Not first-line (may worsen perforation)

### Treatment Principles 治療原則

| Situation 情況 | Treatment 治療 |
|--------------|--------------|
| **Cervical perforation** 頸部穿孔 | Broad-spectrum antibiotics, NPO, observation 廣效抗生素、NPO、觀察 |
| **Intrathoracic perforation** 胸內穿孔 | Surgery (primary repair) if early (<24h) 早期(<24小時)手術(初步修復) |
| **Late presentation (>24h)** 晚期表現 | Diversion, drainage, or conservative management 轉向、引流或保守治療 |
| **Boerhaave (transection)** | Emergency surgery (primary repair + drainage) 緊急手術 |

### Prognostic Factors 預後因子

- Time to diagnosis (>24h worse) 診斷時間
- Location (cervical better than thoracic) 位置
- Contamination 污染程度
- Underlying esophageal disease 底層食道疾病

## Principles of Diaphragmatic Hernia Repair 橫膈膜疝氣修補原則

### 手術入路

- **Laparoscopic** (preferred for elective cases) 腹腔鏡(選擇性病例優先)
- **Open (thoracic or abdominal)** for complex/emergency cases 開腹/開胸(複雜/緊急病例)

### 修補技術

1. **Reduction** of herniated contents 回復內容物
2. **Excision of hernia sac** 切除疝氣囊
3. **Primary repair** vs. **Mesh repair** (for large defects) 初次修補對網片修補
4. **Fundoplication** (for sliding hernias or to prevent reflux) 胃底摺疊術
5. **Collis gastroplasty** (if short esophagus) 短食道時

### Mesh Repair 網片修補

- **Indications:** Large defects, recurrent hernias 適應症
- **Types:** Synthetic (polyester), biologic, absorbable 類型
- **Position:** Subcutaneous vs. intraperitoneal 位置

---

## Postoperative Considerations 術後注意事項

- **Monitoring:** Dysphagia, aspiration, leak 監測
- **Imaging:** Water-soluble contrast study before oral intake (if concern for leak)
- **Long-term:** Surveillance for recurrence, Barrett's in GERD patients

Treatable diseases of the stomach are common, and it is accessible and relatively forgiving of surgical manipulation. To provide accurate diagnosis and rational treatment, the physician must understand gastric anatomy, physiology, and pathophysiology; this includes a sound understanding of the mechanical, secretory, and endocrine processes by which the stomach accomplishes its important functions and a familiarity with the common benign and malignant gastric disorders. ANATOMY Anatomic Relationships and Gross Morphology The stomach is the most proximal abdominal organ of the digestive tract (Fig. 26-1).7 The part of the stomach attached to the esophagus is called the cardia. Just proximal to the cardia at the gastroesophageal (GE) junction is the anatomically indistinct but physiologically demonstrable lower esophageal sphincter. At the distal end, the readily apparent pyloric sphincter connects the stomach to the proximal duodenum. The stomach is relatively fixed at these points, but the majority of the stomach is quite mobile with the shorter lesser curvature on the right and the longer greater curvature on the left. The superior-most part of the stomach is the distensible fundus, bounded superiorly by the diaphragm and laterally by the spleen. The angle of His is where the fundus meets the left side of the GE junction. Generally, the inferior extent of the fundus is considered to be the horizontal plane of the GE junction, where the body (corpus) of the stomach begins. The body of the stomach contains most of the parietal (oxyntic) cells, some of which are also present in the cardia and fundus. At the angularis incisura, the lesser curvature turns rather abruptly to the right, marking the anatomic beginning of the antrum, which comprises the distal 25% to 30% of the stomach.
