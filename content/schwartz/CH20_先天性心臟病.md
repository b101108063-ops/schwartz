# CHAPTER 20 CONGENITAL HEART DISEASE 先天性心臟病

source: Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

## Congenital Heart Disease Overview 先天性心臟病概述

### Classification 分類

| Type 類型 | Shunt Direction 分流方向 | Cyanosis 發紺 |
|-----------|------------------------|--------------|
| **Left-to-right (acyanotic)** 左至右分流(非發紺型) | ASD, VSD, PDA | No cyanosis 無 |
| **Right-to-left (cyanotic)** 右至左分流(發紺型) | TOF, TGA, Tricuspid atresia | Cyanosis 有 |
| **Obstructive** 阻塞型 | Coarctation, AS, PS | No cyanosis 無 |

### Timing of Presentation 發病時間

| Timing 時間 | Lesions 病變 |
|------------|-------------|
| **Neonatal (immediate)** 新生兒期(立即) | Transposition, critical obstruction 轉位、嚴重阻塞 |
| **First weeks-months** 數週至數月 | Large VSD, coarctation 大型VSD、主動脈窄狹 |
| **Childhood** 兒童期 | ASD, small VSD, mild lesions 房缺、小型室缺、輕度病變 |

## Atrial Septal Defect (ASD) 心房中膈缺損

### Types 類型

| Type 類型 | Location 位置 | Features 特徵 |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Secundum** 續發性孔 | Mid fossa ovalis 卵圓窩中部 | Most common (70%), isolated 最常見，單獨發生 |
| **Primum** 原發性孔 | Lower septum 下部中膈 | Part of AV canal, associated cleft mitral valve 心臟傳導系統的一部分，關聯裂隙性二尖瓣 |
| **Sinus venosus** 靜脈竇型 | Near SVC/IVC entrance 上下腔靜脈入口附近 | Associated anomalous pulmonary veins 關聯肺靜脈回流異常 |

### Pathophysiology 病理生理學

- Left-to-right shunt (LA → RA → RV → PA) 左至右分流
- **Qp:Qs >1.5:1** → indications for closure 為關閉適應症
- Volume overload of right heart 右心容量過度負荷
- Progressive pulmonary vascular disease if untreated 未治療會進展為肺血管疾病

### Clinical Features 臨床表現

- **Fixed split S2** (pathognomonic) 固定性第二心音分裂(特有病徵)
- **Diastolic flow murmur** (tricuspid) 舒張期血流雜音(三尖瓣)
- **Ejection flow murmur** (pulmonary) 噴射性血流雜音(肺动脉)

### Treatment 治療

| Approach 方法 | Indication 適應症 |
|-------------|------------------|
| **Percutaneous closure** 經皮關閉 | Secundum, suitable anatomy 續發性孔， anatomy適宜 |
| **Surgical closure** 手術關閉 | Primum, sinus venosus, large defects 原發性孔、靜脈竇型、大型缺損 |
| **Timing** 時機 | Age 4-5 years (elective) 4-5歲(選擇性) |

### Outcomes 預後

- **Excellent** with closure before age 10 10歲前關閉預後極佳
- RV volumes normalize if closed early 早期關閉右心室容積可恢復正常
- Stroke risk reduced after closure 關閉後中風風險降低

## Ventricular Septal Defect (VSD) 心室中膈缺損

### Types 類型

| Type 類型 | Location 位置 | Features 特徵 |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Perimembranous** 膜周邊 | Membranous septum 膜性中膈 | Most common (80%) 最常見 |
| **Muscular** 肌肉型 | Muscular septum 肌肉性中膈 | May be multiple 可能多發 |
| **Inlet** 入口型 | Near AV valves 靠近房室瓣 | Associated with primum ASD 關聯原發性ASD |
| **Doubly committed** 雙側支撐型 | Below aortic/pulmonary valves 介於主/肺动脉瓣下方 | Rare 罕見 |

### Pathophysiology 病理生理學

- Left-to-right shunt (LV → RV → PA) 左至右分流
- **Qp:Qs >1.5-2:1** → closure indicated 為關閉適應症
- Size determines hemodynamic significance 大小決定血液動力學意義

### Clinical Features 臨床表現

- **Holosystolic murmur** (left sternal border) 全收縮期雜音(左胸骨緣)
- Thrill common with large VSD 大型VSD常見震顫
- Signs of heart failure (failure to thrive, tachypnea) 心衰竭徵象(生長遲緩、呼吸急促)

### Treatment 治療

| Size 大小 | Management 處理 |
|----------|----------------|
| **Small VSD** 小型 | Observation (may close spontaneously) 追蹤觀察(可能自行關閉) |
| **Large VSD** 大型 | Surgical closure (patch) 手術關閉(補片) |
| **Mid-size** 中型 | Consider if Qp:Qs >2:1, symptoms 依Qp:Qs比值及症狀評估 |

### Complications 併發症

- Eisenmenger syndrome (reversal to right-to-left) 艾森曼格症候群(逆转为右至左分流)
- Aortic regurgitation (from prolapse) 主動脈瓣逆流(因脫垂)
- Endocarditis 心內膜炎

---

## Patent Ductus Arteriosus (PDA) 開放性肺導管

### Pathophysiology 病理生理學

- Aorta → PA shunting 主動服→肺动脉分流
- Continuous "machinery" murmur 連續性"機械"雜音
- Volume overload of left heart 左心容量過度負荷

### Treatment 治療

| Method 方法 | Agent/Approach 藥物/方法 |
|-----------|------------------------|
| **Medical** 藥物 | Indomethacin, ibuprofen (neonates) 消炎痛、布洛芬(新生兒) |
| **Percutaneous** 經皮 | Amplatzer occlusion device Amplatzer關閉器 |
| **Surgical** 手術 | Ligation, division 結紮、切斷 |

---

## Tetralogy of Fallot (TOF) 法洛氏四重症

### Anatomy (4 Components) 解剖學(四個組成)

1. **VSD** (large, unrestrictive) 大型非限制性VSD
2. **Overriding aorta** 主動脈跨坐
3. **Right ventricular outflow tract obstruction (RVOTO)** 右心室出口阻塞
4. **Right ventricular hypertrophy** 右心室肥大

### Pathophysiology 病理生理學

- Right-to-left shunt through VSD 右至左分流經由VSD
- Degree of cyanosis depends on RVOTO severity 發紺程度取決於RVOTO嚴重度
- "Tet spells" (hypercyanotic episodes) "法洛氏發作"(高發紺發作)

### Clinical Features 臨床表現

- **Cyanosis** (varying severity) 發紺(程度各異)
- **Squatting** (increases SVR, reduces R→L shunt) 蹲踞(增加全身血管阻力，減少右至左分流)
- **Clubbing** 杵狀指
- Systolic ejection murmur (RVOTO) 收縮期噴射性雜音(RVOTO)

### Diagnosis 診斷

- **Echocardiography** (definitive) 超音波心圖(確診)
- Cardiac catheterization (if needed) 心導管檢查(必要時)

### Treatment 治療

| Stage 分期 | Procedure 術式 |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Staged repair** 分期修復 | Initial: Blalock-Taussig shunt (BT shunt) 初始：BT分流 |
| **Complete repair** 完全修復 | VSD closure + RVOTO relief (transannular patch) VSD關閉+RVOTO緩解 |

### Timing 時機

- **Symptomatic infants:** Early repair 有症狀嬰兒：早期修復
- **Blue spells:** Urgent repair 發紺發作：緊急修復
- **Elective:** 3-6 months if stable 選擇性：穩定時3-6個月

### Long-term 長期預後

- Transannular patch → pulmonary regurgitation (may need later valve replacement) 經環補片→肺動脈逆流(未來可能需瓣膜置換)
- 30-year survival >90% 30年存活率>90%

## Coarctation of the Aorta 主動脈窄狹

### Location 位置

- Near ligamentum arteriosum (juxtaductal) 韌帶動脉附近(導管旁)
- 95% at or near ductus 95%發生於導管處或其附近

### Pathophysiology 病理生理學

- Obstruction → Upper body hypertension 阻塞→上半身高血壓
- Collateral circulation (intercostals, internal mammary) 側枝循環(肋間、乳內)
- **Arm-leg BP gradient >20 mmHg** 臂-腿血壓差>20mmHg

### Clinical Features 臨床表現

- **Upper extremity hypertension** 上肢高血壓
- **Radio-femoral delay** 橈股延遲
- **Weak femoral pulses** 股脈微弱
- Systolic murmur (back) 收縮期雜音(背部)

### Associated Lesions 關聯病變

- **Bicuspid aortic valve** (50-85%) 雙葉主動脈瓣
- VSD, ASD 室間隔缺損、房間隔缺損
- Berry aneurysm (Willis circle) 漿果樣動瘤(威利氏環)

### Treatment 治療

| Method 方法 | Indication 適應症 |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Balloon angioplasty** 氣球擴張術 | Infants, discrete stenosis 嬰兒、侷限性窄狹 |
| **Surgical resection + end-to-end anastomosis** 手術切除+對端吻合 | Long segment, infants 長段、嬰兒 |
| **Patch aortoplasty** 補片主動脈整形術 | Recurrent, older children 復發、較大兒童 |
| **Gore-Tex interposition** Gore-Tex置換 | Long gaps 長距離缺損 |

### Complications 併發症

- **Paradoxical hypertension** (post-op) 反常性高血壓(術後)
- Spinal cord injury (rare) 脊髓損傷(罕見)

## Transposition of Great Arteries (TGA) 大血管轉位

### Anatomy 解剖學

- **Aorta** arises from RV 主動脈來自右心室
- **Pulmonary artery** arises from LV 肺动脉來自左心室
- Two parallel circuits (incompatible with life without mixing) 兩套平行循環(不混合無法存活)

### Types 類型

| Type 類型 | Relationship 關係 |
|----------|-----------------|
| **D-TGA (dextro-TGA)** | Aorta anterior and rightward of PA 主動服在肺动脉前方及右側 |
| **L-TGA (levo-TGA)** | Corrected transposition 校正型轉位 |

### Pathophysiology (D-TGA) 病理生理學

- Deoxygenated blood → body (no O₂ delivery) 未氧合血→全身(無氧氣供應)
- Oxygenated blood → lungs (no systemic delivery) 氧合血→肺(無全身供應)
- Survival depends on mixing (PDA, ASD, VSD) 存活取決於混合(PDA、ASD、VSD)

### Treatment 治療

| Procedure 術式 | Description 描述 |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Balloon atrial septostomy** 氣球心房間隔穿破術 | Neonatal (Rashkind) - creates mixing 新生兒期-創造混合 |
| **Arterial switch operation (ASO)** 动脉轉換術 | Aorta and PA translocated to correct ventricles 主肺动脉轉位至正確心室 |
| **Mustard/Senning** 心房轉換術 | Atrial switch (rarely done now) 心房層面轉換(現已少用) |

### Arterial Switch Operation (ASO) 动脉轉換術

- **Timing:** First 2-3 weeks (before LV pressure drops) 時機：前2-3週(LV壓力下降前)
- **Coronary transfer** is critical step 冠狀动脉移植是關鍵步驟
- **Long-term:** Good outcomes, but coronary issues 長期：預後良好，但可能有冠狀动脉問題

## Total Anomalous Pulmonary Venous Connection (TAPVC/TAPVR) 完全性肺靜脈回流異常

### Anatomy 解剖學

- Pulmonary veins → systemic venous system (not LA) 肺靜脈→體靜脈系統(非左心房)
- 4 subtypes based on drainage site 依引流位置分4型

### Types 類型

| Type 類型 | Drainage 引流 | Features 特徵 |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Supracardiac** 心上型 | SVC/innominate 上腔/無名靜脈 | Most common 最常見 |
| **Cardiac** 心臟型 | Coronary sinus 冠狀竇 | |
| **Infracardiac** 心下型 | Portal system 門脈系統 | Risk of obstruction 有阻塞風險 |
| **Mixed** 混合型 | Combination 混合 | |

### With vs Without Obstruction 依是否有阻塞

| Form 型態 | Presentation 表現 | Treatment 治療 |
|----------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Obstructed** 阻塞性 | Severe cyanosis, pulmonary edema 嚴重發紺、肺水腫 | Emergency surgery 緊急手術 |
| **Non-obstructed** 非阻塞性 | Mild cyanosis, heart failure 輕微發紺、心衰竭 | Elective repair 選擇性修復 |

## Tricuspid Atresia 三尖瓣閉鎖

### Features 特徵

- No tricuspid valve 無三尖瓣
- RV hypoplasia 右心室發育不良
- Always associated with ASD 一定關聯ASD
- Always associated with some form of TGA 一定關聯某型TGA

### Treatment 治療

- **Palliative:** Systemic-PA shunt (BT or RV-PA conduit) 緩解：體循環-肺动脉分流(BT或RV-PA導管)
- **Staged Fontan:** Eventually total cavopulmonary connection 分期Fontan：最終全腔肺連接

---

## Fontan Procedure Fontan 手術

### For 適用於

- **Single ventricle** physiology 單心室生理
- Tricuspid atresia, HLHS, other univentricular hearts 三尖瓣閉鎖、左心發育不良症候群、其他單心室心臟

### Evolution 演進

1. **Classic Fontan** (1971): RA → PA direct 經典Fontan：右心房→肺动脉直接
2. **Lateral tunnel Fontan** (1980s): Intracardiac baffle 側通道Fontan：心內分流
3. **Extracardiac Fontan** (1990s): Extracardiac conduit 心外Fontan：心外導管
4. **TCPC (Total Cavopulmonary Connection)** with fenestration TCPC加開窗

### Complications 併發症

- Arrhythmias 心律不整
- Protein-losing enteropathy 蛋白質流失性腸病變
- Plastic bronchitis 塑膠性支氣管炎
- Fontan failure (late) Fontan衰竭(晚期)

## Ebstein's Anomaly 三尖瓣下移畸形

### Anatomy 解剖學

- **Septal and posterior leaflets** displaced into RV 隔葉及後葉移入右心室
- "Atrialized" portion of RV 右心室"心房化"部分
- Tricuspid regurgitation 三尖瓣逆流

### Clinical Features 臨床表現

- Cyanosis (if R→L shunting) 發紺(如右至左分流)
- Arrhythmias (WPW) 心律不整(WPW預激症候群)
- Heart failure 心衰竭

### Treatment 治療

| Approach 方法 | Indication 適應症 |
|-------------|------------------|
| **Medical** 藥物 | Mild symptoms 輕微症狀 |
| **Tricuspid valve repair** 三尖瓣修復 | Moderate-severe TR, symptoms 中重度三尖瓣逆流、症狀 |
| **Starnge procedure** Starnge手術 | Cone reconstruction 圓錐重建 |
| **One-and-a-half ventricular repair** 1.5心室修復 | Severe RV dysfunction 嚴重右心室功能不良 |

## Pediatric Cardiac Surgery Principles 小兒心臟外科原則

### Cardiopulmonary Bypass (CPB) 體外循環

- **Cannulation:** Aorta, vena cava (or right atrium) 插管：主動服、腔靜脈(或右心房)
- **Myocardial protection:** Cardioplegia (crystalloid or blood) 心肌保護：心麻痹液(晶體或血液)
- **Hypothermia:** Provides organ protection 低溫：提供器官保護

### Surgical Approaches 手術入路

| Approach 入路 | Use 用途 |
|-------------|---------|
| **Median sternotomy** 正中胸骨切開 | Most repairs 大部分修復 |
| **Thoracotomy** 胸廓切開 | PDA, coarctation 肺導管、主動脈窄狹 |
| **Minimal incision** 微小切口 | Selected cases 特定病例 |

### Postoperative Care 術後照護

- **Hemodynamic monitoring** 血流動力學監測
- **Ventilator support** (often 24-48h) 呼吸器支援(常需24-48小時)
- **Arrhythmia management** 心律不整處理
- **Fluid/nutrition management** 液體/營養管理

### Long-term Follow-up 長期追蹤

- **Re-intervention** may be needed 可能需要再次介入
- **Neurodevelopmental** surveillance 神經發育監測
- **Exercise restrictions** (some lesions) 運動限制(部分病變)
- **Endocarditis prophylaxis** (certain lesions) 心內膜炎預防(特定病變)

## Adult Congenital Heart Disease 成人先天性心臟病

### Common Issues 常見問題

| Issue 問題 | Lesions 病變 |
|-----------|------------|
| **Residual lesions** 殘餘病變 | Regurgitation, stenosis 逆流、窄狹 |
| **Arrhythmias** 心律不整 | Post-operative scarring, atrial dilation 術後疤痕、心房擴大 |
| **Heart failure** 心衰竭 | Long-term volume/pressure overload 長期容量/壓力過度負荷 |
| **Pregnancy** 懷孕 | Risk assessment needed 需風險評估 |

### Eisenmenger Syndrome 艾森曼格症候群

- **Irreversible pulmonary vascular disease** 不可逆肺血管疾病
- **Reversal of shunt** (R→L) 分流逆轉(右至左)
- **Contraindication to defect closure** 缺損關閉的禁忌症
- **Transplant** (lung or heart-lung) is final option 移植(肺或心肺)是最終選項

### Common Adult Presentations 成人常見表現

- **Unrepaired ASD** → arrhythmias, paradoxical emboli 未修復ASD→心律不整、奇異性栓塞
- **Repaired TOF** → pulmonary regurgitation, arrhythmias 修復後TOF→肺动脉逆流、心律不整
- **Coarctation** → hypertension, berry aneurysm rupture 窄狹→高血壓、漿果樣動瘤破裂
- **Bicuspid aortic valve** → stenosis, dissection risk 雙葉主动脉瓣→窄狹、剝離風險
