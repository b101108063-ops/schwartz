# CH05 休克 (Shock)

**來源：** Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

---

## Key Points

1. **Shock** is defined as a failure to meet the metabolic demands of cells and tissues.
2. A central component of shock is **decreased tissue perfusion**.
3. Physiologic responses to shock include **neuroendocrine, metabolic, and immune/inflammatory signaling**.
4. **Hemorrhagic/hypovolemic shock：** volume resuscitation with blood products; timely control of bleeding is essential.
5. **Prevention of hypothermia, acidemia, and coagulopathy** (lethal triad) is essential in hemorrhagic shock management.
6. **Septic shock：** fluid resuscitation, broad-spectrum antibiotics within 1 hour, source control.
7. Assessment of shock uses **physiologic parameters and markers of tissue perfusion**.
8. Shock exists in phases: **compensated → decompensated → irreversible**。

## 歷史背景 (Historical Background)

| 年份 | 發現 |
|------|------|
| **1872** | Samuel V. Gross：「生命機器的粗暴脫軌」 |
| **Early 20th c.** | Walter Cannon：homeostasis, "fight or flight" response |
| **1934** | Alfred Blalock：四類休克（hypovolemic, vasogenic, cardiogenic, neurogenic） |
| **1947** | Wiggers：不可逆失血性休克模型 |
| **1950s** | G. Tom Shires：third spacing and extracellular fluid deficit |
| **Vietnam War** | ARDS ("shock lung") recognized |

---

## 休克的分類 (Classification of Shock)

| 類型 | 機制 |
|------|------|
| **Hypovolemic/Hemorrhagic** | Loss of circulating blood volume |
| **Cardiogenic** | Heart pump failure |
| **Septic (Vasodilatory)** | Decreased vascular resistance |
| **Neurogenic** | Loss of sympathetic vascular tone |
| **Obstructive** | Mechanical impediment to circulation |
| **Traumatic** | Combined hypovolemia + inflammation |

---

## 休克的病理生理 (Pathophysiology of Shock)

### 細胞層次 (Cellular Level)

**Oxidative metabolism failure：**

- ↓ O₂ delivery (DO₂) or utilization。
- ATP depletion → Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase failure → cellular swelling。
- **Reversible injury：** if perfusion restored in time。
- **Irreversible injury：** prolonged/severe hypoperfusion。

### "Vicious Cycle" of Shock

```
Decreased tissue perfusion
        ↓
Cellular hypoxia/ischemia
        ↓
Parenchymal cell injury
        ↓
Endothelial activation / Microcirculatory dysfunction
        ↓
↑ Capillary leak / ↓ Vascular resistance
        ↓
Persistent hypoperfusion → Hemodynamic collapse → Death
```

### 神經內分泌反應 (Neuroendocrine Response)

**目標：** maintain perfusion to heart and brain (at expense of peripheral organs)。

**感測器 (Afferent signals)：**

- **Baroreceptors：** carotid sinus, aortic arch — respond to pressure/stretch。
- **Chemoreceptors：** respond to hypoxemia, hypercarbia, acidosis。
- **Volume receptors：** atria — activated by low volume。
- **Pain receptors：** spinothalamic tracts → hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis activation。

**交感神經系統效應：**

- <span style="color:#fb923c">Catecholamine</span> release (epinephrine, norepinephrine)。
- Vasoconstriction (α₁ receptors)。
- ↑ Heart rate and contractility (β₁ receptors)。
- Retains sodium and water。

**內分泌反應：**

- **RAAS activation** → angiotensin II + aldosterone。
- **ADH release** → water retention。
- **Cortisol release** (ACTH stimulation)。

## 失血性休克的臨床表現 (Clinical Presentation of Hemorrhagic Shock)

### 各階段 (Stages)

| Stage | Blood Loss | HR | BP | Pulse Pressure | Mental Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | <15% | Normal | Normal | Normal | Slightly anxious |
| **II** | 15–30% | >100 | Normal/↓ | Narrow | Mildly anxious |
| **III** | 30–40% | >120 | ↓ Systolic | Narrow | Confused, anxious |
| **IV** | >40% | >140 | ↓↓ | Very narrow | Lethargic |

### 補償機制 (Compensatory Mechanisms)

- Tachycardia (catecholamines)。
- Vasoconstriction (sympathetic)。
- ↓ Urine output (ADH, RAAS)。
- ↑ Respiratory rate。

---

## 低血量/失血性休克的治療 (Treatment of Hypovolemic/Hemorrhagic Shock)

### 基本原則

1. **控制出血** — most critical (delayed control increases mortality)。
2. **容量復甦** — limited crystalloid, blood products preferred。
3. **避免低溫、酸血症、凝血病** (lethal triad prevention)。
4. **允許性低血壓** (permissive hypotension) in uncontrolled hemorrhage。

### 輸液策略

| 產品 | Indication |
|---|---|
| **LR or NS** | Initial resuscitation |
| **pRBC** | Oxygen-carrying capacity |
| **FFP** | Coagulation factors |
| **Platelets** | Platelet count <50,000 or active bleeding |
| **TXA** | Within 3 hours of injury (CRASH-2) |

### 大量輸血協議 (Massive Transfusion)

**定義：** >10 units pRBC/24h or >4 units/h。

**目標比例 1:1:1：** pRBC : FFP : platelets。

---

## 敗血症休克 (Septic Shock / Vasodilatory Shock)

### 發病機制

- **Vasodilation：** inflammatory mediators (NO, prostaglandins)。
- **Increased capillary permeability。**
- **Myocardial depression。**
- **Mitochondrial dysfunction。**

### 微生物產物

| 來源 | 產物 |
|------|------|
| **Gram-negative** | LPS (lipopolysaccharide) → TLR4 activation |
| **Gram-positive** | Peptidoglycan, teichoic acid, mannan |
| **DAMPs** | HMGB1, heparan sulfate, ATP (from damaged cells) |

### 治療原則

1. **早期診斷** (identifying source of infection)。
2. **液體復甦：** ≥<span style="color:#34d399">30 mL/kg</span> crystalloid within 3 hours。
3. **抗生素：** broad-spectrum within <span style="color:#f87171">1 hour</span>。
4. **血管加壓劑：** norepinephrine (MAP target ≥<span style="color:#34d399">65 mmHg</span>)。
5. **感染源控制：** drainage, debridement, source control。

### 感染源控制的時機

| 情況 | Timing |
|---|---|
| Necrotizing soft tissue infection | Immediate |
| Intra-abdominal infection | Within 12–24h |
| Abscess drainage | Within 24–48h |
| Device/foreign body removal | As soon as possible |

---

## 心因性休克 (Cardiogenic Shock)

### 病因

- **AMI** (most common — LAD occlusion)。
- Arrhythmias。
- Valvular dysfunction。
- Cardiac tamponade。
- Tension pneumothorax (obstructive component)。

### 表現

- Hypotension (SBP < <span style="color:#f87171">90 mmHg</span>)。
- Low cardiac output。
- Pulmonary congestion。
- Peripheral hypoperfusion。

### 治療

- **Revascularization：** PCI (最重要 — early PCI improves survival)。
- **IABP** (historical; less used now)。
- **Impella, LVAD** (mechanical circulatory support)。
- **Inotropes：** dobutamine, milrinone。

---

## 阻塞性休克 (Obstructive Shock)

### 病因

- **Tension pneumothorax。**
- **Cardiac tamponade。**
- **Massive PE** (pulmonary embolism)。
- **Aortic obstruction。**

### 治療

| 病因 | 治療 |
|------|------|
| Tension pneumothorax | **Immediate needle decompression** |
| Cardiac tamponade | **Pericardiocentesis** |
| Massive PE | **Thrombolysis or embolectomy** |

---

## 神經性休克 (Neurogenic Shock)

### 病因

- **脊髓損傷** (especially cervical/thoracic)。
- Spinal anesthesia。
- Brain injury。

### 表現

- **Hypotension + bradycardia** (loss of sympathetic tone)。
- Warm, flushed skin (vasodilation)。
- Poikilothermia。

### 治療

- **Fluid resuscitation。**
- **Vasopressors：** phenylephrine, dopamine, norepinephrine。
- **Atropine** (for bradycardia)。
- Treat spinal injury。

---

## 復甦的終點 (Endpoints of Resuscitation)

### 傳統指標

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| MAP | ≥<span style="color:#34d399">65 mmHg</span> |
| HR | <110 bpm |
| Urine output | ≥<span style="color:#34d399">0.5 mL/kg/h</span> |
| Serum lactate | <span style="color:#34d399"><2 mEq/L</span> |
| Central venous O₂ saturation (ScvO₂) | ≥<span style="color:#34d399">70%</span> |

### 先進指標

- **Gastric tonometry** (mucosal pH)。
- **Near-infrared spectroscopy (NIRS)**。
- **Microcirculatory imaging。**
- **Oxymetry parameters。**

### 乳酸清除 (Lactate Clearance)

- Elevated lactate is marker of hypoperfusion。
- **Lactate-guided resuscitation** improves outcomes。
- Target: lactate normalization。

---

## 創傷性休克的特殊考量 (Traumatic Shock)

### 急性創傷性凝血病 (Acute Traumatic Coagulopathy, ATC)

- Present on admission in <span style="color:#f87171">25–30%</span> of severely injured patients。
- **機制：** tissue injury + hypoperfusion → protein C activation → factor Va, VIIIa inactivation。
- Hypocoagulable + hyperfibrinolytic state。
- Associated with <span style="color:#f87171">4× higher mortality</span>。

### 低體溫-酸血症-凝血病三聯徵 (Lethal Triad)

| 組成 | 影響 |
|------|------|
| **低體溫** (<35°C) | ↓ Platelet function, ↓ coagulation enzyme activity |
| **酸血症** (pH <7.2) | ↓ Fibrinogen synthesis, ↓ platelet function |
| **凝血病** | Massive hemorrhage |

**預防：** damage control surgery, permissive hypotension, early TXA。

### ARDS (Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome)

- Formerly "shock lung," "DaNang lung"。
- Part of multiple organ dysfunction syndrome (MODS)。
- **低潮氣量通氣** (<span style="color:#34d399">6 mL/kg</span>) — ARDS Network。

---

## 休克狀態評估流程 (Assessment Algorithm)

```
Shock suspected
    ↓
ABC (Airway, Breathing, Circulation)
    ↓
Identify shock type:
- Hypovolemic? → Control bleeding + volume resuscitation
- Cardiogenic? → ECG, echo, revascularization
- Septic? → Fluids + antibiotics + source control
- Neurogenic? → Fluids + vasopressors
- Obstructive? → Immediate decompression
    ↓
Resuscitation endpoints:
- MAP ≥65, HR <110, UOP ≥0.5 mL/kg/h, lactate normalize
```
