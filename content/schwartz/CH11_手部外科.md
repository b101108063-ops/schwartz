# CH11 手部外科 (Surgery of the Hand and Wrist)

**Source:** Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

---

## Key Points

1. **Hand infections** require prompt treatment; tetanus prophylaxis is essential for all hand wounds
2. **Fractures and dislocations** require evaluation of displacement, rotation, and angulation; MP joints at 90°, IP joints at 0° (intrinsic plus position)
3. **Scaphoid fractures** are prone to nonunion due to precarious blood supply; treat nondisplaced with cast, consider ORIF for displaced
4. **Flexor and extensor tendon injuries** require meticulous repair and early mobilization
5. **Compartment syndrome of the hand** requires urgent fasciotomy
6. **Replantation** should be considered for thumb loss, multiple digit loss, or wrist-level amputation

## 手部解剖學 (Hand Anatomy)

### 骨性結構

手部骨性結構：

| 結構 | 數量/組成 |
|------|----------|
| **腕骨 (Carpal bones)** | 8 塊，分近遠兩排 |
| **近排** | scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum, pisiform |
| **遠排** | trapezium, trapezoid, capitate, hamate |
| **掌骨 (Metacarpals)** | 5 塊 |
| **指骨 (Phalanges)** | 14 塊（近節、中節、遠節） |
| **拇指** | 只有近節 + 遠節 |

### 肌肉與肌腱

| 肌肉/肌腱 | 功能 |
|-----------|------|
| **Flexor digitorum superficialis (FDS)** | PIP flexion（屈曲近端指間關節） |
| **Flexor digitorum profundus (FDP)** | DIP flexion（屈曲遠端指間關節） |
| **Extensor digitorum communis (EDC)** | Finger extension（手指伸展） |
| **Lumbricals** | IP extension, MP flexion |
| **Interossei** | Finger abduction/adduction（手指外展/內收） |

### 神經支配

| 神經 | 節段 | 功能 |
|------|------|------|
| **正中神經 (Median nerve)** | C6-T1 | 拇指對掌、魚際肌、食指/中指感覺 |
| **尺神經 (Ulnar nerve)** | C8-T1 | 內在肌（interossei、lumbricals 3&4）、握力、尺側 1.5 指感覺 |
| **橈神經 (Radial nerve)** | C5-T1 | 腕/手指伸展、手背感覺 |

## 手部感染 (Hand Infections)

### 治療原則

- **Tetanus prophylaxis（破傷風預防）：**
  - Clean wounds with ≥3 prior tetanus toxoid doses → TD booster
  - Other wounds → TD + TIG (tetanus immunoglobulin)
- **Empiric antibiotics：** first-generation cephalosporin (MSSA coverage)；human bites 需覆蓋 Pasteurella
- **早期引流 (early drainage)** for abscesses
- **抬高患肢**

### 常見感染

| 感染類型 | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **Paronychia（甲溝炎）** | 指甲周圍感染；warm soaks, antibiotics, I&D if abscess |
| **Felon（指腹膿腫）** | 指尖膿腫；I&D through lateral incision |
| **Cellulitis（蜂窩組織炎）** | 口服 antibiotics；IV if severe |
| **Deep space infection（深層空間感染）** | 需緊急引流；可能導致功能障礙 |
| **Human bites（人咬傷）** | Eikenella corrodens；amoxicillin-clavulanate |
| **Tendon sheath infection（腱鞘感染）** | 緊急引流；否則肌腱壞死 |

## 手部骨折與脫位 (Fractures and Dislocations)

### 評估要點

評估骨折重點：
- **Deformity：** displacement, rotation, angulation
- **Comminution** and number of fragments
- **Open vs. closed**
- **Associated neurovascular injury**

### 常見骨折

| 骨折類型 | 治療 |
|---|---|
| **Distal phalanx tuft fractures** | 保守治療；保護 distal tuft |
| **Phalangeal fractures (displaced)** | ORIF or closed reduction + splinting |
| **Scaphoid fractures** | 拇指 spica cast (nondisplaced)；ORIF (displaced) — 注意避免骨壞死 |
| **Metacarpal neck fractures ("boxer's fracture")** | 保守治療或 closed reduction；Jahss maneuver |
| **Bennett fracture** | ORIF（不穩定） |
| **Rolando fracture** | ORIF（always） |
| **Distal radius fractures** | Closed reduction + cast；ORIF if unstable |

### Scaphoid 骨折的特殊考量

**血供特點：** 近端血供最差（retrograde blood supply）。可達 20% nonunion 風險。

**治療：**
- Nondisplaced → 拇指 spica cast 6-12 weeks
- Displaced → ORIF（Herbert screw or headless compression screw）
- 延遲診斷 → 可能需 bone graft

### Jahss Maneuver

用於复位 metacarpal neck fractures（boxer's fracture）：

1. 屈曲 MP joint 至 90°
2. 向下按压 proximal phalanx base
3. 向上推 distal fragment

## 肌腱損傷 (Tendon Injuries)

### Flexor Tendon 修復

**原則：**
- **早期被動/主動運動 (early mobilization)** — 減少粘連
- **4-strand repair technique** preferred
- **Pulley preservation** essential

**術後康復：**
- 屈肌腱：place-and-hold exercises, blocking exercises
- 恢復時間：8-12 weeks for full use

### Extensor Tendon 修復

| Zone | 損傷位置 | 治療 |
|---|---|---|
| **I (DIP)** | Mallet finger | 伸直夾板 6-8 weeks |
| **III (PIP)** | Boutonniere deformity | 伸直夾板；可能需手術 |
| **V (MP)** | 中央 slip 損傷 | 手術修復 |

### Laceration at Different Levels

- **No man's land (Zone II)：** FDS and FDP 同時受損。最具挑戰的手術區域。
- **Tendon grafting** vs. **primary repair** depending on injury
- **Free tendon graft** for extensive damage

## 腕部韌帶損傷 (Wrist Ligament Injuries)

### 舟月韌帶損傷 (Scapholunate Ligament Injury)

- **Mechanism：** Fall on outstretched hand (FOOSH)
- **Radiographs：** 失去 Gilula's arcs, Terry Thomas sign（scapholunate gap >3mm）
- **Arthroscopy** gold standard for diagnosis

**治療：**
- Partial → arthroscopic debridement
- Complete → ligament repair or reconstruction

### Mayfield Classification (Progressive Perilunate Dislocation)

| Type | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **I** | Scapholunate disruption |
| **II** | Lunocapitate disruption |
| **III** | Lunotriquetral disruption |
| **IV** | Lunate dislocation into carpal tunnel |

**需评估：** median nerve function（可能需要緊急減壓）

---

## 腔室綜合徵 (Compartment Syndrome)

### 臨床表現（5 P's）

- **Pain：** out of proportion, pain with passive stretch
- **Paresthesias**
- **Paralysis**
- **Pallor**
- **Pulselessness**（晚期）

### 診斷

- **臨床評估**（most important）
- **Compartment pressure：** >30 mmHg 或 Δ pressure <30 mmHg（diastolic BP – compartment pressure）

### 治療

- **緊急筋膜切開術 (Fasciotomy)** — 4 compartments of hand
- **傷口延遲關閉 (delayed closure)** or skin graft

## 再植與移植 (Replantation and Transplantation)

### 適應症

| 情況 | 建議 |
|---|---|
| **Single digit amputation (not thumb)** | 通常不建議 replant（功能結局差） |
| **Thumb amputation** | 建議 replant（功能重要） |
| **Multiple digit amputation** | 考慮 replant |
| **Wrist-level or more proximal** | 考慮 replant（可能有好功能） |
| **Clean cut (guillotine)** | 更好的候選 |

### 禁忌症

- 嚴重 crush injury
- Prolonged warm ischemia (>12h) or cold ischemia (>24h)
- 嚴重其他創傷
- 精神問題

### 再植技術

順序：
1. 先固定骨骼（K-wires or plates）
2. 修復屈/伸肌腱
3. 修復神經（primary neurorrhaphy）
4. 修復血管（首要 — 恢復血流）
5. 修復皮膚（可能需植皮）

## 常見手部疾病

### 腕管綜合徵 (Carpal Tunnel Syndrome)

- **正中神經在腕部受壓**
- **表現：** 拇指/食指/中指麻木、夜間麻木、thenar atrophy
- **診斷：** Tinel's sign, Phalen's test, EMG/NCS
- **治療：**
  - 保守 → wrist splint, NSAIDs
  - 手術 → carpal tunnel release（open or endoscopic）

### 尺神經麻痺 (Ulnar Nerve Palsy)

- **Claw hand deformity：** MP 過伸，IP 屈曲（lumbricals 功能喪失）
- **Grip weakness**
- **治療：** ulnar nerve decompression/transposition

### 扳機指 (Trigger Finger)

- **A1 pulley thickening → FDP tendon 卡住**
- **表現：** clicking, locking, pain
- **治療：** steroid injection, A1 pulley release

### De Quervain's Tenosynovitis

- **First dorsal compartment（abductor pollicis longus, extensor pollicis brevis）炎症**
- **Finkelstein test positive**
- **治療：** thumb spica splint, NSAIDs, steroid injection

## 手部康復原則 (Rehabilitation)

### 夾板原則 (Splinting)

| 情況 | 位置 |
|---|---|
| **Flexor tendon repair** | Intrinsic plus（MP 90°, IP 0°） |
| **Extensor tendon repair** | 伸直位 |
| **Scaphoid fracture** | 拇指 spica |
| **Colles' fracture** | 腕部伸直位 |
| **Protection after surgery** | 根據手術類型 |

### 運動康復

- **Early mobilization**（within limits of repair）
- **Place-and-hold exercises** for flexor tendons
- **肌力和功能訓練 (strengthening)**
- **回歸運動：** 通常 4-6 months
