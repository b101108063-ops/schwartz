# CH03 外科病人的液體與電解質管理 (Fluid and Electrolyte Management of the Surgical Patient)

**來源：** Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

---

## Key Points

1. **Proper management of fluid and electrolytes** facilitates crucial homeostasis that allows cardiovascular perfusion, organ system function, and cellular mechanisms to respond to surgical illness.
2. **Knowledge of the compartmentalization of body fluids** forms the basis for understanding pathologic shifts in these fluid spaces in disease states. Although difficult to quantify, a deficiency in the functional extracellular fluid compartment often requires resuscitation with isotonic fluids in surgical and trauma patients.
3. **Alterations in serum sodium concentration** have profound effects on cellular function due to water shifts between the intracellular and extracellular spaces.
4. **Different rates of compensation** between respiratory and metabolic components of acid-base homeostasis require frequent laboratory reassessment during therapy.
5. Although active investigation continues, **alternative resuscitation fluids** have limited clinical utility, other than the correction of specific electrolyte abnormalities.
6. **Enhanced recovery after surgery (ERAS) protocols** have markedly changed perioperative fluid management and are being used more frequently. ERAS minimizes perioperative fluid administration and focuses on early enteral intake to reduce morbidity associated with IV fluid administration.
7. **Most acute surgical illnesses** are accompanied by some degree of volume loss or redistribution. Consequently, isotonic fluid administration is the most common initial intravenous fluid strategy, while attention is being given to alterations in concentration and composition.
8. Some surgical patients with **neurologic illness, malnutrition, acute renal failure, or cancer** require special attention to well-defined, disease-specific abnormalities in fluid and electrolyte status.

## 水平衡與電解質交換 (Normal Exchange of Fluid and Electrolytes)

### 每日水平衡 (Daily Water Balance)

| 項目 | 平均 (mL) | 最小 | 最大 |
|------|----------|------|------|
| **攝入：** | | | |
| — Oral fluids | 800–1500 | 0 | 1500/h |
| — Solid foods | 500–700 | 0 | — |
| — Water of oxidation | 250 | 125 | 800 |
| **排出：** | | | |
| — Urine | 800–1500 | 300 | 1400/h |
| — Intestinal | 0–250 | 0 | 2500/h |
| — Insensible (lungs/skin) | 600 | 600 | 1500 |

- 正常人每日攝入約 **2000 mL** 水。
- 攝入來源：75% oral intake, 25% from solid foods。
- 每日鹽分攝取：<span style="color:#34d399">3–5 g/day</span>。
- 腎臟排泄最低需求：<span style="color:#34d399">500–800 mL/day</span>。

### 滲透壓 (Osmotic Pressure)

- **Serum osmolality 計算公式：** `2×Na⁺ + (glucose/18) + (BUN/2.8)`
- Normal：<span style="color:#34d399">290–310 mOsm/L</span>
- 鈉離子受限於 ECF compartment。
- 水分可自由通過 cell membranes。

## 體液失衡 (Disturbances in Fluid Balance)

### 體液容積不足 (Extracellular Volume Deficit)

**最常見於外科病人。**

**病因：**

- GI fluids loss (nasogastric suction, vomiting, diarrhea)
- Enterocutaneous fistula
- Sequential soft tissue injuries, burns
- Intra-abdominal processes (peritonitis, obstruction, prolonged surgery)

**理學檢查：**

| 系統 | Volume Deficit | Volume Excess |
|---|---|---|
| General | Weight loss | Weight gain |
| Skin | Decreased skin turgor | — |
| Cardiac | Tachycardia | Increased cardiac output |
| Vascular | Orthostasis/hypotension | Increased CVP |
| Renal | Oliguria, Azotemia | — |
| GI | Ileus | Bowel edema |
| Pulmonary | — | Pulmonary edema |
| Neck veins | Collapsed | Distended |

**實驗室發現：**

- **BUN 升高**（if severe deficit reduces GFR）
- Urine osmolality > serum osmolality
- **Urine Na⁺ <span style="color:#f87171"> < 20 mEq/L</span>**

### 體液容積過量 (Extracellular Volume Excess)

**病因：**

- Iatrogenic (overhydration)
- Renal dysfunction
- Congestive heart failure
- Cirrhosis

## 電解質異常 (Electrolyte Disorders)

### 鈉離子異常 (Sodium Disorders)

#### 低鈉血症 (Hyponatremia)

- 定義：serum Na⁺ < <span style="color:#f87171">135 mEq/L</span>。
- 機制：extracellular water excess relative to sodium。
- ECF volume 可高、正或低。

**病因：**

- **Dilutional hyponatremia**：excess extracellular water (excessive oral water intake, iatrogenic IV free water)
- Postoperative patients 特別容易有 **ADH** 分泌增加
- Thiazide diuretics
- Adrenal insufficiency
- SIADH

**症狀：**（取決於鈉離子下降速度）

- 急性嚴重：**seizures, coma, death**
- 慢性：confusion, nausea, muscle cramps

**治療：**

- 急性 Symptomatic：**3% <span style="color:#fb923c">hypertonic saline</span>**（careful correction: <10–12 mEq/L in 24h to avoid osmotic demyelination）
- Chronic asymptomatic：fluid restriction, demeclocycline, vaptans
- **Hypertonic saline 風險：** central pontine myelinolysis (correction >12 mEq/L/24h)

#### 高鈉血症 (Hypernatremia)

- 定義：serum Na⁺ > <span style="color:#f87171">145 mEq/L</span>。
- 反映游離水分不足。

**病因：**

- **Water loss**（diabetes insipidus, osmotic diarrhea, hyperventilation, fever, burns）
- **Sodium gain**（hypertonic fluids, hyperalimentation, Cushing's syndrome, primary hyperaldosteronism）

**症狀：**（CNS 為主）

- Confusion, seizures, coma
- Severe：**subarachnoid hemorrhage, cerebral edema**

**治療：**

- 計算水分缺乏：`Water deficit = 0.5–0.6 × body weight × (Na⁺/140 – 1)`
- 慢慢糾正：<12 mEq/L/24h
- Free water 或 hypotonic fluids

### 鉀離子異常 (Potassium Disorders)

#### 低鉀血症 (Hypokalemia)

- 定義：serum K⁺ < <span style="color:#f87171">3.5 mEq/L</span>。

**病因：**

- GI losses (vomiting, diarrhea, NG suction)
- Renal losses (diuretics, hyperaldosteronism, Cushing's syndrome)
- Intracellular shift (alkalosis, insulin, β-agonists)
- 攝取不足

**症狀：**

- **Muscle weakness, fatigue, cramping**
- Constipation, ileus
- **Cardiac arrhythmias**（U waves, flattened T waves, prolonged QT）
- **Rhabdomyolysis**（severe）

**治療：**

- Oral KCl（首選）
- IV KCl：**<10–20 mEq/h**（peripheral）、**<40 mEq/h**（central）
- 最大濃度：**40 mEq/L**（peripheral）、**80 mEq/L**（central）
- **Risk of hyperkalemia** with rapid administration

#### 高鉀血症 (Hyperkalemia)

- 定義：serum K⁺ > <span style="color:#f87171">5.0 mEq/L</span>。

**病因：**

- **Acute kidney injury / oliguria**
- Massive tissue destruction (crush injury, rhabdomyolysis, burns, tumor lysis)
- Potassium-sparing diuretics
- ACE inhibitors, ARBs
- Metabolic acidosis
- Pseudohyperkalemia (hemolysis, thrombocytosis)

**心電圖變化：**（依序出現）

1. Peaked T waves
2. Prolonged PR interval
3. Widened QRS
4. Sine wave pattern
5. **Ventricular fibrillation / asystole**

**治療：**

- **Calcium gluconate**（stabilizes cardiac membrane, no effect on K⁺）
- **Insulin + glucose**（shifts K⁺ intracellularly）
- **β-agonists**（salbutamol）
- **Sodium bicarbonate**（if metabolic acidosis）
- **Loop diuretics**（if renal function preserved）
- **Dialysis**（refractory or severe）

### 鈣離子異常 (Calcium Disorders)

#### 低鈣血症 (Hypocalcemia)

- 定義：serum Ca²⁺ < <span style="color:#f87171">8.5 mEq/L</span> or ionized Ca²⁺ < <span style="color:#f87171">4.2 mg/dL</span>。

**病因：**

- **Pancreatitis**（fat saponification）
- **Necrotizing soft tissue infections**
- Chronic kidney disease
- Hypoalbuminemia
- Vitamin D deficiency
- **Hypoparathyroidism**（post-thyroidectomy）
- Massive transfusion (citrate)
- Magnesium deficiency

**症狀：**

- **Perioral numbness, tingling**（fingers, toes）
- **Chvostek sign, Trousseau sign**
- Muscle spasms, tetany
- **Seizures**
- **Prolonged QT**
- Severe：**laryngospasm, cardiac arrest**

**治療：**

- **Acute symptomatic：** IV <span style="color:#fb923c">calcium gluconate</span>（10–20 mL of 10%）
- **Maintenance：** oral calcium + vitamin D
- Correct magnesium deficiency

#### 高鈣血症 (Hypercalcemia)

- 定義：serum Ca²⁺ > <span style="color:#f87171">10.5 mEq/L</span>。

**病因：**

- **Primary hyperparathyroidism**（most common in ambulatory）
- **Malignancy**（bone metastases, PTHrP, ectopic PTH）
- Thiazide diuretics
- Immobilization
- Sarcoidosis, tuberculosis

**症狀：**（"stones, bones, groans, thrones, and psychiatric overtones"）

- **Kidney stones**（nephrolithiasis）
- **Bone pain**
- **Abdominal pain, nausea, pancreatitis**
- **Polyuria, polydipsia**（nephrogenic DI）
- Confusion, depression, coma

**治療：**

- **Hydration**（NS 200–500 mL/h）— most important first step
- **Bisphosphonates**（pamidronate, zoledronic acid）— inhibits osteoclasts
- **Calcitonin** — rapid but transient effect
- **Hemodialysis** — if renal failure
- **Loop diuretics**（ONLY after hydration established）

### 磷離子異常 (Phosphate Disorders)

#### 低磷血症 (Hypophosphatemia)

**病因：**

- Decreased intake
- Intracellular shift (respiratory alkalosis, insulin, refeeding syndrome)
- Increased excretion

**症狀：**

- Muscle weakness
- Cardiac dysfunction
- Rhabdomyolysis
- Hemolysis

**治療：**

- Sodium phosphate 或 potassium phosphate（IV or oral）

#### 高磷血症 (Hyperphosphatemia)

**病因：**

- Renal failure
- Tumor lysis syndrome
- Rhabdomyolysis

**治療：**

- Phosphate binders
- Dialysis

### 鎂離子異常 (Magnesium Disorders)

**鎂的特點：**

- 第四常見礦物質；主要存在細胞內。
- 正常攝取：~<span style="color:#34d399">20 mEq/day</span>。
- 腎臟有很強的鎂保存能力（deficiency 時排泄 <1 mEq/day）。

#### 低鎂血症 (Hypomagnesemia)

**病因：**

- Malabsorption
- Alcoholism
- Diuretics
- Proton pump inhibitors
- Amphotericin B

**症狀：**

- Neuromuscular irritability
- Arrhythmias
- Seizures

**治療：**

- MgCl₂ or MgSO₄（IV or oral）
- 糾正低鉀和低鈣

#### 高鎂血症 (Hypermagnesemia)

**病因：**

- **Renal failure**
- Excessive Mg²⁺ administration（antacids, laxatives）

**症狀：**

- Hyporeflexia
- Respiratory depression
- Cardiac arrest

**治療：**

- **IV calcium gluconate**
- Dialysis
- Withhold Mg²⁺

## 酸鹼平衡 (Acid-Base Balance)

### 緩衝系統 (Buffers)

- **Intracellular：** proteins, phosphates
- **Extracellular：** **bicarbonate–carbonic acid system**（HCO₃⁻/H₂CO₃）

### 補償機制 (Compensation)

- **Respiratory compensation：** 肺臟調節 CO₂ 排出。
- **Renal compensation：** 腎臟調節 HCO₃⁻ reabsorption。

### 單純型酸鹼失衡

| Disorder | Primary Problem | Compensation |
|---|---|---|
| **Metabolic acidosis** | ↓ HCO₃⁻ | ↓ PCO₂ (hyperventilation) |
| **Metabolic alkalosis** | ↑ HCO₃⁻ | ↑ PCO₂ (hypoventilation) |
| **Respiratory acidosis** | ↑ PCO₂ | ↑ HCO₃⁻ (renal retention) |
| **Respiratory alkalosis** | ↓ PCO₂ | ↓ HCO₃⁻ (renal excretion) |

### 代謝性酸中毒 (Metabolic Acidosis)

**病因分類 (Anion Gap)：**

**↑ Anion Gap：**

- **Lactic acidosis**（shock, hypoxia, liver failure）
- **Ketoacidosis**（DKA, starvation, alcohol）
- **Renal failure**（sulfate, phosphate retention）
- **Toxins：** ethylene glycol, methanol, salicylates

**Normal Anion Gap (Hyperchloremic)：**

- **Diarrhea**（bicarbonate loss）
- Renal tubular acidosis
- Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors（acetazolamide）
- Ureterosigmoidostomy

**Anion Gap 計算：** `AG = Na⁺ – (Cl⁻ + HCO₃⁻)`；正常：<span style="color:#34d399">8–12 mEq/L</span>

**治療：**

- Treat underlying cause
- **Sodium bicarbonate：** 當 HCO₃⁻ < <span style="color:#f87171">10–15 mEq/L</span> 且 pH < <span style="color:#f87171">7.2</span> 時考慮
- 注意：rapid correction 可導致 **hypernatremia, volume overload, paradoxical CNS acidosis**

### 代謝性鹼中毒 (Metabolic Alkalosis)

**病因：**

- **Vomiting, NG suction**（H⁺, Cl⁻, K⁺ loss）
- **Diuretics**（post-hydrochlorothiazide）
- **Hyperaldosteronism, Cushing's syndrome**
- Massive transfusion（citrate → metabolic alkalosis）
- Excessive bicarbonate administration

**治療：**

- **糾正病因**（stop NG suction, replace K⁺）
- **Normal saline-responsive：** 0.9% NaCl（chloride-responsive）
- **Normal saline-refractory：** mineralocorticoid excess → spironolactone, amiloride
- **Severe：** dilute HCl, arginine HCl, acetazolamide

## 輸液治療 (Fluid Therapy)

### 膠體 vs. 晶體 (Colloids vs. Crystalloids)

| | Crystalloids | Colloids |
|---|---|---|
| **Examples** | NS, LR, Plasmalyte | Albumin, Hes, Dextran |
| **Distribution** | ECF (1/3 intravascular) | Remain in vasculature |
| **Cost** | Cheap | Expensive |
| **Coagulation** | Minimal effect | Dilutional effect |

- **ERAS protocols：** minimized perioperative fluid administration。
- Goal-directed fluid therapy (GDFT) 減少併發症、住院天數、ICU停留。

### 常用靜脈輸液

| Fluid | Na⁺ | Cl⁻ | K⁺ | Lactate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0.9% NS** | 154 | 154 | 0 | 0 | Hyperchloremic |
| **Lactated Ringer's** | 130 | 109 | 4 | 28 | More physiologic |
| **PlasmaLyte** | 140 | 98 | 5 | 0 | Balanced crystalloid |

### 圍手術期液體管理原則

1. **Isotonic fluids** 為首選 initial resuscitation。
2. 評估 **fluid responsiveness**（PPV, SVV, fluid challenge）。
3. **Goal-directed therapy** in major surgery。
4. **ERAS protocols：** minimize IV fluids, early enteral intake。
5. 避免 **fluid overload**（肺水腫、心衰竭風險）。
6. **Type and phase of illness** 決定 specific electrolyte correction。

### 特殊情況

| 情況 | 考量 |
|------|------|
| **急性腎損傷 (AKI)** | 限制液體、考慮透析 |
| **心衰竭** | 限制鈉水分攝取 |
| **肝衰竭** | 白蛋白補充 |
| **創傷/休克** | 早期目標導向治療 (EGDT) |
