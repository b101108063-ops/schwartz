# CH02 損傷的系統性反應與代謝支持 (Systemic Response to Injury and Metabolic Support)

**來源：** Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition, p.27–82

---

## 損傷的系統性反應

### 先天免疫（Innate Immune Response）

#### Damage-Associated Molecular Patterns (DAMPs)

- 損傷細胞釋放的內源性分子，啟動 **sterile systemic inflammatory response**。
- 常見 DAMPs 包括：

| DAMPs | 說明 |
|-------|------|
| **HMGB1** | 研究最廣，與不良預後相關 |
| **ATP** | 能量分子 |
| **Uric acid** | 代謝產物 |
| **Hematin** | 血基質衍生物 |
| **S100 proteins** | S100A8/A9 (calprotectin)：中性球與單核球表達，創傷後升高 |

#### Pattern Recognition Receptors (PRRs)

##### TLRs (Toll-like receptors)

- 辨識 PAMPs 與 DAMPs。
- **TLR4**：辨識 LPS（Gram-negative）和 DAMPs。
- TLR4 activation → **NF-κB 活化** → 促炎細胞激素釋放。
- 嚴重出血性休克時 TLR4 功能下調（endotoxin tolerance 樣現象）。

##### NLRs (NOD-like receptors)

- 形成 **inflammasome**。
- **NLRP3 inflammasome** 為最大宗。
- Inflammasome 活化 → **caspase-1 → IL-1β、IL-18 成熟**。
- 與創傷後多器官功能障礙（MODS）相關。

##### RAGE (Receptor for Advanced Glycation End-products)

- 與 HMGB1、S100 蛋白結合。
- 與術後併發症、敗血症死亡率相關。

#### 補體系統（Complement System）

- 三大途徑：**classical、lectin、alternative**。
- **C3a、C5a** 為 anaphylatoxins，吸引免疫細胞、釋放組織胺。
- C5a 在嚴重創傷/敗血症中升高，與 MODS 發展相關。
- **C3a、C5a inhibitors** 為新興治療標的。
- Pentraxin 3 (PTX3)：endogenous modulator，結合 C1q、FGF2。

#### 細胞激素與化學因子

| 細胞激素 | 功能 |
|----------|------|
| **TNF-α** | 早期最強烈的促炎因子，誘導發燒、CRP、肝臟急性期反應 |
| **IL-1β** | 發燒、血管內皮活化、吸引中性球 |
| **IL-6** | 急性期蛋白誘導（CRP、fibrinogen）；術後首24小時 IL-6 峰值可預測併發症 |
| **IL-10** | 最重要的抗炎因子；抑制促炎細胞激素；創傷後升高與感染易感性相關 |
| **IL-17** | Th17 細胞產生；召集中性球；與自體免疫和慢性發炎相關 |
| **IFN-γ** | 巨噬細胞活化；增強細胞介導免疫；膿毒症中降低 |
| **MIF** | 促炎；對抗糖皮質激素抗炎作用；與敗血症死亡率相關 |

- **Cytokine storm**：嚴重創傷/敗血症時，促炎與抗炎介質失衡。
- **CARS (Compensatory Anti-inflammatory Response Syndrome)**：抗炎介質（IL-10、TGF-β）主導，增加感染易感性。

#### 嗜中性球（Neutrophils）

- 創傷後最先被招募至損傷組織的免疫細胞。
- **NETosis**：嗜中性球釋放 **neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs)** — DNA 陷阱，捕捉病原體；但也可造成組織損傷。
- 嚴重創傷後嗜中性球功能受損（granulocytic myeloid-derived suppressor cells, MDSCs）。
- 嗜中性球失能與膿毒症易感性相關。

#### 樹突細胞（Dendritic Cells）

- 最重要的**抗原呈現細胞（APCs）**。
- 捕捉、加工、呈現抗原 → 誘導初始 T 細胞反應。
- 調節促炎與抗炎反應。

#### 血小板（Platelets）

- 表達 **TLR4**：參與局部與全身炎症反應。
- 活化後釋放：
  - **HMGB1、IL-1β、CD40 ligand (CD154)**
  - **TGF-β**（免疫抑制因子）：創傷/休克後升高

#### 肥大細胞（Mast Cells）

- 位於組織中，創傷後釋放 **TNF-α** 招募嗜中性球。
- 產生：
  - **Histamine**
  - **Cytokines**
  - **Eicosanoids**
  - **Proteases**
- 作用：血管擴張、毛細血管滲漏、免疫細胞招募。

## 內皮介導的損傷（Endothelium-Mediated Injury）

### 血管內皮

- 正常狀態：**抗凝血**、**維持屏障功能**。
- 創傷後：**procoagulant shift** → 微血栓形成與器官損傷。
- **Traumatic endotheliopathy**：syndecan-1（內皮細胞 glycocalyx 損傷標記）升高，與 ISS、兒茶酚胺水平和死亡率相關。

### 嗜中性球-內皮交互作用

| Phase | 速度 | 調控分子 |
|-------|------|----------|
| **Capture (tethering)** | — | L-selectin |
| **Fast rolling** | 50-150 µm/sec | L-selectin |
| **Slow rolling** | 20-50 µm/sec | P-selectin |
| **Very slow rolling** | 10-20 µm/sec | E-selectin |
| **Arrest** | 0-10 µm/sec | β-integrins (CD18/11a, CD18/11b) |

- **Selectins**：L-selectin（白血球）、P-selectin（血小板/內皮）、E-selectin（內皮）。
- **ICAM-1、VCAM-1**：促進 stable adhesion 與 transmigration。
- **Chemokines**（IL-8/CXCL8 等）：形成化學梯度，引導免疫細胞。

### 一氧化氮（Nitric Oxide, NO）

- 正常：內皮來源的血管擴張劑（EDRF）。
- **Constitutive NOS (eNOS)**：維持血管張力。
- **Inducible NOS (iNOS)**：創傷/敗血症後上調。
  - 過量 NO → 嚴重低血壓、**血管反應性降低**。
  - 調節 T 細胞功能（免疫抑制）。
- Nonselective NOS 抑制劑增加敗血症死亡率；**iNOS 選擇性抑制**可能有效。

### 前列環素（Prostacyclin, PGI₂）

- 強效血管擴張、抑制血小板聚集。
- 全身炎症時內皮表達受損。
- 急性肺損傷時吸入性 PGI₂ 類似物可改善氧合。

### 內皮素（Endothelins, ETs）

- **最強內源性血管收縮劑**（比 angiotensin II 強 10 倍）。
- **ET-1**：創傷、休克、敗血症時升高。
- 低水平時與 NO 共同調節血管張力；高水平導致組織灌注不足。
- 與肺血管收縮、肺高壓相關。

### 血小板活化因子（Platelet-Activating Factor, PAF）

- 活化血小板與嗜中性球。
- 誘導 NETs 形成、IL-1 產生、proinflammatory 基因上調。
- 嚴重敗血症時 PAF-acetylhydrolase（使 PAF 失活）降低。

### 鈉尿肽（Natriuretic Peptides）

- **ANP**（心房）、**BNP**（心室）。
- 功能：**利尿、排鈉、血管擴張、心臟重構**。
- 敗血症患者 elevated proBNP 可預測不良預後。
- 嚴重創傷後持續高 NT-proBNP 與不良結局相關。

## 創傷代謝反應

### 禁食代謝 vs. 創傷後代謝

| 特點 | 禁食（短期） | 創傷/敗血症 |
|------|------------|------------|
| **時程** | < 5 天 | 持續數週 |
| **主要燃料** | 脂肪（60%） | 蛋白質（肌肉）消耗 ↑ |
| **蛋白質消耗** | 75 g/day | **250 g/day**（顯著增加） |
| **特徵** | 糖異生 ↑、生酮 | 高代謝、高血糖、胰島素阻抗 |
| **氮流失** | 7-10 g/day | **30 g/day 以上** |
| **腎上腺素/升糖激素** | 中度升高 | 顯著升高 |

### 飢餓適應

- **前 3 天**：肝糖原快速耗盡。
- **第 1-2 天**：蛋白質大量分解供糖異生。
- **第 2 天起**：酮體開始成為腦部燃料。
- **長期適應**：腦部主要燃料為 ketone bodies，蛋白質分解降至 20 g/day。
- 脂肪是最大能量儲備（140,000 kcal / 70 kg）。

### 創傷後高代謝（Fig. 2-17, 2-18）

- **第一幾天**：非高代謝狀態（"metabolic hibernation"）。
- **第 2 週起**：能量消耗大幅增加。
- 嚴重程度排序：**Major burns > Sepsis/peritonitis > Skeletal trauma > Elective surgery**。
- 交感神經活化與兒茶酚胺釋放介導能量消耗增加。

## 三大營養物質代謝

### 脂肪代謝

- 脂肪是**應激狀態主要燃料（50-80%）**。
- **Lipolysis**：catecholamine → hormone-sensitive lipase → free fatty acids + glycerol。
- 創傷/敗血症**抑制 lipoprotein lipase**（由 TNF 媒介）。
- **MCTs (Medium-chain triglycerides)**：穿過 carnitine shuttle，直接進入粒線體。
- **LCTs (Long-chain triglycerides)**：需要 carnitine shuttle。
- **β-oxidation**：每輪產生 acetyl-CoA → 進入 TCA cycle。
- **Respiratory quotient (RQ)**：脂肪氧化 RQ ≈ 0.7；葡萄糖氧化 RQ ≈ 1.0。
- 創傷後 ketone 生成減少（胰島素升高 + 游離脂肪酸快速氧化）。
- **Omega-3 fatty acids (fish oil)**：減少炎症介質（leukotriene B4 等）。

### 碳水化合物代謝

- 葡萄糖是**腦、嗜中性球、腎髓質的必須燃料**。
- 1g 葡萄糖氧化產生 **4 kcal**（IV dextrose：3.4 kcal/g）。
- **Hepatic gluconeogenesis**：敗血症增加 50-60%、燒傷增加 50-100%。
- **Peripheral glucose intolerance**：despite high insulin（由 TNF-α、IL-1β、cortisol 媒介）。
- **Pyruvate dehydrogenase 活性降低**：丙酮酸無法進入 TCA cycle。
- 過量葡萄糖 → **de novo lipogenesis**（RQ > 1.0）、CO₂ 產生增加。
- 燒傷患者 lactate 清除受損（肝功能受損 + 組織缺氧）。

### 蛋白質與氨基酸代謝

- 健康年輕人蛋白質攝入：**80-120 g/day**。
- 1g 氮 ≈ 6g 蛋白質。
- **創傷後尿氮排泄可達 30 g/day 以上**。
- **Ubiquitin-proteasome system**：急性創傷時肌肉蛋白質降解主要途徑。
- 加速的尿素排泄在**第 7 天達峰值**，可持續 **3-7 週**。
- **Taurine、glutamine**：創傷時消耗增加，可能需要補充。
- **Glutamine**：免疫細胞燃料；嚴重創傷/敗血症時消耗殆盡。
- **Arginine**：NO 前驅物；精索切除術後、傷口癒合時需求增加。

## 外科病人的營養支持

### 營養篩查工具

| 工具 | 描述 |
|------|------|
| **NRS 2002** | 根據體重減輕、BMI、疾病嚴重度評估 |
| **mNUTRIC score** | 包含 age、APACHE II、comorbidities、住院天數；預測 ICU 營養效益 |
| **SGA** | 臨床評估營養狀態 |

### 熱量需求

| 壓力程度 | 熱量需求 |
|----------|----------|
| 正常 | 22-25 kcal/kg/day |
| **輕度 stress** | 25-30 kcal/kg/day |
| **中度 stress** | 30-35 kcal/kg/day |
| **重度 stress（燒傷）** | 35-40+ kcal/kg/day |

- **目標**：indirect calorimetry 測量 actual resting energy expenditure (REE)。

### 腸道營養 vs. 靜脈營養

| 項目 | 腸道營養（EN） | 靜脈營養（PN） |
|------|--------------|--------------|
| **優先性** | 首選 | 當 EN 不可能時使用 |
| **維護腸道屏障** | 是 | 否 |
| **感染風險** | 較低 | 較高（sepsis、CLABSI） |
| **Mortality benefit** | 多個 RCT 顯示 | 不明確 |
| **胃腸道功能** | 需要 functional gut | 不需要 |

- **NUTRIREA-2 trial (2018)**：early EN vs early PN in ventilated shock patients，**死亡率無差異**，EN 組嘔吐/腹瀉更多。
- 免疫營養（魚油、谷氨酰胺、精氨酸）：效益不確定（REDOXS trial 陰性）。

### 特定族群考量

| 族群 | 考量點 |
|------|--------|
| **肝衰竭** | 支鏈氨基酸（BCAA）製劑；避免 aromatic amino acids |
| **腎衰竭** | 限制蛋白質（透析者 1.2-1.5 g/kg/day）；能量 25-30 kcal/kg/day |
| **呼吸衰竭** | 避免過度餵養（高 CO₂ 產出）；蛋白質 1.5 g/kg/day |
| **肥胖** | 餵養不足比過度餵養更常見；protein-sparing 策略 |
| **老年** | 高蛋白質（1.2-1.5 g/kg/day）；維生素 D 補充 |
| **大手術/創傷** | 免疫營養可能有益（魚油、BCAA） |
| **嚴重敗血症** | PN 不優於 EN；早期 EN（24-48h 內）可能減少感染 |

### 特殊營養製劑

| 製劑 | 證據 |
|------|------|
| **Glutamine** | 重症患者可能有益（REDOXS 陰性，但趨勢支持） |
| **Fish oil (omega-3)** | 減少炎症介質；敗血症 RCT 結果不一致 |
| **Arginine** | 免疫調節；膿毒症可能有害（增加死亡率） |
| **BCAAs** | 改善蛋白質合成；肝/腎衰竭時作為燃料 |
| **Vit D** | 老年手術患者術前補充有益 |

## 內分泌壓力反應

### 兒茶酚胺

- **Epinephrine、Norepinephrine** 立即釋放。
- 作用：
  - ↑ 心率
  - ↑ 心輸出量
  - ↑ 血管阻力
  - ↑ 血糖
  - ↑ 游離脂肪酸
- 嚴重創傷：血清兒茶酚胺可達**100 倍正常**。
- 創傷性腦損傷（TBI）：血漿 epinephrine 峰值與死亡率直接相關。

### 皮質醇（Glucocorticoids）

- **CRH → ACTH → cortisol** 路徑。
- 抑制炎症（抑制 NF-κB、↓ 促炎細胞激素）。
- 嚴重創傷/敗血症：皮質醇不足 → **Critical illness-related corticosteroid insufficiency (CIRCI)**。
- **CIRCI 定義**：free cortisol < 500 ng/dL 或 ACTH stimulation test 不反應。
- 劑量：**hydrocortisone 200-300 mg/day**（持續輸注或分次）。

### 升糖激素（Glucagon）

- 促進糖原分解、糖異生。
- 敗血症/創傷時升高。
- 與胰島素阻抗相關。

### 甲狀腺激素

- **Low T3 syndrome**（euthyroid sick syndrome）：常見於重症患者。
- 表現：**rT3 升高、T3 降低、T4 正常或降低**。
- 不需要甲狀腺激素治療。

### 胰島素阻抗

- 組織對胰島素敏感性降低。
- 骨骼肌最重要受影響部位。
- 導致高血糖、肌肉蛋白質分解。
- **Tight glycemic control**（Stryker 等）：ICU 患者維持 80-110 mg/dL 可減少併發症、死亡。
- 但過嚴控制（< 80 mg/dL）增加低血糖風險。

### 生長激素（Growth Hormone）

- 嚴重燒傷後 **ghrelin**（生長激素釋放肽）升高。
- **GH → IGF-1**：促進蛋白質合成、傷口癒合。
- 外源性 GH 在重症患者：**增加死亡率**（SUSPICION trial）。
- 重組 human GH + propranolol：燒傷兒童可減少高代謝與炎症。

### 下丘腦-垂體-腎上腺軸（HPA Axis）

- 嚴重創傷/敗血症：免疫細胞對 cortisol 敏感性 ↑（受體上調）。
- 皮質醇相對不足（CIRCI）→ 需要外源性類固醇。

## 急性期反應（Acute Phase Response）

| 急性期蛋白 | 功能 |
|-----------|------|
| **CRP** | 結合 phosphocholine；啟動補體；臨床上最常用 |
| **Fibrinogen** | 促凝血；血漿中升高 |
| **Serum amyloid A** | 膽固醇代謝；免疫調節 |
| **Haptoglobin** | 結合游離血紅素；抗氧化 |
| **Ceruloplasmin** | 銅攜帶蛋白；抗氧化 |

- **Negative acute phase proteins**：Albumin、Transferrin、Triglycerides（營養不良指標）。
- CRP 在術後 **6-48 小時內升高**，可持續數天。
- **IL-6** 是肝臟急性期蛋白合成主要誘導物。
- **Procalcitonin (PCT)**：細菌感染/敗血症標記；但創傷後也會升高。

## 臟器功能障礙（Organ Dysfunction）

### 術後內分泌反應時間表

| 時間 | 反應 |
|------|------|
| **0-4 小時** | 皮質醇 ↑、醛固酮 ↑、兒茶酚胺 ↑ |
| **4-24 小時** | 胰島素阻抗出現、高血糖、胰高血糖素 ↑ |
| **24-72 小時** | 甲狀腺激素 ↓（低 T3） |
| **3-7 天** | 持續高代謝、尿氮流失高峰 |
| **7 天後** | 取決於併發症與康復進程 |

Changes in both fluid volume and electro -lyte composition occur preoperatively, intraoperatively, and postoperatively, as well as in response to trauma and sepsis. These concepts are then discussed in relationship to management of specific surgical patients and their commonly encountered fluid and electrolyte abnormalities.BODY FLUIDSTotal Body WaterWater constitutes approximately 50% to 60% of total body weight. The relationship between total body weight and total body water (TBW) is relatively constant for an individual and is primarily a reflection of body fat. Lean tissues such as muscle and solid organs have higher water content than fat and bone. As a result, young, lean males have a higher proportion of body weight as water than elderly or obese individuals. In an average young adult male, TBW accounts for 60% of total body weight, whereas in an average young adult female, it is 50%.1 The lower percent-age of TBW in females correlates with a higher percentage of adipose tissue and lower percentage of muscle mass in most. Estimates of percentage of TBW should be adjusted downward approximately 10% to 20% for obese individuals and upward by 10% for malnourished individuals. The highest percentage of TBW is found in newborns, with approximately 80% of their total body weight comprised of water. This decreases to approximately 65% by 1 year of age and thereafter remains fairly constant.Fluid CompartmentsTBW is divided into three functional fluid compartments: plasma, extravascular interstitial fluid, and intracellular fluid (Fig. 3-1). The extracellular fluids (ECF), plasma, and intersti -tial fluid together compose about one-third of the TBW, and the intracellular compartment composes the remaining two thirds. The extracellular water composes 20% of the total body weight and is divided between plasma (5% of body weight) and inter -stitial fluid (15% of body weight). Intracellular water makes up approximately 40% of an individual’s total body weight, with the largest proportion in the skeletal muscle mass.Composition of Fluid CompartmentsThe normal chemical composition of the body fluid compart -ments is shown in Fig. 3-2. The ECF compartment is bal-anced between sodium, the principal cation, and chloride and bicarbonate, the principal anions. The intracellular fluid compartment is composed primarily of the cations potassium and magnesium, the anions phosphate and sulfate, and proteins. The concentration gradient between compartments is maintained by adenosine triphosphate–driven sodium-potassium pumps located within the cell membranes. The composition of the plasma and interstitial fluid differs only slightly in ionic compo-sition. The slightly higher protein content (organic anions) in plasma results in a higher plasma cation composition relative to the interstitial fluid, as explained by the Gibbs-Donnan equilib-rium equation. Proteins add to the osmolality of the plasma and contribute to the balance of forces that determine fluid balance across the capillary endothelium. Although the movement of ions and proteins between the various fluid compartments is restricted, water is freely diffusible. The concentration of electrolytes usually is expressed in terms of the chemical combining activity, or equivalents. An equiva -lent of an ion is its atomic weight expressed in grams divided by the valence:Equivalent = atomic weight (g)/valenceFor univalent ions such as sodium, 1 mEq is same as 1 mmol. For divalent ions such as magnesium, 1 mmol equals 2 mEq. The number of milliequivalents of cations must be bal -anced by the same number of milliequivalents of anions. How-ever, the expression of molar equivalents alone does not allow a physiologic comparison of solutes in a solution.The movement of water across a cell membrane depends primarily on osmosis. To achieve osmotic equilibrium, water moves across a semipermeable membrane to equalize the con -centration on both sides. This movement is determined by the concentration of the solutes on each side of the membrane. Osmotic pressure is measured in units of osmoles (osm) or milliosmoles (mOsm) that refer to the actual number of osmotically active particles. For example, 1 mmol of sodium chloride contributes to 2 mOsm (one from sodium and one from chloride). Daily water losses include 800 to 1200 mL in urine, 250 mL in stool, and 600 mL in insensible losses. Insensible losses of water occur through both the skin (75%) and lungs (25%) and can be increased by such factors as fever, hypermetabolism, and hyperventilation. Sensible water losses such as sweating or pathologic loss of gastrointestinal (GI) fluids vary widely, but these include the loss of electrolytes as well as water (Table 3-1). To clear the products of metabo -lism, the kidneys must excrete a minimum of 500 to 800 mL of urine per day, regardless of the amount of oral intake.The typical individual consumes 3 to 5 g of dietary salt per day, with the balance maintained by the kidneys. With hypo -natremia or hypovolemia, sodium excretion can be reduced to as little as 1 mEq/d or maximized to as much as 5000 mEq/d to achieve balance except in people with salt-wasting kidneys. Sweat is hypotonic, and sweating usually results in only a small sodium loss. GI losses are isotonic to slightly hypotonic and contribute little to net gain or loss of free water when measured and appropriately replaced by isotonic salt solutions.Classification of Body Fluid ChangesDisorders in fluid balance may be classified into three general categories: disturbances in (a) volume, (b) concentration, and (c) composition. Although each of these may occur simultaneously, each is a separate entity with unique mechanisms demanding individual correction. Isotonic gain or loss of salt solution results in extracellular volume changes, with little impact on intracellular fluid volume. If free water is added or lost from the ECF, water will pass between the ECF and intracellular fluid until solute concentration or osmolarity is equalized between the compartments. Magnesium is the fourth most common mineral in the body and, like potassium, is found pri -marily in the intracellular compartments. Approximately one-half of the total body content of 2000 mEq is incorporated in bone and is slowly exchangeable. Of the fraction found in the extracellular space, one-third is bound to serum albumin. There-fore, the plasma level of magnesium may be a poor indicator of total body stores in the presence of hypoalbuminemia. Mag-nesium should be replaced until levels are in the upper limit of normal. The pH of body fluids is maintained within a narrow range despite the ability of the kidneys to gen -erate large amounts of HCO 3− and the normal large acid load produced as a by-product of metabolism. This endogenous acid load is efficiently neutralized by buffer systems and ultimately excreted by the lungs and kidneys.Important buffers include intracellular proteins and phos-phates and the extracellular bicarbonate–carbonic acid system. Compensation for acid-base derangements can be by respiratory mechanisms (for metabolic derangements) or metabolic mecha-nisms (for respiratory derangements). Changes in ventilation in response to metabolic abnormalities are mediated by hydrogen-sensitive chemoreceptors found in the carotid body and brain stem. Acidosis stimulates the chemoreceptors to increase venti-lation, whereas alkalosis decreases the activity of the chemore-ceptors and thus decreases ventilation. The kidneys provide compensation for respiratory abnormalities by either increasing or decreasing bicarbonate reabsorption in response to respira -tory acidosis or alkalosis, respectively. Unlike the prompt change in ventilation that occurs with metabolic abnormalities, the compensatory response in the kidneys to respiratory abnor-malities is delayed. Significant compensation may not begin for 6 hours and then may continue for several days. Because of this delayed compensatory response, respiratory acid-base derange-ments before renal compensation are classified as acute, whereas those persisting after renal compensation are categorized as chronic.
