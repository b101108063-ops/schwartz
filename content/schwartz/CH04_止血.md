# CH04 止血、血液保存與輸血 (Hemostasis, Surgical Bleeding, and Transfusion)

**來源：** Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

---

## Key Points

1. **Hemostasis** is a complex process to limit blood loss from injured vessels, involving vascular constriction, platelet plug formation, fibrin formation, and fibrinolysis.
2. **Platelet function** is essential for primary hemostasis — adhesion to subendothelial collagen via vWF, release reaction, and aggregation.
3. **Coagulation cascade** involves intrinsic, extrinsic, and common pathways — aPTT tests intrinsic pathway, PT tests extrinsic pathway.
4. **Thrombin generation** is the central event in coagulation — cell-based model includes initiation, amplification, and propagation phases.
5. **Fibrinolysis** is balanced by plasmin and antifibrinolytic mechanisms (α2-antiplasmin, TAFI).
6. **Trauma-induced coagulopathy (TIC)** is present on admission in 25–30% of severely injured patients — hypocoagulable and hyperfibrinolytic state.
7. **Massive transfusion** protocols (>10 units pRBC/24h) require balanced replacement of blood components — 1:1:1 ratio (pRBC:FFP:platelets) improves survival.
8. **Tranexamic acid (TXA)** within 3 hours of injury reduces death in bleeding trauma patients (CRASH-2 trial).

## 止血生物學 (Biology of Hemostasis)

### 血管收縮 (Vascular Constriction)

- Initial response to vessel injury.
- More pronounced in vessels with medial smooth muscle.
- Mediated by：**TXA₂, endothelin, serotonin (5-HT)**。
- **TXA₂ (thromboxane A2)**：arachidonic acid → cyclooxygenase → PGH₂ → TXA₂。
- Extent varies with degree of injury.

### 血小板功能 (Platelet Function)

**正常值：** 150,000–400,000/μL；平均壽命 **7–10 days**。

**血小板在止血中的作用：**

1. **Adhesion：** vWF binds to GP I/IX/V receptor on platelet。
2. **Release reaction：** ADP, serotonin, TXA₂, α-granule proteins。
3. **Aggregation：** reversible (primary) → irreversible (secondary)。

**Arachidonic acid pathway：**

- Arachidonic acid → COX → PGG₂ → PGH₂ → **TXA₂** (platelet) or **PGI₂** (endothelium)。
- TXA₂：vasoconstriction + platelet aggregation。
- PGI₂：vasodilation + inhibits platelet aggregation。

**抗血小板藥物：**

| 藥物 | 機制 |
|------|------|
| **Aspirin** | Irreversible COX inhibitor → ↓ TXA₂ |
| **NSAIDs** | Reversible COX inhibitor |
| **Clopidogrel, prasugrel, ticagrelor** | P2Y₁₂ ADP receptor antagonists |

### 凝血路徑 (Coagulation Cascade)

**傳統二元模型：Intrinsic + Extrinsic → Common pathway**

| Pathway | Initiator | Lab Test |
|---|---|---|
| **Intrinsic** | Factor XII → XI → IX → VIII | aPTT |
| **Extrinsic** | Tissue factor + Factor VII | PT/INR |
| **Common** | Factor X → V → II (thrombin) → I (fibrinogen) → fibrin | Both |

- **Vitamin K-dependent factors：** II, VII, IX, X (and protein C, S)。

**Cell-based model of hemostasis：**

1. **Initiation：** TF exposure → TF-VIIa complex → Xa + IXa → small thrombin。
2. **Amplification：** thrombin activates platelets + factors V, VIII。
3. **Propagation：** tenase (VIIIa-IXa) + prothrombinase (Va-Xa) on platelet surface → "thrombin burst"。

**Thrombin 的作用：**

- Converts fibrinogen → fibrin + fibrinopeptides A and B。
- Fibrinopeptide A → end-to-end polymerization。
- Fibrinopeptide B → side-to-side polymerization。
- Factor XIII → cross-linked fibrin clot。

### 纖維蛋白溶解 (Fibrinolysis)

**路徑：** plasminogen → plasmin (via tPA, uPA) → fibrin degradation products (FDPs, D-dimer)。

**抗纖維蛋白溶解：**

- **α2-antiplasmin**
- **TAFI (thrombin-activatable fibrinolysis inhibitor)**
- **PAI-1 (plasminogen activator inhibitor-1)**

**臨床意義：**

- **Hyperfibrinolysis：** trauma, surgery, AML → bleeding。
- **Hypofibrinolysis：** DVT, PE。

**抑制 plasmin 的藥物：**

| 藥物 | 機制 |
|------|------|
| **Tranexamic acid (TXA)** | Lysine analog, inhibits plasminogen binding |
| **ε-Aminocaproic acid (EACA)** | Inhibits plasminogen activation |

## 先天性凝血因子缺乏 (Congenital Factor Deficiencies)

### 血友病 (Hemophilia)

| Type | Deficiency | Inheritance | Lab |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hemophilia A** | Factor VIII | X-linked recessive | aPTT ↑, FVIII:C ↓ |
| **Hemophilia B** (Christmas disease) | Factor IX | X-linked recessive | aPTT ↑, FIX:C ↓ |
| **Hemophilia C** | Factor XI | Autosomal recessive | aPTT ↑ |

**治療：** Factor VIII or IX replacement (recombinant or plasma-derived)；**FEIBA**（factor VIII inhibitor bypassing activity）for inhibitors。

### von Willebrand Disease (vWD)

- **vWF deficiency** → platelet adhesion defect。
- **實驗室：** bleeding time ↑, aPTT ↑ (vWF carries factor VIII), vWF antigen ↓, ristocetin aggregation ↓。
- **類型：** Type 1 (partial quantitative), Type 2 (qualitative), Type 3 (total)。
- **治療：** DDAVP (Type 1), vWF concentrates (severe), cryoprecipitate。

### 其他缺乏症

| 疾病 | 特色 |
|------|------|
| **Factor VII deficiency** | Rare, autosomal recessive, bleeding symptoms |
| **Factor XIII deficiency** | Delayed wound healing, umbilical stump bleeding |

## 血小板功能障礙 (Platelet Functional Defects)

### 先天性

| 疾病 | 缺陷 | 結果 |
|------|------|------|
| **Bernard-Soulier syndrome** | GP Ib-IX-V defect | ↓ platelet adhesion |
| **Glanzmann thrombasthenia** | GP IIb-IIIa defect | ↓ platelet aggregation |
| **Storage pool disorders** | δ-granule deficiency | — |

### 後天性

- **Uremia：** guanidinosuccinic acid accumulation。
- **Medications：** antiplatelet agents (aspirin, clopidogrel, GP IIb/IIIa inhibitors)。
- **Liver disease。**
- **Myeloproliferative disorders。**

## 後天性止血障礙 (Acquired Hemostatic Defects)

### 血小板減少 (Platelet Abnormalities)

**Thrombocytopenia：** platelet count < 150,000/μL。

| 病因 | 機制 |
|---|---|
| **稀釋性** (massive transfusion) | Dilution |
| **免疫性** (ITP, HIT, TTP) | Destruction/consumption |
| **骨髓抑制** | Decreased production |
| **脾功能亢進** | Sequestration |

#### HIT (Heparin-Induced Thrombocytopenia)

| Type | 特色 |
|------|------|
| **Type I (non-immune)** | Mild, transient, no thrombosis |
| **Type II (immune-mediated)** | 抗體 against PF4-heparin complex → **thrombosis** (not bleeding!) |

- **4T score** for diagnosis。
- **治療：** stop all heparin, start argatroban or bivalirudin。

#### TTP (Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura)

- ADAMTS13 deficiency → ultra-large vWF multimers → platelet microthrombi。
- **Pentad：** thrombocytopenia, microangiopathic hemolytic anemia, neurological symptoms, renal dysfunction, fever。
- **Plasma exchange**（removes antibodies + provides ADAMTS13）。

### 纖維蛋白原減少 (Acquired Hypofibrinogenemia)

- **DIC (Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation)**。
- **嚴重創傷/大量輸血。**
- **治療：** fibrinogen concentrate (4–6 g) or cryoprecipitate (each bag ~2 g fibrinogen)。

---

## 肝臟疾病的凝血病 (Coagulopathy of Liver Disease)

- **Decreased synthesis：** factors II, VII, IX, X, XI, XIII, protein C, S, antithrombin。
- **Decreased clearance：** fibrinolytic proteins。
- **Thrombocytopenia：** splenic sequestration, decreased thrombopoietin。
- **Hyperfibrinolysis。**
- **PT/INR not reliable** for bleeding risk assessment (does not reflect rebalanced hemostasis)。
- **血栓風險**反而增加（protein C, S ↓, factors VIII, vWF ↑）。

---

## 創傷性凝血病 (Coagulopathy of Trauma / TIC)

**急性創傷性凝血病 (Acute Traumatic Coagulopathy, ATC)：**

- 25–30% of severely injured patients have on admission。
- **機制：** tissue injury + hypoperfusion → endothelial release of thrombomodulin → protein C activation → factors Va, VIIIa inactivation。
- **表型：** hypocoagulable + hyperfibrinolytic。
- **Early coagulopathy associated with 4× higher mortality。**

**創傷性凝血病的預防/治療：**

- **Damage control surgery。**
- **Restrictive transfusion**（低血小板、低溫、低酸血症）。
- **早期使用 TXA：** CRASH-2 (<3h)。
- **目標導向輸液治療。**

## 抗凝治療與出血 (Anticoagulation and Bleeding)

### 維生素K拮抗劑 (Warfarin)

- Inhibits vitamin K epoxide reductase → ↓ factors II, VII, IX, X。
- **Reversal：**

| 方法 | 說明 |
|------|------|
| **Immediate** | 4-factor PCC (prothrombin complex concentrate) — preferred |
| **Vitamin K** | 5–10 mg IV (slow push) |
| **FFP** | if PCC unavailable |

### Direct Oral Anticoagulants (DOACs)

| Drug | Mechanism | Half-life | Reversal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dabigatran** | Direct thrombin inhibitor | 12–17h | **Idarucizumab** (Praxbind) |
| **Rivaroxaban, Apixaban** | Factor Xa inhibitors | 5–9h | **Andexanet alfa** |

### Heparin

- **UFH：** potentiates antithrombin III → inhibits IIa, Xa。
- **LMWH：** inhibits Xa > IIa。
- **Reversal：** protamine sulfate (1 mg per 100 units UFH)。

## 輸血治療 (Transfusion)

### 歷史背景

| 年份 | 事件 |
|------|------|
| **1918** | kernicterus first described |
| **1939** | first successful RBC transfusion with crossmatch |
| **1970s** | component therapy |

### 血液製品

| Product | Indication | Dose |
|---|---|---|
| **pRBC** (packed RBC) | Anemia, blood loss | 1 unit ↑ Hgb ~1 g/dL |
| **FFP** (Fresh Frozen Plasma) | Coagulation factor deficiency | 10–15 mL/kg |
| **Platelets** | Thrombocytopenia | 1 apheresis unit ↑ plt 30–50K |
| **Cryoprecipitate** | Hypofibrinogenemia, Hemophilia A, vWD | 1 unit/10 kg |
| **Tranexamic acid** | Hyperfibrinolysis, trauma | 1 g bolus + 1 g infusion |

### 大量輸血協議 (Massive Transfusion Protocol)

**定義：** >10 units pRBC in 24h or >4 units in 1h。

**目標比率 (1:1:1)：**

- 1 pRBC : 1 FFP : 1 platelet (equivalent to 6-pack platelets)。
- **Higher FFP ratio improves survival** in trauma。

### 輸血併發症

| 併發症 | 發生率 | 預防/治療 |
|---|---|---|
| **Febrile non-hemolytic reaction** | 1–3% | Acetaminophen, leukoreduction |
| **Allergic reaction** | 1–3% | Antihistamines, anaphylaxis: epinephrine |
| **Acute hemolytic reaction** | 1:12,000–1:38,000 | Stop transfusion, IV fluids, monitor |
| **TRALI** (輸血相關急性肺損傷) | 1:5,000 | 停止輸血, 支持治療 |
| **TACO** (循環超負荷) | — | 利尿劑 |
| **Bacterial contamination** | 1:5,000–1:100,000 | 冰箱保存血小板 |
| **Virus transmission** | Rare | Nucleic acid testing (NAT) |

### 血液保存 (Blood Conservation)

- **Cell salvage：** intraoperative blood 回收。
- **Acute normovolemic hemodilution：** withdraw blood, replace with crystalloid。
- **Erythropoietin。**
- **Desmopressin (DDAVP)：** releases vWF — for vWD, platelet dysfunction。
- **Topical hemostatic agents。**
- **Restrictive transfusion threshold：** Hgb < 7–8 g/dL (most patients)；< 8 g/dL (cardiovascular disease)。

### 凝血功能檢測

| Test | Pathway | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **PT/INR** | Extrinsic + common | Normal ~11–13 sec |
| **aPTT** | Intrinsic + common | Normal ~25–35 sec |
| **Thromboelastography (TEG/ROTEM)** | Whole blood clot dynamics | Guides transfusion in trauma |
| **Platelet count** | — | <50,000 需輸血小板 |
| **Fibrinogen level** | — | <100–150 mg/dL 需補充 |
| **D-dimer** | — | Fibrinolysis marker |

## 膠體與晶體輸液 (Volume Replacement)

### 晶體 (Crystalloids)

| Fluid | Na⁺ | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Normal saline (0.9% NS)** | 154 | Hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis |
| **Lactated Ringer's** | 130 | More physiologic |
| **PlasmaLyte** | 140 | Balanced crystalloid |

### 膠體 (Colloids)

- **Albumin：** 5% (isooncotic), 25% (hyperoncotic)。
- **Hydroxyethyl starch (HES)：** risk of AKI, coagulopathy。
- **Gelatin。**

### 新概念：Whole Blood Resuscitation

- **Low-titer O whole blood (LTOWB)**。
- **優勢：** simpler, less citrate, oxygen-carrying capacity + clotting factors。
- **Civilian trauma：** showing promise in massive transfusion。
