# CH07 創傷 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

**來源：** Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

---

## Key Points

1. **Trauma is the #1 cause of death for ages 1–44**, #3 cause of death overall。
2. **Primary survey (ABCs)：** Airway with C-spine protection, Breathing, Circulation, Disability, Exposure。
3. **所有 blunt trauma 假設有 C-spine injury** until proven otherwise。
4. **Hemodynamically unstable patients** (non-responders or transient responders) need prompt intervention。
5. **Trauma-induced coagulopathy (TIC)** 需要早期識別 — massive transfusion protocol。
6. **Penetrating cervical injury：** indications for immediate surgery = hemodynamic instability + significant external arterial hemorrhage。
7. **Blunt aortic injury gold standard：** CT angiography (CTA)。
8. **Abdomen is a diagnostic "black box"** — FAST, CT scan for evaluation。
9. **Damage control surgery：** 控制出血 + 減少 GI spillage；lethal triad (coagulopathy, hypothermia, acidosis) 是主要適應症。
10. **Blunt carotid/vertebral artery injury：** systemic antithrombotic therapy。
11. **Abdominal compartment syndrome：** primary (injury-related) or secondary (reperfusion)。

---

## 創傷流行病學 (Epidemiology)

- **#1 cause of death ages 1–44**。
- **#3 cause of death overall**。
- Leading causes：**motor vehicle collisions, falls, <span style="color:#60a5fa">penetrating</span> injuries, burns**。
- **"<span style="color:#e2e8f0">Golden hour</span>"** — early intervention within 1 hour improves <span style="color:#34d399">survival</span>。

---

## 初級評估 (Primary Survey — <span style="color:#e2e8f0">ABCDE</span>)

### A. 氣道 (Airway) with <span style="color:#60a5fa">C-spine</span> Protection

**所有創傷病人在假設有 <span style="color:#60a5fa">C-spine</span> injury 下處理。**

**氣道評估：**

- 清醒、正常發音、無呼吸窘迫 → 不太可能需要氣道干預。
- 異常發音、呼吸音異常、心搏過速、意識改變 → 需要進一步評估。

**立即氣道干預的適應症：**

- Apnea。
- 無法保護氣道 (意識障礙)。
- 即將發生的氣道阻塞 (吸入性損傷、血腫、軟組織腫脹)。
- 無法維持氧合。

**氣道技術：**

| 技術 | 適應症 |
|------|--------|
| **Nasotracheal** | 清醒、自發呼吸 |
| **Orotracheal** | 首選技術 (需要神經肌肉阻斷) |
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Cricothyroidotomy</span>** | 無法插管時；<11歲相對禁忌 |
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Tracheostomy</span>** | 氣管斷裂、甲狀軟骨骨折；緊急情況 |

### B. 呼吸 (Breathing)

**立即危及生命的呼吸問題：**

| 情況 | 治療 |
|------|------|
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Tension pneumothorax</span>** | 立即 needle decompression → <span style="color:#60a5fa">chest tube</span> |
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Open pneumothorax</span>** | 密封敷料 → <span style="color:#60a5fa">chest tube</span> |
| **Flail chest + pulmonary <span style="color:#c084fc">contusion</span>** | 插管 + 機械通氣 |
| **Massive hemothorax** | <span style="color:#60a5fa">Chest tube</span> (output ><span style="color:#f87171">1500 mL</span> initial or ><span style="color:#f87171">200 mL/h</span>) |
| **Tracheobronchial injury** | 插管 + 手術修復 |

### C. 循環 (Circulation)

**休克評估 (各階段)：**

| Stage | Blood Loss | <span style="color:#e2e8f0">HR</span> | BP | Pulse Pressure | Mental Status |
|-------|-----------|-----|-----|---------------|---------------|
| **I** | <span style="color:#f87171"><15%</span> | <span style="color:#34d399">Normal</span> | <span style="color:#34d399">Normal</span> | <span style="color:#34d399">Normal</span> | Slightly anxious |
| **II** | <span style="color:#f87171">15–30%</span> | <span style="color:#f87171">>100</span> | <span style="color:#34d399">Normal</span>/↓ | Narrow | Mildly anxious |
| **III** | <span style="color:#f87171">30–40%</span> | <span style="color:#f87171">>120</span> | ↓↓ Systolic | Narrow | Confused |
| **IV** | <span style="color:#f87171">>40%</span> | <span style="color:#f87171">>140</span> | ↓↓↓ | Very narrow | Lethargic |

**立即危及生命的循環問題：**

| 情況 | 治療 |
|------|------|
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Hemorrhagic shock</span>** | 控制出血 + 容量復甦 |
| **Massive hemothorax** | <span style="color:#60a5fa">Chest tube</span>；可能需要開胸 |
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Cardiac tamponade</span>** | <span style="color:#60a5fa">Pericardiocentesis</span> or 開胸心包膜切開 |
| **Mechanically unstable <span style="color:#22d3ee">pelvis</span> fracture** | <span style="color:#60a5fa">Pelvic binder</span>, <span style="color:#60a5fa">angiography</span> |
| **Cardiogenic shock** | 強心劑, 機械循環支持 |
| **Neurogenic shock** | 液體 + 血管加壓劑 |

**目標：**

- <span style="color:#60a5fa">MAP</span> ≥65 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span>。
- <span style="color:#60a5fa">UOP</span> ≥0.5 mL/kg/h。
- <span style="color:#60a5fa">Lactate</span> normalize。

### D. 殘疾 (Disability)

- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">GCS</span> (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Glasgow Coma Scale</span>)**。
- **Pupil assessment**。
- **Motor/sensory exam**。
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">C-spine</span> assessment**。

### E. 暴露/環境控制 (Exposure/Environment)

- 完全脫衣檢查。
- 避免低溫 (維持體溫 >36°C)。

---

## 次級評估 (Secondary Survey)

- 完整病史 (過敏、藥物、過去病史、最後一餐、創傷相關事件)。
- 從頭到腳的理學檢查。
- 生命徵象持續監測。
- 診斷檢驗 (實驗室、影像)。

## 頭部創傷 (Head Injury)

### 顱內壓升高 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">ICP</span> Elevation)

**表現：** headache, nausea/vomiting, papilledema, altered consciousness, Cushing's triad (HTN, bradycardia, irregular respiration)。

**治療：**

- **Head elevation** (30°)。
- **<span style="color:#fb923c">Mannitol</span>** (0.25–1 g/kg) or **<span style="color:#fb923c">hypertonic saline</span>**。
- **Sedation**。
- **維持 <span style="color:#60a5fa">MAP</span>** 目標腦灌流壓 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">CPP</span> = <span style="color:#60a5fa">MAP</span> – <span style="color:#60a5fa">ICP</span>) ≥60–70 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span>。
- **氣道保護** (<span style="color:#60a5fa">GCS</span> ≤8 需要插管)。
- **手術減壓** (hemicraniectomy for refractory <span style="color:#60a5fa">ICP</span>)。

### 創傷性腦損傷 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">TBI</span>)

| <span style="color:#60a5fa">GCS</span> | Severity |
|-----|---------|
| 13–15 | Mild |
| 9–12 | Moderate |
| 3–8 | Severe |

## 頸部創傷 (Neck <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

### Zone 分類

| Zone | 解剖位置 | 管理策略 |
|------|----------|----------|
| **I** | Clavicles to cricoid | <span style="color:#60a5fa">CTA</span> first；often require OR |
| **II** | Cricoid to angle of mandible | <span style="color:#60a5fa">CTA</span>；OR if unstable or hard signs |
| **III** | Angle of mandible to skull base | <span style="color:#60a5fa">CTA</span>；vascular intervention |

### Hard Signs (需要立即手術)

- Hemodynamic instability。
- Significant external arterial <span style="color:#c084fc">hemorrhage</span>。
- Expanding/pulsatile <span style="color:#c084fc">hematoma</span>。
- Airway obstruction。
- Massive hemoptysis/hematemesis。

---

## 胸部創傷 (Thoracic <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

### 立即危及生命的情況

| 情況 | 診斷 | 治療 |
|------|------|------|
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Tension pneumothorax</span>** | Clinical (hypotension, absent breath sounds, JVD) | Needle decompression → <span style="color:#60a5fa">chest tube</span> |
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Open pneumothorax</span>** | Visible wound | 密封敷料 → <span style="color:#60a5fa">chest tube</span> |
| **Massive hemothorax** | <span style="color:#e2e8f0">CXR</span>, hypotension | <span style="color:#60a5fa">Chest tube</span> (output)；可能需要開胸 |
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Cardiac tamponade</span>** | Beck's triad, hypotension | <span style="color:#60a5fa">Pericardiocentesis</span>；可能需要開胸 |
| **Flail chest** | 連枷樣運動 | 插管 + PEEP |
| **Tracheobronchial injury** | <span style="color:#e2e8f0">CXR</span> (<span style="color:#22d3ee">subcutaneous</span> emphysema, pneumothorax) | 插管 + 手術修復 |

### 胸腔鏡輔助手術 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">VATS</span>)

- 肺損傷。
- <span style="color:#22d3ee">Diaphragm</span> injury。
- Pericardial window。
- Decortication for empyema。

---

## 腹部創傷 (Abdominal <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

### 評估工具

| 工具 | 優點 | 缺點 |
|------|------|------|
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa">FAST</span>** | 快速, bedside, 敏感性 85-90% for hemoperitoneum | 操作者依賴 |
| **<span style="color:#e2e8f0">CT</span> scan** | 精確, 評估實質器官和空腔器官損傷 | 需要穩定病人 |
| **<span style="color:#e2e8f0">DPL</span>** | 敏感性高 | 侵入性, 已少用 |
| **Physical exam** | 準確性有限 (醉酒, 脊髓損傷) | — |

### 適應立即開腹探查 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Laparotomy</span>)

- Hemodynamic instability with positive <span style="color:#60a5fa">FAST</span>。
- Peritonitis。
- Evisceration。
- GI bleeding (hematochezia, melena)。
- Free air on X-ray。

### 肝臟創傷 (Hepatic Injuries)

**社區獲得性肝臟創傷：** 大多數可用非手術治療 (NOT)。

**手術治療：**

1. **Pringle maneuver** (hepatic pedicle clamping)。
2. Direct pressure, packing。
3. Hepatotomy with individual ligation。
4. **Hepatic artery ligation**。
5. **Angioembolization**。
6. **Hepatic resection** (rare)。
7. **肝移植** (extremely rare, devastating injuries)。

**併發症：** biloma, arterial pseudoaneurysm, biliary <span style="color:#60a5fa">fistula</span>, hemobilia。

### 脾臟創傷 (Splenic Injuries)

**非手術治療 (NOT)：** 首選（大部分）。

**適應症：** hemodynamically <span style="color:#34d399">stable</span>, no other indication for <span style="color:#60a5fa">laparotomy</span>。

**失敗風險因素：** contrast blush, <span style="color:#c084fc">high-grade</span> injury, older age。

**SAE (Selective <span style="color:#60a5fa">Angioembolization</span>)：** for <span style="color:#c084fc">high-grade</span> injuries with contrast blush。

**適應早期開腹：** needing <span style="color:#f87171">>12h</span> transfusion in first 12h, hemodynamic instability。

**脾切除後風險：** overwhelming postsplenectomy sepsis (encapsulated bacteria)。

**疫苗：** pneumococcal, Hib, meningococcal (術後 >14天)。

### 胰臟創傷 (Pancreatic Injuries)

- Often associated with duodenal injuries。
- **主胰管損傷：** 需要手術修復 (distal → distal pancreatectomy；proximal → complex repair)。
- **併發症：** pancreatic <span style="color:#60a5fa">fistula</span>, pseudocyst, abscess。

### 胃腸道創傷 (GI <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

**小腸：** 

- 修補 (transverse 1-layer closure) if <1/3 circumference。
- 切除 + 吻合 if destructive injuries。

**胃：** 

- 修補或切除 (Billroth procedures)。

**結腸/直腸：** 

- Colostomy for destructive injuries, heavy contamination。
- Primary repair for simple injuries in <span style="color:#34d399">stable</span> patients。

## 骨盆創傷 (Pelvic Fractures)

### 穩定性分類

| 類型 | 穩定性 |
|------|--------|
| **APC (Anterior-Posterior Compression)** | 可不穩定 (I-III) |
| **LC (Lateral Compression)** | 通常穩定 |
| **VS (Vertical Shear)** | 不穩定 |
| **CM (Combined Mechanism)** | 不穩定 |

### 出血控制

1. **Pelvic binder** (immediate)。
2. **Angiography + embolization** (主要出血控制)。
3. **External fixation**。
4. **Pelvic packing** (術中)。
5. **REBOA** (Resuscitative Endovascular Balloon Occlusion of the Aorta)。

---

## 筋膜室綜合徵 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Compartment Syndrome</span>)

### 診斷

**5 P's：** Pain (out of proportion), Paresthesias, Paralysis, Pallor, Pulselessness (late)。

**客觀標準：** 

- **Intra-compartmental pressure >30 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span>**。
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Δ pressure</span> (舒張壓 – 室內壓) <30 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span>**。

### 治療

- **緊急筋膜切開術 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Fasciotomy</span>)**。
- 四個筋膜室都需要減壓 (小腿: 前, 側, 後淺, 後深)。

---

## 創傷性凝血病 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>-Induced <span style="color:#60a5fa">Coagulopathy</span>, <span style="color:#60a5fa">TIC</span>)

- 25-30% 的嚴重創傷病人在入院時已有。
- **機制：** tissue injury + hypoperfusion → protein C activation → factors Va, VIIIa inactivation。
- **表現：** hypocoagulable + hyperfibrinolytic。
- **創傷致死三聯徵：** 低溫 + 酸血症 + 凝血病。

### 大量輸血協議 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Massive Transfusion</span> Protocol)

**定義：** >10 units <span style="color:#fb923c"><span style="color:#fb923c">pRBC</span></span>/24h or >4 units in 1h。

**目標比率：** 1:1:1 (<span style="color:#fb923c"><span style="color:#fb923c">pRBC</span></span>:<span style="color:#fb923c">FFP</span>:platelets)。

| 藥物 | 劑量 |
|------|------|
| **<span style="color:#60a5fa"><span style="color:#fb923c">TXA</span></span>** | 1 g bolus + 1 g infusion (within 3h) |
| **<span style="color:#fb923c">Fibrinogen</span>** | 4-6 g (目標 <span style="color:#fb923c">fibrinogen</span> >150-200 mg/dL) |

---

## <span style="color:#60a5fa">Damage Control</span> Surgery (<span style="color:#60a5fa">DCS</span>)

### 適應症

- **<span style="color:#f87171">Lethal</span> triad** (hypothermia, acidosis, <span style="color:#60a5fa">coagulopathy</span>)。
- **Hemodynamic instability**。
- **High-energy transferred patients**。
- **Complex injuries** requiring long surgical time。

### 三階段方法

1. **Stage 1：** 控制出血 + 控制污染；暫時性關腹。
2. **ICU：** 糾正低體溫、凝血病、酸血症。
3. **Stage 2：** 計劃性再探查 + 確定性修復。

---

## 脊柱創傷 (Spinal <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

### 脊髓損傷分類

| 類型 | 表現 |
|------|------|
| **Complete** | 無感覺或運動功能 below injury level |
| **Incomplete** | Some preservation of function |

### 常見脊髓綜合徵

| 綜合徵 | 表現 |
|--------|------|
| **Anterior cord syndrome** | 運動 + 痛溫覺喪失；本體感覺保留 |
| **Posterior cord syndrome** | 本體感覺喪失；運動和痛溫覺保留 |
| **Brown-Séquard syndrome** | 同側運動 + 對側痛溫覺 |
| **Central cord syndrome** | 上肢 >下肢；感覺程度不等 |

---

## 軟組織創傷 (Soft Tissue <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

### 傷口護理

- **Irrigation** (大量生理鹽水)。
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Debridement</span>** (失活組織)。
- **Antibiotics** (根據傷口類型)。
- **<span style="color:#fb923c">Tetanus prophylaxis</span>**。
- **延遲一期關閉** or 二期癒合。

### 咬傷 (Bites)

| 類型 | 抗生素 |
|------|--------|
| **Dog/Cat** | <span style="color:#fb923c">Amoxicillin-clavulanate</span> |
| **Human** | <span style="color:#fb923c">Amoxicillin-clavulanate</span> (MRSA risk → TMP-SMX + <span style="color:#fb923c">clindamycin</span>) |
| **Snake** | <span style="color:#fb923c">Antivenin</span>；避免傷口包紮 |

## 燒傷 (Burns)

### 面積計算 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Rule of Nines</span>)

| 部位 | 成人 | 小孩 |
|------|------|------|
| Head/neck | 9% | 18% |
| Each upper extremity | 9% | 9% |
| Each lower extremity | 18% | 14% |
| Anterior trunk | 18% | 18% |
| Posterior trunk | 18% | 18% |
| Perineum | 1% | 1% |

### 深度分類

| 深度 | 層次 | 表現 |
|------|------|------|
| **Superficial (1st)** | <span style="color:#22d3ee">Epidermis</span> | 紅, 疼痛, 無水泡 |
| **Partial thickness (2nd)** | <span style="color:#22d3ee">Dermis</span> | 水泡, 潮濕, 疼痛 |
| **Deep partial (2nd)** | Deep <span style="color:#22d3ee">dermis</span> | 水泡, 白色, 輕微疼痛 |
| **Full thickness (3rd)** | 全層皮膚 | 乾燥, 皮革樣, 無疼痛 |
| **4th degree** | 皮下組織, 肌肉, 骨頭 | 焦炭, 乾硬 |

### 燒傷復甦 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Parkland Formula</span>)

**初始輸液需求：** 4 mL × 體重(kg) × %TBSA燒傷。

- 第一個 24 小時：**一半在受傷後 8 小時內給予**；另一半在接下來 16 小時。
- 膠體通常在燒傷後 24 小時才給予。

### 吸入性損傷 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Inhalation Injury</span>)

**跡象：** 封閉空間火災, 面部燒傷, 鼻毛燒焦, 咳痰碳屑, 聲音改變, 喘鳴。

**治療：** early intubation (不要等呼吸衰竭!)。

---

## 婦產科創傷 (Obstetric <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

- **胎兒評估：** 先檢查母親穩定性。
- **胎兒宮內復甦：** 母親左側臥位, O₂, fluids。
- **創傷時早產風險增加。**
- **胎盤剝離** (abruption) — 最常見的胎兒死亡原因。
- **C-section** only for life-threatening fetal indications。

With direct thermal injury to the upper airway or smoke inhalation, rapid and severe airway edema is potentially lethal. Anticipating the need for intubation and establishing an early airway are critical. Signs of impending respiratory compromise include a hoarse voice, wheezing, or stridor; subjective dyspnea is a particularly concerning symptom and should trigger prompt elective endotracheal intubation. Though not always avoidable, main-taining vigilance for typical complications and using appro -priate techniques for prevention may limit the frequency and severity of complications. Ventilator-associated pneumonia, as in all critically ill patients, is a significant problem in burned patients. However, it is so common in patients with inhalation injury that a better nomenclature may be postinjury pneumonia. Unfortunately, commonly used scores in critical illness such as the Clinical Pulmonary Infection Score (CPIS) have not been shown to be reliable in burn patients. Quantitative broncho -scopic cultures in the setting of clinical suspicion of pneumo-nia should guide treatment of pneumonia.<span style="color:#f87171">170</span> Simple measures such as elevating the head of the bed and maintaining excel -lent oral hygiene and pulmonary toilet are recommended to help decrease the risk of postinjury pneumonia. There is some question as to whether early tracheostomy decreases infectious morbidity in burn patients and whether it improves long-term outcomes. The resulting compart-ment syndrome is most common in circumferential extremity burns, but abdominal and thoracic compartment syndromes also occur. Warning signs of impending compartment syndrome may include paresthesias, pain, decreased capillary refill, and progression to loss of distal pulses; in an intubated patient, the surgeon should anticipate the compartment syndrome and perform frequent neurovascular evaluations. Abdominal com -partment syndrome should be suspected with decreased urine output, increased ventilator airway pressures, and hypotension. Hypoventilation, increased airway pressures, and hypotension may also characterize thoracic compartment syndrome. Escha -rotomies are rarely needed within the first <span style="color:#f87171">8</span> hours following injury and should not be performed unless indicated because of the terrible aesthetic sequelae. When indicated, they are usu-ally performed at the bedside, preferably with electrocautery to minimize blood loss. Extremity incisions are made on the lateral and medial aspects of the limbs in an anatomic position and may extend onto thenar and hypothenar eminences of the hand. Digital escharotomies do not usually result in any meaningful salvage of functional tissue and are not recommended. Inad -equate perfusion despite proper escharotomies may indicate the need for fasciotomy, but this procedure should not be routinely performed as part of the eschar release. Thoracic escharotomies should be placed along the anterior axillary lines with bilateral subcostal and subclavicular extensions. Extension of the ante -rior axillary incisions down the lateral abdomen typically will allow adequate release of abdominal eschar.The strategy of early excision and grafting in burned patients revolutionized survival outcomes in burn care. Not only did it improve mortality, but early excision also decreased reconstruction surgery, hospital length of stay, and costs of care.<span style="color:#f87171">182</span>-<span style="color:#f87171">184</span> Once the initial resuscitation is complete and the patient is hemodynamically stable, attention should be turned to excising the burn wound. Burn excision and wound coverage should ideally start within the first several days, and in larger burns, serial excisions can be performed as patient condition allows. Excision is performed with repeated tangential slices using a Watson or Goulian blade until viable, diffusely bleeding tissue remains. It is appropriate to leave healthy dermis, which will appear white with punctate areas of bleeding. Excision to fat or fascia may be necessary in deeper burns. The downside of tangential excision is a high blood loss, though this may be ame-liorated using techniques such as instillation of an epinephrine tumescence solution underneath the burn. Pneumatic tourni -quets are helpful in extremity burns, and compresses soaked in a dilute epinephrine solution are necessary adjuncts after excision. A fibrinogen and thrombin spray sealant (Tisseel Fibrin Sealant; Baxter, Deerfield, IL) also has beneficial effects on both hemo-stasis and graft adherence to the wound bed. The use of these techniques has markedly decreased the number of blood trans -fusions given during burn surgery.<span style="color:#f87171">185</span> For patients with clearly deep burns and concern for excessive blood loss, fascial exci -sion may be employed. In this technique, electrocautery is used to excise the burned tissue and the underlying subcutaneous tis-sue down to muscle fascia. This technique markedly decreases blood loss but results in a cosmetically inferior appearance due to the loss of subcutaneous tissue. For excision of burns in dif-ficult anatomic areas, such as the face, eyelids, or hands, a pres-surized water dissector may offer more precision but is time consuming, has a steep learning curve, and is expensive.WOUND COVERAGESince full-thickness burns are impractical for most burn wounds, split-thickness sheet autografts harvested with a power dermatome make the most durable wound coverings and have a decent cosmetic appearance. In larger burns, meshed auto -grafted skin provides a larger area of wound coverage. This also allows drainage of blood and serous fluid to prevent accumula-tion under the skin graft with subsequent graft loss. Areas of cosmetic importance such as the face, neck, and hands should be grafted with nonmeshed sheet grafts to ensure optimal appear -ance and function. Unfortunately, even extensive meshing of skin grafts in patients with limited donor sites may not provide adequate amounts of skin. One emerging technique for large burns with limited donor sites is the Meek micrografting tech -nique, or “postage-stamp” technique, where expansion ratios of up to <span style="color:#f87171">9</span>:<span style="color:#f87171">1</span> are able to be achieved. This allows temporary biologic wound coverage until donor sites heal enough so that they may be reharvested.
