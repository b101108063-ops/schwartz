# CH16 分子生物學、疾病的原子理論與精準外科 (Molecular Biology, The Atomic Theory of Disease, and Precision Surgery)

**Source:** Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

---

## 概述 (Overview)

- 現代外科越來越依賴**分子研究**（精準外科）。
- **基因體資訊**（如 BRCA、RET proto-oncogene 突變）引導預防性手術。
- 分子工程導致**癌症特異性基因治療**作為手術減積的輔助。

### 關鍵歷史里程碑

| 年份 | 事件 |
|------|------|
| 1869 | Miescher — 分離出 DNA |
| 1900 | 孟德爾定律重新發現 |
| 1944 | DNA 確認為遺傳物質（Avery, MacLeod, McCarty） |
| 1953 | Watson & Crick — DNA 雙螺旋結構 |
| 1970s | 重組 DNA 技術、PCR 發展 |
| 2003 | 人類基因體計畫完成（~20,000-25,000 基因，30 億鹼基對） |

## 中心法則：DNA → RNA → 蛋白質

```
DNA → Transcription → RNA → Translation → Protein
```

### 關鍵分子

| 分子 | 功能 |
|------|------|
| **DNA** | 遺傳資訊儲存 |
| **mRNA** | 攜帶遺傳密碼從 DNA 到核糖體 |
| **tRNA** | 轉譯時傳遞胺基酸 |
| **rRNA** | 核糖體 RNA — 蛋白質合成機器 |
| **miRNA** | 微小 RNA — 轉錄後基因調控 |
| **piRNA** | Piwi-interacting RNA — 表觀遺傳調控 |
| **lincRNA** | Long intergenic noncoding RNA — 染色質修飾 |

## DNA 結構與複製

### DNA 結構

- **右手雙螺旋**（每圈 10.4 核苷酸對）
- **反平行鏈**（5' → 3' 方向）
- **骨幹：** 糖-磷酸（磷酸二酯鍵）
- **鹼基：** Adenine (A), Thymine (T), Cytosine (C), Guanine (G)
- **鹼基配對：** A=T（2 個氫鍵），C≡G（3 個氫鍵）

### DNA 複製

複製步驟：
1. **Helicase** 解開雙螺旋
2. **Primase** 合成 RNA 引子
3. **DNA polymerase III** 以 5'→3' 方向延伸
4. **Leading strand** — 連續合成
5. **Lagging strand** — Okazaki fragments（不連續）
6. **DNA ligase** 連接片段
7. **Proofreading** 確保高精確度

### 複製錯誤 → 突變

- 可改變編碼蛋白質 → 改變細胞行為。
- **遺傳不穩定性** — 大量基因組變化，尤其在癌細胞。

## 基因調控 (Gene Regulation)

### 轉錄（DNA → RNA）

- **Bacteria：** 單一 RNA 聚合酶（mRNA, rRNA, tRNA）。
- **Eukaryotes：** 更複雜的調控。

### 轉錄後修飾

| 過程 | 描述 |
|------|------|
| **5' capping** | 添加甲基鳥苷 cap |
| **Polyadenylation** | 添加 Poly-A tail |
| **Splicing** | 移除 introns，連接 exons |
| **Alternative splicing** | 不同 exon 組合 → 蛋白質多樣性 |
| **mRNA stability** | 影響轉譯效率 |
| **Nuclear export** | 調控轉運至細胞質 |

### 表觀遺傳調控

| 修飾 | 效應 |
|------|------|
| **DNA methylation** | 基因沉默（CpG islands） |
| **Histone acetylation** | 開放染色質 → 基因活化 |
| **Histone methylation** | 可活化或抑制 |
| **Chromatin remodeling** | 核小體重新定位 |

## 人類基因體 (Human Genome)

### 關鍵特徵

- **~20,000-25,000 蛋白質編碼基因**（先前估計為 100,000）
- **~30 億鹼基對**
- **98% 非編碼區域**（introns、調控序列、轉座子）
- **~1.5% 編碼蛋白質**

### 外科重要基因家族

| 基因 | 相關疾病 |
|------|---------|
| **BRCA1/BRCA2** | 遺傳性乳癌/卵巢癌 |
| **RET** | 多發性內分泌腫瘤（MEN2A/2B） |
| **APC** | 家族性腺瘤性息肉病（FAP） |
| **TP53** | Li-Fraumeni syndrome |
| **VHL** | von Hippel-Lindau syndrome |
| **KRAS, BRAF** | 結腸癌、黑色素瘤 |
| **HER2/ERBB2** | 乳癌 |

## 細胞週期與細胞凋亡 (Cell Cycle & Apoptosis)

### 細胞週期階段

| 階段 | 關鍵事件 |
|------|---------|
| **G0** | 靜止狀態 |
| **G1** | 生長、蛋白質合成 |
| **S** | DNA 複製 |
| **G2** | 生長、有絲分裂準備 |
| **M** | 有絲分裂（前期、中期、後期、末期） |

### 細胞週期檢查點

- **G1/S 檢查點** — DNA 損傷檢查
- **G2/M 檢查點** — 複製完整性
- **Metaphase 檢查點** — 染色體排列

### 細胞凋亡（程式性細胞死亡）

**外在路徑：**
```
Death receptors (Fas, TRAIL-R) → Caspase-8 → Caspase-3
```

**內在路徑：**
```
Mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization (MOMP)
→ Cytochrome c release → Caspase-9 → Caspase-3
```

**關鍵調控因子：**

| 促凋亡 | 抗凋亡 |
|--------|--------|
| **BAX, BAK** | **BCL-2, BCL-XL** |
| **p53** | **MCL-1** |
| **Caspase-3, -8, -9** | **IAPs (survivin)** |

**與癌症的關聯：** 凋亡減少 → 無限制增殖。

## 訊號傳遞路徑 (Signal Transduction Pathways)

### 主要路徑

#### 1. 受體酪氨酸激酶 (RTK) 路徑

```
Growth factor → RTK → RAS → RAF → MEK → ERK → Cell proliferation
```

- **EGFR** — 表皮生長因子受體（針對結腸癌、肺癌）。
- **HER2/ERBB2** — 乳癌中放大（trastuzumab 標的）。

#### 2. PI3K/AKT/mTOR 路徑

```
RTK → PI3K → PIP3 → PDK1 → AKT → mTOR → Protein synthesis, cell growth
```

- **PIK3CA 突變** — 常見於乳癌、結腸癌、子宮內膜癌。
- **PTEN 缺失** — 腫瘤抑制基因（負向調控）。

#### 3. MAPK 路徑

- 細胞增殖、分化、壓力反應。
- **BRAF V600E 突變** — 黑色素瘤、結腸癌（vemurafenib 標的）。

#### 4. Wnt/β-catenin 路徑

- 幹細胞調控、細胞命運。
- **APC 突變** — 結腸癌（家族性腺瘤性息肉病）。

#### 5. TGF-β 路徑

- 細胞生長抑制、免疫調控。
- 雙重角色：腫瘤抑制（早期）→ 促進（晚期）。

#### 6. JAK/STAT 路徑

- 細胞激素訊號。
- **JAK2 V617F** — 骨髓增生病。

## 精準醫學與外科 (Precision Medicine & Surgery)

### 關鍵原則

1. **根據分子特徵進行患者分層**
2. **根據生物標記測試選擇標靶治療**
3. **個人化治療決策**
4. **減少無效治療**

### 外科中的分子檢測

| 檢測 | 目的 | 臨床應用 |
|------|------|---------|
| **Next-generation sequencing (NGS)** | 多基因組合 | 腫瘤分析 |
| **PCR (KRAS, BRAF, etc.)** | 特定突變檢測 | 治療選擇 |
| **Fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH)** | 基因放大 | HER2 測試 |
| **Immunohistochemistry (IHC)** | 蛋白質表現 | HER2, PD-L1 |
| **Liquid biopsy (ctDNA)** | 非侵入性監測 | 抗性突變 |

### 精準外科應用

- **BRCA1/2 檢測** → 預防性乳房切除/卵巢切除。
- **RET 突變** → MEN2 中預防性甲狀腺切除。
- **MSI-H/dMMR 檢測** → 免疫治療反應（pembrolizumab）。
- **循環腫瘤 DNA (ctDNA)** → 微量殘留疾病偵測。

## CRISPR-Cas9 基因體編輯

### 組成

- **Guide RNA (gRNA)** — 20 nt 序列，與目標互補。
- **Cas9 nuclease** — 產生雙股斷裂。
- **PAM sequence** — 目標旁邊所需（SpCas9 為 NGG）。

### 應用

- **基因剔除** — 框架位移突變。
- **基因修正** — 同源 directed repair (HDR)。
- **基因活化/抑制** — 催化死亡 Cas9 (dCas9)。
- **治療** — 鐮刀型紅血球疾病（ex vivo）、LCA10（in vivo）。

### 限制

- Off-target effects（脫靶效應）。
- Delivery efficiency（遞送效率）。
- HDR efficiency（在非分裂細胞中低）。

## 疾病的原子理論 (The Atomic Theory of Disease)

### 概念

- 疾病起源於**分子/細胞改變**。
- **基因突變** → 蛋白質功能改變 → 細胞功能障礙 → 組織/器官衰竭。
- **精準外科** 旨在分子層面糾正或補償。

### 外科影響

1. 手術前**分子診斷**。
2. 標靶**新輔助/輔助治療**。
3. **分子分期** 輔助 TNM 分期。
4. 根據腫瘤生物學**個人化手術方法**。

Teknos 18chapterCOMPLEX ANATOMY AND FUNCTIONThe anatomy of the head and neck is complex because of the proximity of vital structures such as framework, nerves, and arteries. Functionally, these structures afford most of the human senses: vision, taste, smell, and hearing. Even more fundamental, the upper aerodigestive tract is critical for breathing, speech, and swallowing. Otolaryngology—head and neck surgery is the field that predominantly deals with disorders of the head and neck; however, a multidisciplinary approach is required to achieve optimal outcomes. The multidisciplinary team can include audi-ology, speech language pathology, allergy/immunology, neurol-ogy, neurosurgery, radiation, and medical oncology. Infectious processes of the ear may be consid -ered by their location (external, middle, or inner ear), their time course (acute or chronic), and the presence of complications. The external ear or pinna consists of a cartilaginous frame -work, perichondrium, and a relatively thin layer of skin. Ery -sipelas (St Anthony’s Fire) or impetigo are causes of external ear infection affecting the dermis or hypodermis of the auricle, typically caused by Streptococcus pyogenes or Staphylococcus aureus, respectively, that may be encountered posttraumatically or related to ear piercing. Treatment is oral antibiotic therapy targeting these organisms. History and clinical features such as presence of bullae and golden crusting distinguish erysipelas and impetigo from other benign entities causing erythema and edema of the auricle, such as relapsing polychondritis, which is typically diffuse, lobule-sparing, and steroid-responsive.Acute otitis externa, often referred to as “swimmer’s ear,” denotes infection of the skin of the external auditory canal.Typically, the pathology is incited by moisture within the canal leading to skin maceration and pruritus. Subsequent trauma to the canal skin by scratching (i.e., instrumentation with a cot -ton swab or fingernail), erodes the normally protective skin/cerumen barrier. Hearing aid use and comorbid dermatologic conditions such as eczema or other forms of dermatitis may similarly serve as predisposing factors. The milieu of the exter-nal ear canal—dark, warm, humid—is ideal for rapid microbial proliferation. The most common offending organism is Pseu-domonas aeruginosa , although other bacteria and fungi may also be involved. Symptoms and signs of otitis externa include itching during the initial phases and pain with marked swelling of the canal soft tissues as the infection progresses. Treatment involves removal of debris under otomicroscopy and applica -tion of appropriate ototopical antimicrobials, such as neomycin/polymyxin or quinolone-containing eardrops. The topical ste -roid component of these drops (e.g., hydrocortisone or dexa -methasone) addresses swelling and, as a result, decreases the often intense pain associated with this infection. In cases of marked ear canal edema, the use of an otowick is required to facilitate delivery of ototopical medication medially into the ear canal. Fungal infections may call for the addition of 2% acetic acid to reestablish the premorbid pH balance. Patients with otitis externa should also be instructed to keep the ear dry. Rhi-nosinusitis is preferred over sinusitis because sinusitis almost always is accompanied by inflammation of the contiguous nasal mucosa. Rhinosinusitis is a significant health burden, affect -ing nearly 12% of the population.17 Rhinosinusitis is the fifth most common diagnosis responsible for antibiotic prescription and accounts for more than 20% of all antibiotics prescribed to adults. The majority of tumors (80%) arise in the parotid gland (Fig. 18-44); however, 80% of these are benign, most commonly, pleomorphic adenomas (benign mixed tumors). However, certain signs and symptoms, such as pain, paresthesia, facial nerve weakness, or rapid growth, raise the concern for malig-nancy. If there is facial nerve weakness (10%–15% of cases), this usually represents tumor invading the facial nerve. Sub -mandibular and sublingual tumors present with a mass or swell-ing in the neck or floor of the mouth, respectively. Tumors in this region can invade the lingual nerve leading to tongue par -esthesia or the hypoglossal nerve invasion leading to paralysis.
