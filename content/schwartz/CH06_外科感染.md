# CH06 外科感染 (Surgical Infections)

**來源：** Schwartz's Principles of Surgery, 11th Edition

---

## Key Points

1. **Sepsis** is a life-threatening syndrome reflecting both an infection and the systemic host response to it; outcomes improved with organized approach (resuscitation + antibiotics + source control).
2. **Source control** is essential — infected/necrotic material must be drained or removed.
3. **Antibiotic prophylaxis principles：** (a) agent covering common organisms at site, (b) within 30 min before incision, (c) redose during long operations based on half-life, (d) limit to ≤24h for routine prophylaxis.
4. **Antibiotic therapy principles：** (a) identify likely sources, (b) appropriate spectrum, (c) rapid initiation within 1 hour, (d) culture-guided refinement, (e) discontinue if no infection after 3 days, (f) appropriate duration.
5. **SSI prevention：** patient preparation, timely perioperative antibiotics, normothermia, normoglycemia, wound management.
6. **Necrotizing soft tissue infection：** early recognition + aggressive debridement until no further signs of infection.
7. **Universal precautions** for blood/body fluid pathogens (HIV, HBV, HCV).

| 年份 | 發現 |

| **1878–1880** | Robert Koch：Koch's postulates, 培養技術 |

| **1902** | King Edward VII 闌尾炎 → 登基典禮延後；推動了外科發展 |

### Koch's Postulates (1884)

1. 疑似病原體存在於所有疾病病例中，健康動物體內不存在。
2. 病原體從病患體內分離出來，可在體外純培養中生長。
3. 純培養可使健康動物致病。
4. 病原體可從新發病動物體內再次分離，並證實與原始菌株相同。

## 微生物學 (Microbiology)

### 人體正常菌叢 (Normal Microbiota)

| 部位 | 主要菌種 |
|------|----------|
| **Skin** | Staphylococcus epidermidis, S. aureus, Corynebacterium, Propionibacterium |
| **Oropharynx** | Streptococcus, Neisseria, anaerobes |
| **Stomach** | ~10²–10³ CFU/mL (acid-sensitive) |
| **Small intestine** | ~10⁵–10⁸ CFU/mL |
| **Colon** | ~10¹¹–10¹² CFU/g; anaerobes:aerobes = 100:1 |

**Colon dominant anaerobes：** Bacteroides fragilis, B. distasonis, B. thetaiotaomicron, Bifidobacterium, Clostridium, Eubacterium, Fusobacterium, Lactobacillus, Peptostreptococcus。

**Colon dominant aerobes：** E. coli, Enterococcus faecalis/faecium, C. albicans。

### 伺機性感染 (Opportunistic Infections)

- Resident flora become pathogenic when：
  - Barrier disrupted (surgery, catheter)。
  - Host defense impaired (immunosuppression)。
  - Translocation to sterile body cavity。

### 常見致病菌

| 病原菌 | 常見感染 |
|--------|----------|
| **Staphylococcus aureus** | Skin, bone, bacteremia, endocarditis |
| **Coagulase-negative Staph** | CLABSI, prosthetic infections |
| **Streptococcus** | Pneumonia, meningitis, pharyngitis |
| **Enterococcus** | UTIs, intra-abdominal infections |
| **E. coli** | UTIs, GI infections, bacteremia |
| **Klebsiella** | Pneumonia, UTIs |
| **Pseudomonas aeruginosa** | Healthcare-associated pneumonia, burns |
| **Bacteroides fragilis** | Intra-abdominal infections |
| **Clostridium difficile** | Antibiotic-associated colitis |
| **Candida** | Fungal infections, especially in immunocompromised |

## 宿主防禦 (Host Defenses)

### 物理屏障

- **Skin：** physical barrier + resident flora + secretions (sebum, sweat)。
- **Mucous membranes：** respiratory (ciliary escalator), GI (peristalsis), urogenital。

### 細胞性防禦

| 細胞 | 功能 |
|------|------|
| **Macrophages** | Phagocytosis, cytokine production, antigen presentation |
| **PMNs (中性球)** | Phagocytosis, NETs (neutrophil extracellular traps), oxidative killing |
| **Dendritic cells** | Antigen presentation, immune activation |
| **Mast cells** | TNF release, neutrophil recruitment, histamine |

### 體液性防禦

- **Complement：** C3a, C4a, C5a (anaphylatoxins)；MAC (C5b-9 membrane attack complex)。
- **Antibodies (IgG, IgM)。**
- **Lysozyme, lactoferrin, transferrin** (sequester iron)。
- **Cytokines：** TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6, IL-8, IFN-γ。

### Pattern Recognition Receptors (PRRs)

- **TLRs：** recognize PAMPs (pathogen-associated molecular patterns)。
- **RAGE：** binds HMGB1。
- **NLRs：** form inflammasome。

## 感染分類 (Classification of Infections)

### 感染結局

| 結局 | 說明 |
|------|------|
| **Eradication** | Complete elimination of pathogen |
| **Containment** | 形成膿瘡 (abscess) — hallmark of chronic infection |
| **Locoregional infection** | Cellulitis, lymphangitis, aggressive soft tissue infection |
| **Metastatic abscess** | Distant spread |
| **Systemic infection** | Bacteremia, fungemia — failure of local host defenses |

### 外科感染的特殊考量

- **Polymicrobial nature：** aerobic + anaerobic synergy (e.g., intra-abdominal infections)。
- **Source control is essential：** drainage, debridement, removal of infected material。

## SIRS / Sepsis 定義

### SIRS Criteria

| 類別 | 標準 |
|------|------|
| **General** | Temp >38.3°C or <36°C；HR >90；RR >20；altered mental status |
| **Inflammatory** | WBC >12,000 or <4,000；bandemia >10%；CRP >2 SD；procalcitonin >2 SD |
| **Hemodynamic** | SBP <90 mmHg；MAP <70；SBP decrease >40 mmHg |
| **Organ dysfunction** | PaO₂/FiO₂ ratio；acute oliguria；Cr↑；coagulopathy；ileus；thrombocytopenia；hyperbilirubinemia |

### Sepsis-3 定義 (2016)

- **Sepsis：** Life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by dysregulated host response to infection。
- **qSOFA (quick SOFA)：** ≥2 of: altered mental status, SBP ≤100 mmHg, RR ≥22 → high mortality risk。
- **Septic shock：** Sepsis + hypotension requiring vasopressors + lactate >2 mmol/L despite resuscitation。
- **Mortality：** sepsis = 10.3/100,000；septic shock >40%。

## 抗生素原則 (Antibiotic Principles)

### 預防性抗生素 (Prophylaxis)

| 原則 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| **Timing** | Within 30 min before incision (cefazolin 60 min for better tissue levels) |
| **Selection** | Agent covering likely organisms for surgical site |
| **Redosing** | During long operations based on half-life |
| **Duration** | ≤24h for routine prophylaxis (some exceptions: cardiac surgery, vascular grafts) |

### 治療性抗生素 (Therapeutic)

1. **Identify source** of infection。
2. **Select appropriate agent(s)** — spectrum based on likely pathogens, site。
3. **Start within 1 hour** of sepsis recognition。
4. **Obtain cultures first** (blood, site-specific)。
5. **Refine based on culture results** (de-escalation)。
6. **Discontinue if no infection** identified after 3 days。
7. **Appropriate duration** — 7–10 days for most infections；longer for source control issues。

### 抗生素選擇

| 感染類型 | 常用藥物 |
|----------|----------|
| **Skin/soft tissue (community)** | Cefazolin, clindamycin |
| **Skin/soft tissue (MRSA risk)** | Vancomycin + β-lactam |
| **Intra-abdominal (community)** | Ceftriaxone + metronidazole or piperacillin-tazobactam |
| **Intra-abdominal (healthcare-associated)** | Carbapenem or β-lactam/β-lactamase inhibitor + enterococcal coverage |
| **Urinary** | Nitrofurantoin, TMP-SMX, fluoroquinolone |
| **Necrotizing fasciitis** | Vancomycin + piperacillin-tazobactam + clindamycin |
| **Healthcare-associated pneumonia** | Anti-pseudomonal β-lactam + vancomycin/linezolid |

## 外科部位感染 (Surgical Site Infection, SSI)

### 分類

| 類型 | 定義 |
|------|------|
| **Superficial incisional** | Skin/subcutaneous tissue within 30 days |
| **Deep incisional** | Fascia/muscle within 30 days (or 1 year if implant) |
| **Organ/space** | Intra-abdominal, pleural, etc. within 30 days (or 1 year) |

### 風險因素

| Patient factors | Operative factors | Wound factors |
|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Diabetes | Duration >T-site | Contamination |
| Obesity | Blood loss >500 mL | Tissue trauma |
| Smoking | Hypothermia | Poor closure |
| Malnutrition | Hyperglycemia | Dead space |
| Immunosuppression | Emergency surgery | Hematoma/seroma |

### 預防措施

| 措施 | 證據 |
|------|------|
| **Preoperative antibiotics** | ↓ SSI rate significantly |
| **Normothermia** (>36°C) | ↓ SSI, ↓ wound disruption |
| **Normoglycemia** (<200 mg/dL) | ↓ SSI, ↓ mortality |
| **Skin preparation** (chlorhexidine-alcohol) | ↓ SSI vs. iodine |
| **Adequate oxygen** (FiO₂ 0.8 intraop) | ↓ SSI (controversial) |
| **Avoid hair removal** (or clipper) | ↓ SSI |
| **Negative pressure wound therapy** | For high-risk closures |

## 壞死性軟組織感染 (Necrotizing Soft Tissue Infection)

### 分類

- **Necrotizing fasciitis：** 沿筋膜擴散。
- **Clostridial myonecrosis (gas gangrene)：** 肌肉壞死。
- **Fournier's gangrene：** 會陰/外生殖器壞死性感染。
- **Meleney's synergistic gangrene：** 混合菌感染。

### 臨床表現

- **疼痛與不成比例的外觀** (pain out of proportion)。
- 快速進展 (hours to days)。
- Bullae, skin discoloration, crepitus (gas)。
- Systemic toxicity (fever, shock, organ failure)。
- **Fournier's gangrene：** necrotizing infection of perineum/external genitalia。

### 治療

1. **Immediate aggressive surgical debridement** — most critical；repeat until healthy tissue。
2. **Broad-spectrum antibiotics** (vancomycin + piperacillin-tazobactam + clindamycin)。
3. Hemodynamic support。
4. Hyperbaric oxygen (adjunctive)。
5. 營養支持。

### 預後

- Mortality **20–40%**。
- Poor prognosis：**age >50, sepsis, organ failure, delay in surgery >24h**。

## 院內感染 (Healthcare-Associated Infection)

### 常見病原

| 感染類型 | 病原 |
|----------|------|
| **Catheter-associated UTI (CAUTI)** | E. coli, Enterococcus, Pseudomonas, Candida |
| **VAP/ventilator-associated pneumonia** | MRSA, Pseudomonas, gram-negatives |
| **CLABSI** | Coagulase-negative Staph, S. aureus, Candida, gram-negatives |
| **C. difficile colitis** | C. difficile |
| **Surgical site infection** | S. aureus, coag-negative Staph |

### 抗生素管理原則

- **De-escalation：** start broad, narrow based on cultures。
- **Pharmacokinetics/Pharmacodynamics：**

| 類型 | 藥物 | 原則 |
|------|------|------|
| Concentration-dependent | Aminoglycosides, fluoroquinolones, daptomycin | High Cmax/MIC |
| Time-dependent | β-lactams, vancomycin | Maximize time above MIC |

- **TDM：** vancomycin (trough 15–20 for severe infections), aminoglycosides。

## 愛滋病與血液傳播病原 (HIV and Blood-Borne Pathogens)

### 傳播風險

| 病原 | 針刺傷風險 |
|------|-----------|
| **HIV** | ~0.3% (1:300) |
| **HBV** | ~30% (if HBeAg positive), ~6% (if HBeAg negative) |
| **HCV** | ~1.8% (1:50) |

### 預防措施 (Universal Precautions)

- 接觸血液/體液時使用屏障 (手套, 隔離衣, 護目鏡)。
- 針頭/尖銳物品小心處理。
- 接觸後立即沖洗。
- 報告所有針刺傷。
- **HBV疫苗** (所有醫護人員)。

### 暴露後預防 (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis, PEP)

| 病原 | PEP |
|------|-----|
| **HIV** | 2–3 drug regimen for 28 days (start within 72h, ideally <2h) |
| **HBV** | HBIG + vaccine series (if non-immune) |
| **HCV** | No PEP available；monitor for seroconversion (anti-HCV at 6 weeks, 3 months, 6 months) |

## 常見外科感染疾病 (Common Surgical Infections)

### 蜂窩性組織炎 (Cellulitis)

- **病原：** β-hemolytic Streptococcus, S. aureus。
- **治療：** cefazolin or nafcillin (outpatient: dicloxacillin, cephalexin)。
- **MRSA coverage：** clindamycin, TMP-SMX, doxycycline。

### 膿瘡 (Abscess)

- **病原：** S. aureus (including MRSA), anaerobes, streptococci。
- **治療：** Incision & Drainage (I&D)；antibiotics for immunocompromised, extensive cellulitis, facial/perineal abscess。

### 骨髓炎 (Osteomyelitis)

- **急性：** S. aureus (most common)；children。
- **慢性：** polymicrobial；post-traumatic/surgical。
- **診斷：** MRI；bone biopsy for culture。
- **治療：** long-term antibiotics (6 weeks minimum)；surgical debridement for necrotic bone。

### 人工關節感染 (Periprosthetic Joint Infection)

| 情況 | 治療 |
|------|------|
| 症狀 <30天, 植入物穩定 | **DAIR：** Debridement + Antibiotics + Implant Retention |
| 需更換 | **Two-stage exchange：** remove implant, antibiotic spacer, reimplant |
| 葡萄球菌感染 | **Rifampin-based regimen** (biofilm penetration) |

### 腹內感染 (Intra-abdominal Infection)

**來源：** GI perforation, anastomotic leak, biliary obstruction, appendicitis, diverticulitis。

**病原：** E. coli, Bacteroides fragilis, Klebsiella, Enterococcus, Candida。

**治療：**

| 類型 | 方案 |
|------|------|
| **Community-acquired** (<5 days, no risk factors) | Ceftriaxone + metronidazole or piperacillin-tazobactam |
| **Healthcare-associated** | Carbapenem or β-lactam/β-lactamase inhibitor + enterococcal coverage |

### 困難梭狀芽孢桿菌 (Clostridioides difficile)

- **風險：** antibiotics, hospitalization。
- **表現：** watery diarrhea, pseudomembranous colitis, toxic megacolon。
- **診斷：** stool toxin assay (EIA, PCR), GDH antigen + toxin。
- **治療：**

| 情況 | 治療 |
|------|------|
| **Initial episode** | **Fidaxomicin** (首選) or vancomycin (oral) |
| **復發** | Fidaxomicin or vancomycin pulse/taper |
| **≥2 recurrences** | **Fecal microbiota transplant (FMT)** |

- 避免止瀉藥。

### 蜂窩性組織炎 vs. 壞死性筋膜炎

| 特徵 | Cellulitis | Necrotizing Fasciitis |
|------|-----------|----------------------|
| **疼痛** | Mild-moderate | Severe, out of proportion |
| **進展** | Gradual | Rapid (hours-days) |
| **Systemic signs** | Mild | Severe toxicity |
| **Skin changes** | Redness, swelling | Bullae, necrosis, crepitus |
| **治療** | Antibiotics | Emergency surgery + antibiotics |
