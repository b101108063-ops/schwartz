# CH10 創傷進階 (Advanced <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

**來源：** Schwartz's Principles of <span style="color:#60a5fa">Surgery</span>, 11th Edition

---

## 心臟與胸腔創傷 (Cardiac & Thoracic <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

### 心臟創傷 (Cardiac Injuries)

- **心臟刺傷：** 存活條件 = 可修補的傷口；stab wounds 為主。
- **修復原則：** 
  - 2-0 polypropylene suture (pledgeted) for right ventricle。
  - Coronary artery 附近 → horizontal mattress sutures（避免 coronary occlusion）。
  - <span style="color:#fb923c">BioGlue</span> (surgical adhesive) for complex wounds with friable myocardium.
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Blunt</span> cardiac injury：** 表現 = 持續心搏過速、傳導異常；<span style="color:#e2e8f0">ECHO</span> 診斷；心電圖與 cardiac enzymes 不一定準確。
- **監測：** hemodynamic instability 者術後 24 小時 telemetry monitoring。

### 氣管、支氣管、肺臟 (<span style="color:#22d3ee">Trachea</span>, Bronchi, <span style="color:#22d3ee">Lung</span>)

- **氣管創傷：** <span style="color:#60a5fa">blunt</span> injury 最常見於 <span style="color:#22d3ee">carina</span> 2.5 cm 內；massive air leak 需要 emergency exploration。
- **支氣管修復：** primary end-to-end anastomosis (3-0 PDS)；周圍組織 decortication 限於受傷區域。
- **肺臟創傷：** tube <span style="color:#60a5fa">thoracostomy</span> 處理大多數 pneumothorax；盡量避免切除。
- **肺臟穿刺術 (Pulmonary tractotomy)：** 適用於槍傷 track，直接結紮 bleeders 和 bronchi，避免 lobectomy。
- **創傷性肺囊腫 (Pneumatocele)：** 保守治療；發燒/白血球高 → <span style="color:#e2e8f0">CT</span> 評估是否形成膿胸。
- **膿胸 (Empyema)：** <span style="color:#e2e8f0">CT</span> 診斷；單一小 loculation 無 rind → percutaneous drainage；fibrinolytics 證據不足；早期 <span style="color:#60a5fa">VATS</span> decortication。

### 食道創傷 (Esophageal Injuries)

- 常與氣管支氣管創傷同時發生。
- **修復：** primary single-layer end-to-end anastomosis；合併氣管修復時需 vascularized tissue interposition（防止 fistula）。
- **胃食道交界處：** repair + Nissen fundoplication 或 segmental <span style="color:#60a5fa">resection</span> with gastric pull-up。
- **大範圍或延遲處理：** esophageal exclusion + wide drainage + diverting loop esophagostomy + gastrostomy。

### 胸壁與橫膈膜 (Chest Wall & Diaphragm)

- 肋骨骨折：疼痛控制 + pulmonary toilet；preemptive rib blocks (0.25% bupivacaine)。
- **Flail chest：** 考慮 open reduction and internal fixation (plates)。
- **横膈膜創傷：** <span style="color:#60a5fa">blunt</span> → linear tear；acute 建議經腹部修補；使用 1 polypropylene suture。

## 腹部創傷 (Abdominal <span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span>)

### 肝臟與膽道 (<span style="color:#22d3ee">Liver</span> & Biliary Tract)

- **非手術治療 (NOM)：** 適用於 hemodynamic stability、無 peritonitis 者。
- **血管栓塞 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Angioembolization</span>)：** 適應症：6 小時內輸血 ≥4 units <span style="color:#e2e8f0">RBC</span> 或 24 小時內 ≥6 units。
- **手術控制出血：** perihepatic packing + manual compression；<span style="color:#60a5fa">Pringle maneuver</span> 控制出血。
- **肝臟縫合 (Hepatic lobar arterial ligation)：** 難控 arterial <span style="color:#c084fc">hemorrhage</span> 的替代方案。
- **肝靜脈/下腔靜脈損傷：** 肝血管隔離技術（suprahepatic/infrahepatic clamping、venovenous bypass）。
- **膽道損傷：** T-tube 引流；Roux-en-Y choledochojejunostomy for 組織缺損。
- **併發症：** biloma、arterial pseudoaneurysm、biliary fistula、hemobilia。

### 脾臟 (<span style="color:#22d3ee">Spleen</span>)

- **非手術治療：** 首選（成人也適用）；contrast extravasation → selective <span style="color:#60a5fa">angioembolization</span> (SAE)。
- **延遲出血：** 可延至數週後（不同於肝臟）。
- **手術適應症：** ><span style="color:#60a5fa">grade</span> II injury + <span style="color:#60a5fa">coagulopathy</span>；hilar injury；pulverized parenchyma。
- **自體脾組織移植：** younger patients without enteric injury（維持部分免疫功能）。
- **Overwhelming postsplenectomy sepsis (OPSI)：** 來源：Streptococcus pneumoniae, H. influenzae, N. meningitidis。
- **疫苗：** 脾切除後 >14 天施打（Pneumovax, H. influenzae type b, meningococcal）。

### 胃腸道 (<span style="color:#22d3ee"><span style="color:#22d3ee">Stomach</span></span>, Small Intestine, <span style="color:#22d3ee"><span style="color:#22d3ee">Colon</span></span>, <span style="color:#22d3ee"><span style="color:#22d3ee">Rectum</span></span>)

- **胃：** oversewn (running single-layer) 或 stapler closure。
- **小腸：** <1/3 周徑 → transverse 3-0 PDS suture；destructive/multiple close injuries → segmental <span style="color:#60a5fa">resection</span> + end-to-end anastomosis。
- **早期腸道營養 (TEN)：** <span style="color:#60a5fa">trauma</span> 患者 early total enteral nutrition 可減少 septic complications。
- **大腸：** primary repair 對大多數穿透傷安全；<span style="color:#60a5fa">damage control</span> + 左結腸重傷 → 考慮 temporary colostomy。
- **直腸：** 建議 diverting colostomy（sigmoid loop colostomy）；Hartmann's procedure for 廣泛損傷。
- **Colorectal 併發症：** intra-abdominal abscess (10%, percutaneous drainage)、fecal fistula (1-3%)。

### 十二指腸與胰臟 (<span style="color:#22d3ee">Duodenum</span> & <span style="color:#22d3ee">Pancreas</span>)

- **十二指腸血腫：** 保守治療（NG suction + TPN）；3 週無改善 → 手術。
- **十二指腸修補：** primary repair (running single-layer 3-0 monofilament)；tissue loss → Roux-en-Y duodenojejunostomy。
- **胰臟創傷：** duct 完整性決定治療方式。

| 損傷類型 | 治療 |
|----------|------|
| <span style="color:#c084fc">Contusion</span> | 保守或 closed suction drainage |
| Distal duct <span style="color:#c084fc">disruption</span> | Distal pancreatectomy（儘量保留脾臟） |
| Proximal injury | Closed suction drainage |

- **Pancreatic head injuries：** 涉及膽管/胰管 → 複雜；可能需要 pancreaticoduodenectomy (Whipple)。
- **Pyloric exclusion：** 保護高風險十二指腸修補；3-4 週後縫線自然鬆開。
- **併發症：** pancreatic fistula (>20% combined injuries)、duodenal fistula、pseudocyst。

## 腹部血管創傷 (Abdominal Vascular Injuries)

### 主動脈 (<span style="color:#22d3ee">Aorta</span>)

- **穿透傷：** 小穿刺 → 4-0 polypropylene suture 或 PTFE patch；多需要 interposition graft。
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Blunt</span> injury：** 通常為 infrarenal <span style="color:#22d3ee">aorta</span> intimal tear；需要 interposition graft。

### 腸繫膜血管 (<span style="color:#22d3ee">Mesenteric</span> Vessels)

- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">SMA</span> injury：** 槍傷常見「black bowel」+ supramesocolic <span style="color:#c084fc">hematoma</span>；Pruitt-Inahara shunt 暫時通血。
- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">SMV</span> injury：** venorrhaphy 或 ligation；ligation 後腸道水腫需大量輸液。

### 骨盆血管 (Pelvic Vessels)

- **Ilioaccessible injury：** Pruitt-Inahara shunt 做 <span style="color:#60a5fa">damage control</span>。
- **下腔靜脈 (<span style="color:#22d3ee">IVC</span>) ligation：** 術後雙下肢水腫 → elastic bandage + 抬高。

---

## 泌尿生殖道創傷 (Genitourinary Tract)

### 腎臟 (<span style="color:#22d3ee">Kidney</span>)

- **>90% <span style="color:#60a5fa">blunt</span> renal injuries：** 保守治療。
- **腎血管損傷：** 修復黃金時間 <5 小時；可考慮 endoluminal stent。
- **腎臟修補原則：** 分別縫合 collecting system；保留腎囊膜覆蓋修補處。
- **Contraindication to NOM：** hematuria + hemoperitoneum requiring >4 units transfusion。

### 輸尿管、膀胱、尿道 (<span style="color:#22d3ee">Ureter</span>, <span style="color:#22d3ee">Bladder</span>, Urethra)

| 部位 | 損傷類型 | 治療 |
|------|----------|------|
| **輸尿管** | — | 5-0 absorbable monofilament 修復；distal injury → reimplantation with psoas hitch |
| **膀胱** | Intraperitoneal <span style="color:#c084fc">rupture</span> | 手術修補（3-0 absorbable） |
| **膀胱** | Extraperitoneal | 保守（Foley decompression 2 週） |
| **尿道** | — | Foley catheter bridging；狹窄常見，可選擇性處理 |

## 骨盆骨折出血控制 (Pelvic Fracture <span style="color:#c084fc">Hemorrhage</span>)

- **60-90% 骨盆骨折出血：** 靜脈或骨頭來源（不是 arterial）。
- **流程：** sheet pelvic → 評估血液動力學 → 不穩定 → external fixation + **preperitoneal pelvic packing**。
- **Pelvic packing：** 6 塊紗布（左右各 3）；48 小時內移除；repacking 感染率高（47%）。
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Angiography</span>：** packing 後仍有出血（術後 12 小時 >4 units <span style="color:#e2e8f0">RBC</span>）→ 栓塞。
- **開放性骨盆骨折：** diverting sigmoid colostomy + 每日 pulsatile irrigation + VAC。

## 四肢血管創傷與腔室症候群 (Extremity Vascular & <span style="color:#60a5fa">Compartment Syndrome</span>)

### 血管修復原則

| 位置 | 修復方式 |
|------|----------|
| <span style="color:#22d3ee">Subclavian</span>/<span style="color:#22d3ee">axillary</span> artery | 6-mm PTFE 或 RSVG |
| <span style="color:#22d3ee">Brachial</span> artery | End-to-end RSVG interposition |

- 骨折 + 血管傷：先放 temporary intravascular shunts (Pruitt-Inhara) 恢復血流，再處理骨折，最後確定性血管修補。
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Fasciotomy</span>：** 適應症 = gradient <30 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span>、absolute pressure >30 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span>、缺血 >6 小時、動靜脈同時受傷。

### 腔室症候群 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Compartment Syndrome</span>)

**診斷：**

- 疼痛（主訴）+ 被動拉伸痛。
- Numbness between 1st-2nd toes（前側腔室）。
- Paralysis 為晚期。
- Pulselessness 為末期。

**治療：**

- 四腔室 <span style="color:#60a5fa">fasciotomy</span>（小腿：外側 + 前側 + 後淺 + 後深）。
- 需 detached soleus from tibia 釋放深後腔室。
- 下肢筋膜切開術後需監測腔室壓力。

## 創傷加護病房管理 (<span style="color:#60a5fa">Trauma</span> <span style="color:#f87171">ICU</span> Management)

### 復甦原則

- **目標：** core temperature >35°C、<span style="color:#60a5fa">base deficit</span> <6 mmol/L、<span style="color:#34d399">normal</span> coagulation、<span style="color:#60a5fa">lactate</span> 24 小時內正常化。
- **輸血：** 初期 Hb <span style="color:#f87171">>10 g/dL</span>；24 小時後改為 <span style="color:#f87171"><7 g/dL</span> trigger。
- **液體：** 限制 <span style="color:#fb923c">crystalloid</span>（避免腹腔腔室症候群）；<span style="color:#60a5fa"><span style="color:#fb923c">norepinephrine</span></span> 為低 SVR 首選。
- **Monitoring：** 10 cc/kg bolus 或 passive <span style="color:#22d3ee">leg</span> raise 後 SV 增加 ≥10% 表示 preload responsive。

### 腹腔腔室症候群 (Abdominal <span style="color:#60a5fa">Compartment Syndrome</span>)

**診斷：** 膀胱壓力測量（正常 <12 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span>；>20 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span> 開始有臨床意義）。

**分級：**

| <span style="color:#60a5fa">Grade</span> | 膀胱壓力 |
|-------|----------|
| **I** | 10-15 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span> |
| **II** | 16-25 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span> |
| **III** | 26-35 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span> |
| **IV** | >35 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span> |

**治療：** 膀胱壓 >35 <span style="color:#f87171">mmHg</span> 或 end-organ dysfunction → emergent decompression。

**預後：** 延遲減壓死亡率 70%。

**開放腹部：** sequential closure with wound VAC（成功率 >95%）；每日 lost 500-2500 mL 含豐富白蛋白的腹液。

Advances in surgical technique and a better under-standing of immunology are the two main reasons that transplants have evolved from experimental procedures, just several decades ago, to a widely accepted treatment today for patients with end-stage organ failure. Throughout the world, for a variety of indications, kidney, liver, pancreas, intestine, heart, and lung transplants are now the current standard of care.But the success of transplantation has created new chal -lenges. A better understanding of the pathophysiology of end-stage organ failure as well as advances in critical care medicine and in the treatment of various diseases led to expanding the cri-teria for, and decreasing the contraindications to, transplants. Orthotopic transplants require the removal of the diseased organ (heart, lungs, liver, or intestine); in heterotopic transplants, the diseased organ is kept in place (kidney, pancreas).According to the degree of immunologic similarity between the donor and recipient, transplants are divided into three main categories: (a) an autotransplant is the transfer of cells, tissue, or an organ from one part of the body to another part in the same person, so no immunosuppression is required; this type of transplant includes skin, artery or vein, bone, carti -lage, nerve, and islet cell transplants; (b) an allotransplant is the transfer of cells, tissue, or an organ from one person to another of the same species; with the exception of identical twins, the immune system of the recipient recognizes the donated organ as a foreign body, so immunosuppression is required in order to avoid rejection; and (c) a xenotransplant is the transfer of cells, tissue, or an organ from one organism to another of a different species.
